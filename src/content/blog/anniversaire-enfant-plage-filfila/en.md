<p>Planning a <strong>child's birthday in Filfila</strong> requires no complicated logistics: Larbi Ben M'hidi beach sits just 700 m from Oasis Residence, making it easy to combine an apartment snack table with sand games the very same afternoon. Between the accommodation, the beach gear and the cake, a few simple guidelines turn an ordinary holiday day into a proper celebration.</p>

<p>Unlike a party hall or restaurant that needs booking well ahead, a birthday built around a seaside apartment leaves plenty of freedom: flexible timing, an indoor space for the snack if the wind picks up, a terrace or balcony to extend the day, and above all the beach just minutes away on foot for the games. This article covers choosing the right apartment, what gear to pack, which activities work best with children, how to handle the cake and snacks, and the safety points worth keeping in mind.</p>

<h2>Why Filfila works well for a child's birthday</h2>

<p>Filfila combines two rare advantages for this kind of event: a seaside apartment and a beach reachable on foot, with no need to drive on the day of the party. For a family preparing a stay with several children, or inviting other families along, this proximity simplifies everything, from carrying the gear to keeping an eye on kids moving between the apartment and the sand.</p>

<h3>A beach right next to the accommodation</h3>

<p>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), roughly 7 km long, offers enough room to set up a group of children without feeling cramped among other swimmers, even in peak season. At 700 m from the residence, it is reached in a few minutes on foot with beach bags and game equipment, with no need to load a car for a simple afternoon out.</p>

<h3>A long swimming season to pick the right date</h3>

<p>The swimming season in Skikda officially runs from June to November. This wide window makes it possible to schedule the birthday whenever suits the family, rather than being limited to July and August. Early or late in the season, the beach remains usable for games even if the water is a touch cooler than at the height of summer, when it hovers around 26 °C in August.</p>

<h2>Choosing the right apartment for the party</h2>

<p>The choice of apartment mainly depends on how many families are invited and when the snack is planned: before the beach, after, or both. The residence has seven independent units, leaving several options depending on group size.</p>

<h3>Which format for how many children and parents</h3>

<p>For one child and their parents, a studio or a classic F2 is more than enough to store beach gear and prepare a simple snack. Once several families join the celebration, the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a>, which holds up to 8 people at base capacity, becomes the most practical format: the living room hosts the snack table, and the terrace lets the day continue outdoors once everyone is back from the beach, rinsed off and changed.</p>

<h3>What to bring to the beach</h3>

<p>A few simple items are enough to run beach games for a child's birthday: one or two large towels or mats to mark out the snack area, buckets and spades for sandcastles, a ball, and a parasol or beach tent to protect the youngest from the midday sun. A small cool bag is handy for keeping drinks cold without constant trips back to the apartment.</p>

<h3>Picking the right time of day</h3>

<p>In the height of summer, late morning or late afternoon stays the most comfortable slot for children: the sun is less intense than between 1 pm and 4 pm, a window better spent on the snack indoors, under the apartment's air conditioning. In June or September, a continuous programme works better, beach in the morning and snack in mid-afternoon, with no heat peak to plan around.</p>

<h2>Game and activity ideas on the sand</h2>

<p>The most effective beach games for a child's birthday are usually the simplest, as long as a minimum of gear and one or two backup activities are planned in case of wind or heavy heat.</p>

<h3>Classic games for all ages</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Sandcastle contests, with a small symbolic prize for the most original build</li>
  <li>Treasure hunt: a few objects or shells buried to be found by hand or with a small spade</li>
  <li>Relay races along the shoreline, adapted to the participants' age</li>
  <li>Ball and beach rackets for more active children</li>
  <li>Flag or statue games, which work well even with a wide age gap among the guests</li>
</ul>

<h3>Supervised water activities for older children</h3>

<p>For children from around 8-10 years old, a few minutes of supervised paddleboarding or kayaking add a more physical activity to the party, provided it stays within a monitored area and under adult responsibility. These activities are usually arranged on the spot, depending on the day's sea conditions, and are best kept as an add-on rather than the main activity for very young children.</p>

<h2>Snacks and the cake: sorting out the catering</h2>

<p>The cake and snack question is easily solved thanks to the fitted kitchen in every apartment at the residence: fridge, oven, hob and basic dishware allow the meal to be prepared or finished on site.</p>

<h3>Cooking on site or ordering delivery</h3>

<p>Both options work without difficulty: preparing part of the snack with the apartment's equipment, or having a cake and prepared dishes delivered directly. The two approaches combine well, for example a cake ordered in advance paired with simple sides prepared on site the same day.</p>

<h3>Keeping the cake cool in the heat</h3>

<p>In full summer, heat is the main concern for a cream-based cake. The apartment's fridge keeps it cool until serving time, and it is best avoided leaving it in direct sun on the beach for long before bringing it back inside to be cut.</p>

<h2>Safety and common sense on the beach</h2>

<p>A successful party is above all one without incidents. A few simple rules limit surprises, especially with a group of children of different ages moving between the towel and the water.</p>

<h3>Supervision and splitting adults between groups</h3>

<p>With several children at once, it is better to split supervision between the adults present rather than relying on a single person for the whole group. Marking out a clear swimming zone, visible from where the snack is set up, makes it easier to watch over children playing on the sand while others are swimming.</p>

<h3>A few points worth checking</h3>

<p>As with any beach in the wilaya, it is worth checking the day's conditions before guests arrive: sea state, possible jellyfish depending on the period, and the forecast if strong wind is announced. None of this rules out holding the party, but it allows the programme to be adjusted, for instance favouring sand games over swimming on a particularly windy day.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Planning the whole party in direct sun between 1 pm and 4 pm with no shade or air conditioning available</li>
  <li>Forgetting to check the apartment's capacity before inviting several families at once</li>

  <li>Relying on a single adult to supervise a large group of children of very different ages</li>
