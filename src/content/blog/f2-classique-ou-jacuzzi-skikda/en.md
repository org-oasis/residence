<p>The <strong>classic F2</strong> and the <strong>F2 with private jacuzzi</strong> at Résidence Oasis in Filfila share the same base capacity of 4 guests, but not the same use. Starting from €{{TIER_F2_EUR}} per night, the classic F2 targets a practical, well-equipped stay; starting from €{{TIER_F2JACUZZI_EUR}} per night, the jacuzzi F2 adds a private jacuzzi and a Norwegian sauna for a stay geared more toward relaxation. The choice between the two depends mainly on budget and the purpose of the stay, not the size of the group.</p>

<p>This question comes up regularly among couples and small families comparing the two formats before booking in Filfila, Jeanne d'Arc district, 700 m from the sea. <h2>Two F2 apartments, one capacity, two different experiences</h2>

<p>Before getting into detail, it's worth clearing up a point that often causes confusion: the classic F2 and the jacuzzi F2 are not distinguished by size. Both apartments sleep up to 4 guests at base capacity, with the option to add extra guests under the same conditions. The <a href="/en/blog/quelle-taille-appartement-choisir">apartment size guide by number of travellers</a> confirms this point for every format at the residence.</p>

<h3>What the two F2 apartments have in common</h3>

<p>Reversible air conditioning, equipped kitchen, high-speed Wi-Fi, full bedding, free secure parking, upkeep between stays and no security deposit at all: this base is identical for both formats. The article on <a href="/en/blog/equipements-appartements-skikda">equipment provided in Skikda apartments</a> details this shared base across the whole residence.</p>

<h3>What actually sets them apart</h3>

<p>The only structural difference is the addition, in the jacuzzi F2, of a two-seat private jacuzzi and a wood-cabin Norwegian sauna, both for free use and included in the nightly rate. The classic F2 includes neither. This wellness equipment, and this alone, accounts for the price gap between the two formats.</p>

It suits both a straightforward beach stay and a shorter stopover on a longer itinerary, with no extra equipment to manage beyond everyday living.</p>

<h2>Which format to choose depending on the stay</h2>

<p>The choice rarely comes down to a question of space, since both F2 apartments sleep the same number of guests. It's more a matter of the purpose of the stay and the budget available for wellness equipment.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Couple on a romantic getaway or honeymoon: the jacuzzi F2 better suits this type of stay</li>
  <li>Family of 4 on a tight budget: the classic F2 covers the same space needs at a more accessible rate</li>
  <li>Off-season stay, autumn or winter: the jacuzzi and sauna really come into their own after a cool coastal walk</li>
  <li>Short stopover, one to three nights: the classic F2 remains sufficient without extra equipment</li>
  <li>Couple's anniversary or a special occasion: the jacuzzi F2 adds a dimension the classic F2 doesn't offer</li>
</ul>

<p>For a group larger than 4 guests, neither format is the best fit: the <a href="/en/blog/f3-terrasse-privative-skikda">F3 with private terrace</a> remains the better option from 5 travellers upward.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes when making this choice</h2>

<p>Several recurring points of confusion come up among travellers torn between the two formats, usually from a lack of information before booking rather than any real disagreement about their needs.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Assuming the jacuzzi F2 sleeps more guests than the classic F2, when base capacity is strictly identical</li>
  <li>Booking a jacuzzi F2 for a family of 5 or more, a size better covered by the F3 with terrace</li>
  <li>Underestimating the appeal of the jacuzzi and sauna off-season, when the cool, humid autumn and winter climate suits them particularly well</li>
  <li>Comparing only the listed price without factoring in what it actually includes: water, electricity, upkeep and no security deposit are identical in both formats</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last minute to book the jacuzzi F2 in summer, when it's the residence's most requested unit for that period</li>
</ul>

<h2>What about a studio or an F3 instead?</h2>

<p>Choosing between the classic F2 and the jacuzzi F2 only makes sense for a group of 3 to 4 people. Below that, a studio remains more economical for a solo traveller or a couple with no need for extra space; above it, an F3 with private terrace becomes necessary for an extended family or a group of friends. Comparing the residence's four formats before deciding between the two F2 units avoids paying for oversized space or, conversely, feeling cramped for the whole stay.</p>
