<p>Choosing between <strong>Airbnb and direct booking</strong> at Oasis Residence mostly depends on when the question comes up. Direct booking via WhatsApp avoids the 15-20% commission charged by third-party platforms and unlocks the 10% discount from 7 nights onward, while Airbnb remains handy for an initial browse or a quick booking without prior contact.</p>

<p>For a traveller preparing a stay in Filfila, in the Skikda region, this question often arises once a listing has been spotted on an international platform: should you book directly on it, or contact the residence to book directly instead? This article details how each channel works, compares real costs, payment methods, deposit policy, and offers a simple method for choosing based on your traveller profile.</p>

<h2>How booking via Airbnb works</h2>

<p>On a platform like Airbnb, the traveller checks the listing, verifies the availability calendar, and pays the full amount by card at the time of booking. The platform acts as an intermediary for all communication, payment, and, in theory, dispute resolution.</p>

<h3>What the displayed price on the platform covers</h3>

<p>The price shown on Airbnb systematically includes the platform's commission, largely taken from the amount paid by the traveller. This commission, which generally reaches 15-20% of the total amount, doesn't always appear in detail in the initial nightly rate shown, but shows up in the final summary before payment.</p>

<h2>How direct booking at Oasis Residence works</h2>

<p>Direct booking follows a different path, centred on human exchange rather than an automated platform system. Our <a href="/en/blog/comment-reserver-appartement-skikda-a-distance">guide to booking an apartment in Skikda remotely</a> details every step in depth.</p>

<h3>Direct contact via WhatsApp</h3>

<p>Everything starts with a WhatsApp exchange to check the availability of the desired apartment format (studio, F2, F2 with jacuzzi, or F3 with terrace) and to learn the rate applicable to the stay's period. This exchange also allows asking precise questions about equipment, capacity, or access, something a standardized platform messaging system doesn't always allow as easily.</p>

<h3>A one-night deposit via bank transfer</h3>

<p>Once dates are confirmed, a deposit equal to one night is paid by bank transfer to block the apartment. The remaining balance is paid directly on arrival, in Algerian dinars or euros depending on the traveller's preference, as detailed in <a href="/en/blog/moyens-paiement-reservation-skikda">our article on payment methods for booking in Skikda</a>. No international bank card is needed at any stage.</p>

<h2>Concrete advantages of direct booking</h2>

<p>For a stay already well defined in terms of dates and apartment format, direct booking offers several measurable advantages compared to booking through a third-party platform.</p>

<ul>
  <li>No platform commission passed on to the final price paid by the traveller</li>
  <li>Automatic access to the 10% discount from 7 consecutive nights onward</li>
  <li>Ability to negotiate a long stay or a booking spanning several apartments for the same group</li>
  <li>No deposit or prior bank hold</li>
  <li>Flexibility to pay the balance in dinars or euros depending on the traveller's situation</li>
  <li>Direct human contact, useful for clarifying specific needs before arrival</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why some travellers still prefer Airbnb</h2>

<p>Direct booking isn't automatically the default choice for every traveller profile. Airbnb keeps its own strengths, particularly for a first stay in Algeria or a booking made in a hurry.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Centralized messaging with conversation history kept on the platform</li>
  <li>Built-in customer support in case of a dispute</li>
  <li>An already familiar process for many travellers who regularly book this way internationally</li>
  <li>The ability to compare several listings on the same screen before deciding</li>
</ul>

<p>This framework reassures some travellers, particularly those discovering the Skikda region for the first time, even though direct contact via WhatsApp remains just as responsive day to day, with a reply generally received within a few hours.</p>

<h2>Concrete steps to move from Airbnb to direct booking</h2>

<p>A traveller who spotted an apartment on a platform can easily switch to a direct booking for a future stay, or even from the very first contact if they'd rather avoid the platform's commission.</p>

<ol>
  <li>Contact the residence via <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a> with the desired dates</li>
  <li>Receive confirmation of availability and the rate applicable to the chosen period</li>
  <li>Pay the one-night deposit by bank transfer to block the dates</li>
  <li>Prepare the balance in cash (DZD or EUR) for payment on arrival</li>
  <li>Arrive at the apartment for the <a href="/en/blog/check-in-arrivee-residence-oasis">check-in and arrival at the residence</a></li>
</ol>

<h2>Cases where Airbnb can still make sense</h2>

<p>In certain situations, booking through a platform still makes sense: a last-minute stay booked outside WhatsApp's usual reply hours, a traveller discovering Filfila for the first time who prefers the reassuring framework of a known platform, or a group still comparing several destinations before settling on one. In these cases, nothing prevents switching to a direct booking for a following stay, once the residence has been tried and approved.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes when choosing a booking channel</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Always booking through a platform without comparing the direct rate, even though the commission can reach 15-20% of the final price</li>
  <li>Forgetting to ask about the 10% discount from 7 nights onward when booking directly</li>
  <li>Thinking an international bank card is required to book directly, when only a bank transfer is needed for the deposit</li>
  <li>Not planning for the cash payment of the balance on arrival with a direct booking</li>
  <li>Hesitating to contact the residence directly via WhatsApp out of fear of a less structured channel, when the process actually stays simple and transparent</li>
</ul>
