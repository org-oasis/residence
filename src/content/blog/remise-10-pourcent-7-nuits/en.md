<p>At Oasis Residence, any rental of <strong>7 nights</strong> or more automatically benefits from a <strong>10% discount</strong> on the rate in force, whatever the format booked. There is no other tier: no discount at 14 nights, no variable percentage, just this single threshold applied from the first full week.</p>

<p>For a traveller planning a stay of a week or more in Filfila, this rule simplifies the total budget calculation even before contacting the residence. What remains to clarify is exactly what amount the discount is calculated on, whether it combines with seasonal rate changes, and when it appears in the quote. This article details the mechanism, with calculation examples by apartment format.</p>

<h2>The principle: a single threshold at 7 nights, 10% off</h2>

<p>The rule fits in one sentence: as soon as the length of stay reaches or exceeds <strong>7 consecutive nights</strong>, a <strong>10%</strong> discount applies to the total rate. This principle stays strictly the same for the four apartment formats of the residence, with no variation by capacity or equipment.</p>

<h3>No intermediate tier, no higher tier</h3>

<p>Unlike some establishments that offer a progressive discount scale (for example 5% from 5 nights, 15% from 15 nights), Oasis Residence works with a single threshold. Booking 7 nights or booking 21 nights gives the same 10% discount rate: there is no additional tier at 14 days, nor a higher percentage for a longer stay.</p>

<h2>How the discount is calculated, step by step</h2>

<p>The calculation happens in two steps: first the total rate of the stay with no discount, then the 10% is applied to that amount. The discount is never applied night by night, but on the whole stay once the 7-night threshold is reached.</p>

<h3>Example with a classic F2</h3>

<p>For a classic F2 listed at <strong>€{{TIER_F2_EUR}} / {{TIER_F2_DZD}} DA</strong> per night at the base rate, a week of 7 nights first comes to seven times that nightly rate, before any reduction. The 10% discount is then deducted from that total to get the final amount of the stay.</p>

<h3>Example with an F3 with terrace for a larger group</h3>

<p>For an F3 with terrace listed at <strong>€{{TIER_F3_EUR}} / {{TIER_F3_DZD}} DA</strong> per night and able to host up to 8 guests, the same principle applies: the nightly rate is multiplied by the number of nights booked, then reduced by 10% once the stay reaches 7 nights. <h2>Does the discount combine with seasonal rates?</h2>

<p>Nightly rates at Oasis Residence vary by time of year: higher in July-August, intermediate in early and mid-September or early and mid-June, more accessible the rest of the year. The 7-night discount applies to the rate matching the booked period, whichever it is.</p>

<ul>
  <li>A stay of 7 nights or more in July-August: 10% off the high-season rate</li>
  <li>A stay of 7 nights or more in June or September: 10% off the intermediate rate of that period</li>
  <li>A stay of 7 nights or more the rest of the year: 10% off the base rate</li>
</ul>

<p>A long stay booked outside the busiest periods therefore combines an already more accessible nightly rate with the 10% reduction, making it the most advantageous option in absolute value. Choosing the right period is detailed in the article on <a href="/en/blog/reserver-appartement-skikda-hors-saison">booking an apartment in Skikda off-season</a>.</p>

<h2>Automatic discount: nothing to negotiate</h2>

<p>The 10% discount is neither negotiable upward nor conditional on a specific request from the traveller. It is built directly into the quote sent during the WhatsApp exchange, as soon as the stated dates cover 7 nights or more.</p>

<h3>What the quote actually shows</h3>

<p>When the booking is confirmed for a length of stay of 7 nights or more, the total amount communicated already includes the reduction: there is no need to claim it separately or check it line by line. <h3>And what about the deposit?</h3>

<p>One point is worth clarifying: the booking deposit stays based on the rate of a single night, with no account taken of the discount. It is the final balance, paid on arrival in DZD or EUR, that includes the 10% reduction on the whole stay. <h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<p>A few points of confusion come up regularly among travellers comparing rental offers in Skikda before booking for a week or more.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Assuming a 14-night stay gives a discount above 10%: it does not, the tier stays single</li>
  <li>Confusing the length-of-stay discount with a one-off promotion displayed on a third-party platform</li>
  <li>Calculating the discount night by night instead of applying it to the total stay amount</li>
  <li>Forgetting that the surcharge for extra guests is calculated separately from the discount</li>
  <li>Waiting until the end of the stay to ask for the discount instead of checking it is already in the initial quote</li>
</ul>
