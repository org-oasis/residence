<p>Yes, working remotely from Résidence Oasis in Filfila is possible: all <strong>7 apartments</strong> at the residence have Wi-Fi, air conditioning and a space to set up a laptop, <strong>700 m from the sea</strong>. The studio suits a solo remote worker, the F2 a couple, the F3 with terrace a family combining remote work and vacation over several weeks.</p>

<p>More and more travelers look to extend a stay in Skikda by working a few hours a day from their apartment, before or after the beach. This article details what a remote-work stay in Filfila actually involves: connection quality, the apartment format best suited to your situation, how to organize a typical day, and the practical points to check before booking a multi-week stay.</p>

<h2>Why Filfila suits remote work</h2>

<p>Filfila, the Jeanne d'Arc neighborhood of Skikda province, combines a quiet setting outside the peak of summer with a location 700 m from the sea, making it easy to switch between a work session and a beach break. The site sits around 20 km from downtown Skikda, giving simple access to shops and services without the daily bustle of a city center.</p>

<h3>An internet connection included in every unit</h3>

<p>Wi-Fi is part of the amenities shared across every unit at the residence, alongside air conditioning, TV, fridge, oven and washing machine. No separate plan or surcharge is charged for the connection: it's included in the nightly rate, whichever format is booked. For a broader look at internet access in the area's rentals, <a href="/en/blog/wifi-haut-debit-location-skikda">a dedicated article on the blog</a> covers the topic in more depth.</p>

<h3>Quiet outside the peak season</h3>

<p>July and August bring the bulk of tourist traffic to Filfila, with a livelier atmosphere by day and by night. For a work-focused stay, the months surrounding the swimming season (June, September) or the off-season offer a quieter setting, while still keeping access to the sea when the weather allows.</p>

<h2>Which format to choose based on your remote-work profile</h2>

<p>The residence offers four formats, from studio to F3, with different capacities and base rates. The choice mainly depends on how many people are present during the stay and whether a work space separate from the living area is needed.</p>

<h3>Studio Deluxe for a solo remote worker</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/studio-deluxe-1">ground-floor deluxe studio</a> suits someone traveling alone to work remotely. Across 23 m², it combines a sleeping area, an equipped kitchen and a bathroom, with a base capacity of 3 people. On the ground floor with no stairs, it also makes daily trips with equipment or groceries easier. A dedicated article covers <a href="/en/blog/appartement-solo-skikda">choosing an apartment for a solo stay</a> in more detail.</p>

<h3>F2 for a couple who both work remotely</h3>

<p>For two people working remotely in parallel, a classic or modern F2 offers a living room separate from the bedroom, allowing an improvised desk space to be set apart from the rest of the unit. Base capacity is 4 people, with up to 2 extra guests possible for a surcharge if the stay includes occasional visitors.</p>

<h3>F3 with terrace for an extended remote-work family stay</h3>

<p>For a family combining parents' remote work and children's remote schooling over several weeks, the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> offers the most space at the residence, with a base capacity of 8 people. The private terrace makes it possible to bring a laptop outside outside the hottest hours, without leaving the unit.</p>

<h2>Organizing your days between work and a beach break</h2>

<p>The sea, 700 m from the units, makes it possible to organize a typical day combining focused work in the morning and a break in the late afternoon, during the swimming season from June to November, with water reaching around 26°C in August. Many remote workers on extended stays schedule their focused work or calls early in the morning or late in the day, to enjoy the sea at the most pleasant hours.</p>

<h3>A climate that stays workable year-round</h3>

<p>Outside the summer period, Skikda's climate stays mild, making it comfortable to work in the unit without constantly relying on air conditioning. For an off-season remote-work stay, this climate stability makes it easier to keep a regular routine.</p>
