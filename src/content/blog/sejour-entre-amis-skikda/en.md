<h2>Why Skikda suits a trip with friends so well</h2>

<p>Filfila, 700 m from the sea, brings together everything a group of friends looks for on holiday: beaches within walking distance, a relaxed seaside atmosphere and a bathing season running from June to November. Unlike a family trip, where priorities often revolve around children, a group of friends generally values flexibility, communal living and immediate proximity to the beach and evening outings.</p>

<h3>One address for the whole group</h3>

<p>Housing everyone in the same place considerably simplifies organizing a trip with friends: no coordination between several addresses in town, a single meeting point for heading to the beach or an excursion, and free, secure parking shared by the whole group on arrival.</p>

<h2>One F3 or several apartments: how to choose</h2>

<p>Up to 8 or 10 people, a single F3 with terrace is more than enough and remains the simplest option to organize. Beyond that threshold, or if the group brings together several sub-groups of friends who know each other less well, combining the F3 with one or more F2 units becomes a better option: everyone keeps their own privacy for sleeping while sharing the F3's terrace as a common living space for meals and evenings. <h2>Which season to choose for a trip with friends</h2>

<p>The ideal period mostly depends on what the group prioritizes. For a lively atmosphere and the sea at its warmest, <a href="/en/blog/climat-skikda-ete">summer in Skikda</a>, in July-August, remains the most obvious choice: the bathing season is in full swing and evenings on the terrace benefit from long, sunny days. It's also the most in-demand period, so the least flexible for available dates on an F3.</p>

<p>For a group of friends with more flexible dates, or who'd rather avoid the peak crowds, late June or late September offer a good compromise: the sea stays swimmable within the June-to-November season, the atmosphere is a bit calmer, and rates sit closer to the base rate than the summer peak. An extended weekend outside July-August is a particularly good option for a group that values calm over maximum buzz.</p>

<h2>Booking and splitting the budget among friends</h2>

<p>Booking is done directly via WhatsApp, with a one-night deposit paid by bank transfer to confirm the dates. The balance is settled on arrival, in dinars or euros, and no deposit is required, which simplifies the upfront outlay for a group that needs to cover costs collectively. One person from the group can centralize contact with the team via the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>, then split the shares among friends as they see fit.</p>

<h3>The 7-night discount, an asset for an extended stay</h3>

<p>A 10% discount automatically applies from 7 nights onward, regardless of how many people are staying in the apartment. For a group of friends planning a full week rather than a simple weekend, this discount noticeably lowers the cost per person once the budget is shared among all participants.</p>
