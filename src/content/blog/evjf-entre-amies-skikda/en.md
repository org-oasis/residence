<p>For a <strong>bachelorette trip to Skikda</strong>, the F3 with private terrace at Résidence Oasis accommodates up to 8 friends at base capacity, and up to 10 with the two extra guests allowed for a surcharge. The apartment sits 700 m from the sea, in the Jeanne d'Arc district in Filfila, making it easy to combine beach, terrace and evenings with friends without relying on a car.</p>

<p>Organizing a bachelorette trip away from home raises a simple but decisive question: choosing an apartment large enough for the group to live together without feeling cramped, while keeping a real outdoor space for relaxed moments. This article explains why the F3 with terrace meets that need, how to organize the programme on site, what budget to plan, and how to book without any bad surprises.</p>

<h2>Why Filfila suits a bachelorette trip</h2>

<p>Filfila brings together the ingredients of a great weekend with friends: beaches within walking distance, a bathing season running from June to November, and a calmer seaside atmosphere than a busy town centre. For a group looking above all to reconnect, away from everyday constraints, this setting avoids the scattering that a stay split across several hotels would cause.</p>

<h3>A single meeting point for the whole group</h3>

<p>Housing all the friends in the same place simplifies the organization: a single place to gather in the morning, shared free and secure parking, and no coordination between several scattered addresses in town. For a group sometimes coming from several different cities, this centralization avoids wasted time from day one.</p>

<h3>An alternative to formulas organized in town</h3>

<p>Rather than a series of separate bookings (hotel, restaurant, activity), an apartment with a private terrace serves as both a living space and a backdrop for shared moments: breakfast on the terrace, getting ready before the beach, returning in the evening to change before heading out again.</p>

<h2>The F3 with terrace, built for group living</h2>

Its 132 m² spread across several rooms leaves everyone a corner to get ready, without the whole group having to cross paths in a single space.</p>

<h2>The F2 with jacuzzi, an option for a smaller group</h2>

<p>For a bachelorette trip bringing together only 4 friends, or for a group that wants to combine group living with a dedicated relaxation moment, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> offers an interesting alternative. Its base capacity of 4 people and private jacuzzi make it a relevant format for a wellness break, either on its own or alongside the F3.</p>

<h3>Combining both apartments</h3>

<p>Some larger groups book the F3 and the F2 jacuzzi in parallel: the F3 serves as the main living space for everyone, while the F2 jacuzzi hosts a few friends in turns for a relaxation moment before rejoining the rest of the group. This formula remains possible thanks to the residence's seven independent units, bookable simultaneously on the same site.</p>

<h2>Typical programme for a bachelorette trip in Filfila</h2>

<p>A good balance for a weekend or multi-day stay with friends combines beach time, relaxation and a slightly more festive moment without relying on a night out in town.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Morning and afternoon beach time in Filfila, a few minutes' walk from the residence</li>

<h2>Booking and splitting the budget among friends</h2>

<p>Booking is done directly via WhatsApp, with a one-night deposit paid by bank transfer to confirm the dates. The balance is settled on arrival, in dinars or euros, and no deposit is required, which simplifies the upfront outlay for a group that often has to pool money together before departure. One person from the group can centralize contact with the team via the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>, then split the shares among friends as organized.</p>

<h2>Which season to choose for a bachelorette trip in Skikda</h2>

<p>The bathing season runs from June to November, leaving a wide window to organize the event. July-August brings the liveliest atmosphere and the warmest sea, but also the strongest demand on the F3, worth booking several weeks ahead. Late June or late September offer a good compromise: a still-pleasant sea, a calmer atmosphere and rates closer to the base rate than the summer peak.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid for a bachelorette trip among friends</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Underestimating the actual number of participants and discovering on site that a per-person surcharge applies beyond base capacity</li>
  <li>Booking a simple F2 thinking it can house 6 or 8 friends in a space designed for 4</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last minute to book the F3 in peak high season, a period when this format books up quickly</li>
  <li>Not designating in advance a person responsible for the deposit and coordination with the residence team</li>
  <li>Forgetting to plan a budget for activities (boat excursion, dinner) on top of the accommodation</li>
</ul>
