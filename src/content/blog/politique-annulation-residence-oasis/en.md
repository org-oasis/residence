<p>Résidence Oasis's <strong>cancellation policy</strong> rests on a simple setup: a one-night deposit by CCP bank transfer confirms the booking, the balance is paid on arrival in dinars or euros, and no security deposit is ever required. The exact terms for refunding the deposit, depending on how far ahead a cancellation is made, are confirmed directly with the team by WhatsApp at the time of booking.</p>

<p>For a traveller preparing a stay in Filfila, the cancellation question often comes up even before booking: what happens in case of an unexpected event, a date change, or a delayed flight from Constantine or Annaba? This article details how booking works at Résidence Oasis, what is guaranteed (no security deposit, payment terms), what depends on a case-by-case basis (deposit refund depending on notice period), and the good habits to adopt before confirming a stay.</p>

<h2>How a booking at Résidence Oasis works</h2>

<p>Booking happens directly, without going through a third-party platform. The process is the same for all 7 apartments at the residence, from the studio to the F3 with terrace.</p>

<h3>Booking steps</h3>

<ul>
  <li>First contact by WhatsApp to check the availability of the desired apartment for the planned dates</li>
  <li>Confirmation of the dates and the rate that applies for the season</li>
  <li>Payment of a one-night deposit by CCP bank transfer to lock in the booking</li>
  <li>Payment of the balance on arrival, in Algerian dinars or euros, with no additional security deposit</li>
</ul>

<p>This process stays the same regardless of the length of stay, with a 10% discount applied automatically from 7 nights.</p>

<h2>Cancelling a booking: what to check before paying the deposit</h2>

<p>The exact terms for refunding the deposit in case of cancellation depend on the time between the request and the planned stay, as well as the period concerned. Rather than guessing a general rule, the safest approach is to ask the team directly by WhatsApp before paying the deposit, specifying the planned dates.</p>

<h3>Why the notice period matters</h3>

<p>A cancellation made well ahead of the stay leaves more time to offer the apartment to another traveller, which favours a more flexible outcome. Conversely, a cancellation just a few days before arrival, especially in July-August when demand is highest, leaves less room to manoeuvre. This is why the residence handles these situations case by case rather than through a single publicly posted scale.</p>

<h3>Good practices before booking</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Explicitly ask about the cancellation terms that apply to your dates before paying the deposit</li>
  <li>Keep a written record of the WhatsApp exchange confirming the agreed terms</li>
  <li>Report any change of plan as early as possible, rather than waiting until the last minute</li>
  <li>Check whether <a href="/en/blog/assurance-voyage-algerie">travel insurance</a> covers unexpected cancellation costs, especially for an international trip</li>
</ul>

<h2>Changing dates rather than cancelling</h2>

<p>When a date change is enough, it's generally handled more easily than a full cancellation, especially when requested with reasonable notice. Reporting the change by WhatsApp as soon as it's known, with the new desired dates and the apartment concerned, lets the team check availability and confirm quickly whether transferring the booking is possible.</p>

<h3>Common case: a delayed or rescheduled flight</h3>

<p>No airport serves Skikda directly: arrivals go through Constantine (Mohamed Boudiaf), about 100 km away, or Annaba (Rabah Bitat), about 110 km away. A flight delay or a longer-than-expected drive from either airport does not put the booking at risk if the residence is informed in advance; see also the practical advice on <a href="/en/blog/arrivee-tardive-residence-oasis">late arrival at Résidence Oasis</a> for handling this kind of situation without stress.</p>

<h2>Booking while limiting cancellation risk</h2>

<p>Certain choices made at booking time mechanically reduce the financial risk tied to an unexpected event, without changing the policy itself.</p>

<h3>Choosing the right period</h3>

<p>July and August concentrate the strongest demand, which reduces flexibility in case of a late cancellation. Booking outside this period, or as soon as the stay's dates are firm, generally leaves more room to adjust a booking if needed. To compare seasons, see the articles on the <a href="/en/blog/quand-partir-skikda">best time to visit Skikda</a>.</p>

<h3>Grouping several apartments</h3>

<p>For a group or extended family booking several apartments at once, the same principles apply to each unit: a one-night deposit per apartment, balance on arrival, no security deposit. Centralising cancellation questions in a single WhatsApp conversation, rather than handling each apartment separately, simplifies things if the group's dates need to change.</p>

<h2>Direct booking vs. a third-party platform: the impact on cancellation</h2>

<p>Booking directly with Résidence Oasis, rather than through a third-party platform, changes how a cancellation is handled. Direct, the conversation happens without an intermediary: a single WhatsApp exchange is enough to ask a question, request a date adjustment, or clarify a term before paying the deposit. On a platform, the same step often involves a form, a longer response time and standardised rules that don't always account for a traveller's specific situation.</p>

<h3>What direct booking simplifies</h3>

<ul>
  <li>A personalised answer to a specific cancellation question, rather than a generic FAQ</li>
  <li>The option to discuss a date adjustment before even paying the deposit</li>
  <li>No extra platform commission added on top of the listed rate</li>
  <li>A direct, traceable WhatsApp contact kept throughout the exchange</li>
</ul>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Paying the deposit without having first asked about the cancellation terms that apply to your dates</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last moment to report a change of plan or a flight delay</li>
  <li>Confusing the one-night deposit with a security deposit: the deposit confirms the booking, there is never a separate security deposit to pay</li>
  <li>Booking in very high season without anticipating that a late cancellation is harder for the residence to absorb then</li>
  <li>Not keeping the written exchange with the team in case it's needed later</li>
</ul>
