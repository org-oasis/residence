<p>No, <strong>Résidence Oasis does not have a swimming pool on site</strong>. Its 7 apartments rely on another asset: the sea is only 700 m on foot away, in the Jeanne d'Arc district of Filfila. Only one unit, the F2 with a private jacuzzi, offers an indoor wellness alternative.</p>

<p>This is a common question from travelers used to pool-equipped residences along the Mediterranean coast or in the Gulf. In Filfila, the calculation is different: being right next to the sea makes an artificial pool less essential than elsewhere, and the space a pool would take up instead serves free parking and private outdoor areas such as the F3's terrace. This article details what the residence actually offers in terms of water and wellness amenities, the real alternatives available on site and nearby, and how to organize a satisfying stay without a private pool.</p>

<h2>Why Résidence Oasis has no swimming pool</h2>

<p>The question is a logical one for any tourist-oriented furnished residence. In Filfila, several practical reasons explain the absence of a shared pool, and they reflect a deliberate choice tied to the location rather than an oversight.</p>

<h3>Choosing the seafront over a basin</h3>

<p>A shared pool makes sense in an area far from the coast, where it compensates for the lack of direct access to swimming. At 700 m from the beach, that logic reverses: the sea offers a volume of water, a freshness, and a setting no residence pool can replicate, and it stays free and accessible on foot throughout the swimming season, from June to November.</p>

<h3>Upkeep that would weigh on the rate</h3>

<p>A pool requires regular chemical treatment, at least minimal supervision, daily cleaning in high season, and a technical shutdown off-season. These costs almost always show up in the nightly rate. By relying on the beach rather than a basin, the residence keeps its base rates accessible while keeping the private jacuzzi as the only wellness feature billed at a premium level.</p>

<h2>What replaces the pool at Résidence Oasis</h2>

<p>Two amenities act as a concrete alternative to a pool, each suited to a different season and type of stay.</p>

<h3>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach, 700 m away</h3>

<p><strong>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</strong> (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), about 7 km long, is a few minutes' walk from the residence. It is the functional equivalent of an open-air pool, with water reaching about 26°C in August and a swimming season running from June to November. For the day-to-day of a beach stay, this proximity matters more than a residence pool, which is often smaller and sometimes billed as an extra elsewhere.</p>

<h3>The private jacuzzi in the F2 Suite</h3>

<p>For windy days, the off-season, or simply an evening of relaxation, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> offers a two-person tub and a Norwegian sauna, for exclusive use within the apartment. It is not a substitute for swimming, but a genuine plus for a couple who want a water-based option available year-round, independent of the weather or the sea-swimming season. The topic is covered in detail in the article on the <a href="/en/blog/appartement-jacuzzi-skikda">apartment with a jacuzzi in Skikda</a>.</p>

<h2>Pool and budget: what choosing the sea actually changes</h2>

<p>On the Algerian coast as elsewhere, an on-site pool almost always shows up in the nightly rate, whether billed as an extra or simply built into the advertised price. By relying on the sea, free and 700 m away, Résidence Oasis avoids that cost line and can put the amenity budget elsewhere: a full kitchen in all 7 units, reversible air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi, free and secure parking, and the private jacuzzi kept exclusive to the F2 Suite rather than a pool shared by every guest.</p>

<p>For a traveler comparing two similarly priced listings, one with a shared pool and one 700 m from the sea, the real question is less about the amenity itself than about how it will actually be used. A modest residence pool, often crowded in midsummer, rarely offers more comfort than a 7 km beach like Larbi Ben M'hidi, walkable and open around the clock.</p>

<h2>Which traveler profile actually needs a pool in Filfila</h2>

<p>The lack of a pool does not affect every profile the same way. Some travelers manage without any difficulty, while others will benefit from adding a water-park outing to their plans.</p>

<h3>Families with young children</h3>

<p>For a family with young children, the sea demands more vigilance than a small, shallow pool, especially outside supervised areas. A half-day at Marina d'Or Water Park can usefully round out the main beach stay in Filfila, particularly on a day when the sea is rough.</p>

<h3>Couples and solo travelers</h3>

<p>For a couple or a solo traveler, the combination of the sea 700 m away plus the private jacuzzi (for those booking the F2 Suite) largely covers the need for water-based relaxation, without the constraints of a shared pool or the fixed hours of a common area.</p>

<h3>Off-season stays</h3>

<p>From December to May, outside the sea-swimming period, only the F2 Suite's private jacuzzi remains available indoors. For an off-season stay without booking that unit, Rusica Park remains the closest option for occasional access to a pool area.</p>

<h2>Good to know before booking without a pool</h2>

<ul>
