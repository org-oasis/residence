<p>The apartments at <strong>Oasis Residence</strong> in Filfila are all <strong>non-smoking indoors</strong>, regardless of the booked type: Studio, classic F2, F2 with jacuzzi, or F3. Only the F3 with private terrace offers a dedicated outdoor space for smoking without leaving the apartment. This rule, common in professional serviced rentals, is worth knowing before booking to avoid any confusion on arrival.</p>

<p>For a smoker planning a stay in Skikda, the question is a fair one: can you smoke in the rented apartment, and if not, what are the alternatives on site? This article details the policy applied at Oasis Residence, the reasons behind it, the outdoor spaces actually available depending on the apartment type, and good practices for a smooth stay.</p>

<h2>The smoking policy at Oasis Residence</h2>

<p>The rule is simple and applies uniformly to all seven apartments of the residence: no cigarettes, cigars, or pipes indoors. This policy follows the one applied by the vast majority of professional serviced rentals, in Algeria as elsewhere, for practical reasons as much as hygienic ones.</p>

<h3>Why the apartments are non-smoking indoors</h3>

<p>Tobacco odour lastingly embeds itself in fabrics (curtains, sofas, bedding), in air-conditioning filters, and in porous surfaces. A smoked-in apartment quickly loses its fresh feel, even with regular maintenance between stays. As the <a href="/en/blog/equipements-appartements-skikda">full list of amenities provided in the apartments</a> shows, the residence invests in a carefully maintained equipment level (air conditioning, bedding, fitted kitchen) that indoor tobacco use would quickly degrade.</p>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/climatisation-location-skikda">reversible air conditioning</a> present in every apartment illustrates the issue well: a system that continuously circulates air also spreads smoke odour faster throughout every room, including the bedroom. An air-conditioning system clogged by smoke also loses efficiency and needs more frequent servicing, which justifies particular care on this specific point.</p>

<h3>A rule that benefits every guest</h3>

<p>Beyond maintenance, this policy directly protects the comfort of the next guests, who often move into the same apartment just a few days after the previous group leaves. Families with young children, people with asthma, or simply travellers sensitive to tobacco odour benefit from an interior guaranteed free of cigarette traces, regardless of the apartment's occupancy history.</p>

<h2>Where to smoke during the stay depending on the apartment type</h2>

<p>Not every apartment has the same private outdoor space, which concretely changes the options available to a smoking guest.</p>

<h3>The F3 with private terrace, the best-suited type</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/f3-terrasse-privative-skikda">F3 with private terrace</a> is the only type in the residence offering a dedicated outdoor space, directly accessible from the apartment. For a group or extended family that includes one or more smokers, this type naturally resolves the question without time constraints or needing to leave the building. The terrace remains a private-use space reserved for the F3's occupants, designed first for outdoor meals and relaxing, but perfectly suited to this occasional use as well.</p>

<h3>Studios and F2 units, with no private outdoor space</h3>

<p>The Studios and most F2 units of the residence, including the <a href="/en/blog/f2-classique-ou-jacuzzi-skikda">classic F2 or the F2 with jacuzzi</a>, have no balcony or private terrace. For these types, the only option compliant with the rules is to step outside the building, for example near the residence's parking area, rather than smoking inside the apartment or just outside the indoor door.</p>

<h3>The special case of the F2 with jacuzzi</h3>

<p>The F2 with jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna concentrates its main appeal on the indoor wellness equipment, with no associated outdoor space. A smoker weighing this type against the F3 with terrace should factor this criterion into their choice, alongside guest capacity and budget for the stay.</p>

<h2>What the non-smoking rule actually covers</h2>

<p>The indoor non-smoking policy applies to every room of the apartment, with no exception for assumed sufficient surface area or ventilation.</p>

<h3>E-cigarettes and vaping</h3>

<p>The same logic applies to vaping. Vapour produced by an e-cigarette can soak into fabrics in a way comparable to classic tobacco smoke, even if the odour fades faster. It is therefore best to vape under the same conditions as for a cigarette: on the F3's private terrace, or outside the building for the other types.</p>

<h2>What happens in case the rule is not followed</h2>

<p>Smoking indoors despite the non-smoking policy has concrete consequences, beyond simple discomfort for the next guests.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Persistent odour requiring thorough cleaning of fabrics, curtains, and bedding</li>
  <li>Faster clogging of air-conditioning filters</li>
  <li>Additional cleaning fees charged at checkout if damage is noted</li>
</ul>

<p>To avoid any ambiguity, it is best to clarify this point directly when making first contact for the booking, especially for a group including several regular smokers.</p>

<h2>Comparing with hotel policies in Skikda</h2>

<p>The smoking question does not only arise for private rentals or serviced residences like Oasis Residence. Hotels in the wilaya generally apply comparable rules, sometimes with a limited number of rooms designated as smoking, often located on a specific floor or near an exit. This supply nonetheless remains limited and does not guarantee a private outdoor space comparable to a terrace.</p>

<h3>The advantage of a private outdoor space</h3>

<p>In a classic hotel, a smoking guest depends on the opening hours and availability of a shared space, sometimes shared with other guests. The <strong>F3 with private terrace</strong> type at Oasis Residence reverses this logic: the outdoor space belongs exclusively to the apartment's occupants, accessible at any time, with no constraint of sharing with other travellers. This is a point worth considering when choosing between a downtown hotel and an apartment in Filfila.</p>

<h2>Planning ahead before booking</h2>

<p>For a group including one or more regular smokers, the right approach is to ask the question directly when making first contact, before confirming the booking. The residence is booked via <strong>WhatsApp</strong>, a direct channel that provides a quick answer about the availability of the F3 with terrace or any other question related to the house rules.</p>

<h3>One criterion among others</h3>

<p>The presence of a private outdoor space is only one criterion among others when choosing an apartment type: guest capacity, available budget, and desired amenities (jacuzzi, sauna, fitted kitchen) also come into play. For a smoker traveling alone or as a couple with no need for large capacity, a Studio or an F2 remains perfectly suitable, provided they accept stepping outside the building to smoke, a minor constraint compared to the rest of the stay.</p>

<h2>Good practices for a smooth stay</h2>

<p>A few simple habits help avoid any friction related to this rule during the stay.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Bring a pocket ashtray or a closed container for cigarette butts during outings outside the building</li>
  <li>Prefer the F3 with private terrace for a group including several regular smokers</li>
  <li>Systematically air out the apartment after time spent on a balcony or terrace, even outdoor ones</li>
  <li>Raise any question about the house rules directly via WhatsApp before booking</li>
  <li>Apply the same rules to guests received occasionally in the apartment</li>
</ul>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>

<p>The apartments at Oasis Residence are <strong>non-smoking indoors</strong>, a policy that protects the quality of the apartments and the comfort of every guest. The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> remains the best-suited type for a smoker who wants an outdoor space without leaving the apartment, while the Studios and F2 units require stepping outside the building. To compare all available types and their respective amenities before booking, <a href="/en/apartments">all residence accommodations</a> are detailed online, and any specific question gets a quick answer via the residence's <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>.</p>
