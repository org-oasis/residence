<p>Having a family picnic around Skikda is easy and free: Filfila's beaches, the Filfila forest, and the edges of the Stora - Carrière trail each offer a different setting, all within 20 minutes of the Jeanne d'Arc neighborhood. No booking is needed, and the only thing that really matters is choosing the right time of day during the summer heat.</p>

<p>For a family staying in Filfila, a picnic is one of the simplest outings to organize between two swims: little gear needed, no entry cost, and several possible settings depending on the mood of the day. This article details the best spots for a picnic around Skikda, seasonal precautions, what to pack, and how to easily fit this activity into a beach vacation.</p>

<h2>Filfila's beaches, the simplest picnic setting</h2>

<p>For a family staying in Filfila, the beach remains the most immediate option: reachable on foot from most residences, 700 m from the sea, requiring no car trip to combine a swim and a picnic in the same outing.</p>

<h3>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach, plenty of room to settle in</h3>

<p>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), which stretches for about 7 km, leaves plenty of room to find an open spot, even during peak season. The gentle slope at the water's edge makes it easier to keep an eye on children between bites, a real advantage for families with young kids alternating between playing in the water and taking a meal break.</p>

<h3>Les Calatines, a more sheltered alternative</h3>

<p>Les Calatines beach, more tucked between the coastal reliefs, offers natural shade in places early and late in the day, unlike a fully open beach where shade depends entirely on a brought-along parasol. It suits a family looking for a slightly more intimate setting than the Grande Plage for their picnic.</p>

<h2>Filfila forest, natural shade to complement the beach</h2>

<p>For the hottest days of summer, <strong>Filfila forest</strong> offers a valuable alternative to a beach in full sun. The pine cover provides steady natural shade all day, a comfort no parasol on the sand can match.</p>

<h3>A welcome setting outside swimming hours</h3>

<p>The forest works particularly well for a midday picnic, when the sun is at its highest and the beach becomes uncomfortable without shade. The terrain remains manageable in the most frequented access areas, with a more pronounced relief further into the massif, best suited to families with older children or those used to walking.</p>

<h3>Combining the forest with a short hike</h3>

<p>A short walk through <a href="/en/blog/randonnee-foret-filfila">Filfila forest</a> before settling in for the picnic lets you get two things done at once: light physical activity followed by a shaded meal break, a format that works well to fill half a day without relying solely on the beach.</p>

<h2>The Stora - Carrière trail, a picnic stop after the walk</h2>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/randonnee-stora-carriere">Stora - Carrière trail</a>, walkable in about thirty minutes, lends itself well to a picnic stop once you reach the end of the route or halfway, depending on the children's stamina. The view over the rocky coastline adds a different setting from Filfila's classic beaches, appreciated by families looking to vary the scenery during their stay.</p>

<h2>When to picnic: adapting the outing to the season</h2>

<p>The <strong>swimming season</strong>, from June to November, is also the best period to combine a picnic with the beach. In the height of summer, with sea water around 26°C in August, the heat makes shade more valuable than ever: it is best to favor Filfila forest or a morning slot at the beach rather than a midday picnic in full sun.</p>

<h3>Off-season, a quieter setting</h3>

<p>Outside the hottest months, a picnic is still possible on the beaches or in Filfila forest, with noticeably fewer people than in July-August. Families on a <a href="/en/blog/randonnee-famille-skikda">family hike near Skikda</a> often use this period to combine a walk with a meal break without the constraint of summer heat.</p>

<h2>What to pack for a successful picnic</h2>

<p>A family picnic in Skikda does not require sophisticated gear, but a few basics avoid unpleasant surprises, especially in the heat.</p>

<ul>
  <li>A cooler to keep food and drinks fresh, essential from late spring onward</li>
  <li>Water in quantities well above your initial estimate, especially with young children</li>
  <li>A blanket or mat, more practical than sitting directly on sand or rocks</li>
  <li>A parasol or umbrella for beach spots without natural shade</li>
  <li>Sunscreen and hats, even for a picnic planned in the shade</li>
  <li>Trash bags to systematically carry out all waste</li>
</ul>

<h2>Best practices for a family picnic</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Arrive early in the morning during peak summer season to find a shaded or open spot before the crowds</li>
  <li>Avoid the hottest midday hours in July-August, especially on a beach with no shade</li>
  <li>Check for any <a href="/en/blog/restrictions-baignade-skikda">swimming restrictions</a> before combining a picnic and a swim at the same spot</li>
  <li>Stay on flat, accessible areas of Filfila forest with young children</li>
  <li>Never leave trash behind, including food scraps, which attract local wildlife</li>
  <li>Bring a quiet activity (cards, a book) to extend the break after the meal</li>
</ul>

<p>A morning of <a href="/en/blog/activites-enfants-skikda">activities for children in Skikda</a> naturally winds down with a picnic, whether on the beach after a swim or in the shade of Filfila forest after a visit to the Marina d'Or Water Park. <h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Underestimating how much water is needed, especially with young children active on the beach</li>
  <li>Setting up the picnic in full midday sun in summer without a parasol or natural shade</li>
  <li>Forgetting trash bags, leaving the site less clean for the next visitors</li>
  <li>Choosing a forest spot without first checking for a flat, accessible area for young children</li>
  <li>Heading out without a cooler in peak summer heat, risking spoiled food quickly</li>
</ul>
