<p>In Skikda, <strong>seafood</strong> other than fish mostly comes down to three families: shrimp, squid and mussels, served grilled or fried at restaurants in the port of Stora and the city center. Unlike whole fish, which is on every menu, seafood remains more occasional, depending on the local fishermen's daily catch.</p>

<p>For a traveler planning a food-focused stay in Filfila, knowing where and how to enjoy something other than classic fish usefully broadens the dining options. This article covers the seafood available along the Skikda coast, the most reliable places to find it, how it's typically prepared, and how to build it into a stay organized around a well-equipped apartment in Filfila.</p>

<h2>The seafood available along the Skikda coast</h2>

<p>The wilaya of Skikda, with its active fishing ports in Stora and Collo, offers a <strong>seafood</strong> selection that's narrower than fish but regularly refreshed depending on the catch. Three products dominate what restaurants and stalls offer.</p>

<h3>Shrimp</h3>

<p>Shrimp is among the most sought-after seafood, served grilled with garlic and lemon or in a spiced tomato sauce. Size varies with the day's catch, with the largest pieces generally reserved for restaurants that work directly with the port's fishermen.</p>

<h3>Squid</h3>

<p>Squid comes in two main preparations: fried in breaded rings, the most widespread and accessible version, or grilled whole and then cut at the table, a preparation that better highlights the product's texture and freshness. Both versions are easy to find in Stora as well as in the city center.</p>

<h3>Mussels and shellfish</h3>

<p>Mussels appear more occasionally on menus, most often in a stewed dish or as part of a mixed seafood platter. Sea urchins and other shellfish remain marginal, more associated with casual seaside eating during the summer season than with a structured restaurant offering.</p>

<h2>Where to eat seafood in Skikda</h2>

<p>The choice of location directly affects freshness and variety, since seafood is more sensitive to storage time than whole fish.</p>

<h3>The port of Stora, a benchmark for freshness</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/poisson-frais-stora">fresh fish of Stora</a> is well known, but the port also serves as a preferred supply point for shrimp and squid at nearby restaurants. Boats coming in at the end of the morning supply neighboring kitchens directly, limiting the time between catch and plate.</p>

<h3>Skikda's city center</h3>

<p>Establishments in Skikda's city center offer a wider range of composed dishes, sometimes including mixed platters combining fish, shrimp and squid. It's a practical option for anyone who wants to try several products in a single meal without multiple outings.</p>

<h3>Collo, a quieter alternative</h3>

<p>The port of <a href="/en/blog/poisson-frais-collo">fresh fish in Collo</a> also offers seafood, in a more authentic, less touristy setting than Stora. The drive to Collo takes more time, but it suits a day trip combining a coastal visit with a seafood lunch.</p>

<h2>Seafood vs fish: what to choose for your stay</h2>

<p>Fish remains the <strong>backbone</strong> of Skikda's seafood cuisine, but shellfish adds welcome variety for a stay of several days. Alternating between the two avoids monotony, especially for a week-long stay or more where the same restaurant may be visited several times.</p>

<ul>
  <li>For a quick, affordable lunch: grilled whole fish remains the safest and most common choice</li>
  <li>To vary textures: fried or grilled squid offers an accessible alternative almost everywhere</li>
  <li>For a special occasion: a mixed platter with shrimp lets you try several products at once</li>
  <li>On a tight budget: mussels and squid generally stay more affordable than shrimp</li>
</ul>

<h2>Cooking seafood in your apartment in Filfila</h2>

<p>Beyond restaurants, many visitors prefer to buy fresh seafood and cook it themselves, an option particularly well suited to a longer stay. Apartments equipped with a <a href="/en/blog/appartement-cuisine-equipee-skikda">full kitchen in Skikda</a>, with a hob, oven, fridge and basic utensils, let you cook shrimp and squid bought directly at the market or from fishermen, at a lower cost than eating out.</p>

<h3>Practical buying tips</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Buy in the morning, when the boats are coming back from fishing</li>
  <li>Check that shrimp and squid are firm and free of a strong odor, a sign of freshness</li>
  <li>Bring a cooler for transport if the apartment is several kilometers from the point of purchase</li>
  <li>Ask for advice on suitable quantities, since seafood keeps less long than whole fish</li>
</ul>
