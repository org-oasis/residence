<p>Check-out from an apartment at <strong>Oasis Residence</strong> in Filfila comes down to one simple step: letting the team know the departure time and handing over the keys, with no formal inspection and no deposit to reclaim, across the residence's 7 apartments located 700 m from the sea.</p>

<p>After a stay in Skikda, departure logistics often get little attention during planning, even though they shape the end of the stay and sometimes the start of a journey to one of the region's two airports. This article details exactly how check-out works, what is and isn't required, the timing options, and the practical points worth anticipating before leaving Filfila.</p>

<h2>How check-out works at Oasis Residence</h2>

<p><strong>Leaving an apartment in Filfila</strong> doesn't follow a heavy protocol. There is no form to sign and no room-by-room inspection like some rentals that require a detailed exit report. The residence's team favours a direct, quick exchange, consistent with the simplicity already applied to <a href="/en/blog/check-in-arrivee-residence-oasis">checking in to an apartment in Filfila</a>.</p>

<h3>Letting the team know the departure time</h3>

<p>The day before or the morning of departure, a message on the WhatsApp conversation used for the booking is enough to state the expected time. This lets the team organise the turnover between stays, including cleaning and preparing the apartment for the next guest.</p>

<h3>Key handover and a quick check-in</h3>

<p>At departure, the keys are handed to the team along with a brief chat about the stay: which amenities worked well, any issue flagged during the stay, any remaining questions. This moment stays short, usually a few minutes, without an exhaustive check of every room.</p>

<h2>No deposit to reclaim at departure</h2>

<p>Unlike much of the seasonal rental market, Oasis Residence asks for <strong>no deposit</strong> at any stage, neither at booking nor on arrival. This absence of a deposit naturally simplifies check-out: there is no amount to return, no possible dispute over a withheld sum, no waiting for a refund after departure.</p>

<h3>The only case of additional billing</h3>

<p>Only deliberate, significant damage found after departure would lead to a discussion and separate billing. This case remains distinct from the normal check-out process and has nothing to do with the ordinary wear of a furnished apartment lived in for several days.</p>

<h2>What time should be planned for departure?</h2>

<p>Oasis Residence does not set a single check-out time imposed on all guests, unlike many hotels that apply a strict cut-off, often mid-morning. The timing is coordinated case by case over WhatsApp, depending on the apartment's availability for the next guest.</p>

<h3>An early departure to catch a flight</h3>

<p>For a flight departing from Constantine airport (Mohamed Boudiaf) or Annaba airport (Rabah Bitat), located roughly 100 km and 110 km from Filfila respectively, an early departure is arranged the day before with the team. Allow a little over an hour's drive to either airport depending on traffic, on top of the usual margin before a flight.</p>

<h3>A slightly later departure</h3>

<p>When the apartment isn't immediately booked by another guest, a slightly delayed departure is often possible at no extra cost, on simple request the day before or that morning. This isn't guaranteed during the swimming season, a period when the turnover between stays is tighter.</p>

<h2>What to do before leaving the apartment</h2>

<p>No deep cleaning is expected from guests: the full clean between stays is handled by the residence's team. A minimum of tidying still makes the transition easier and remains an appreciated courtesy.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Gather used dishes in the sink or dishwasher, depending on the format</li>
  <li>Take out the bins if full</li>
  <li>Close the windows and turn off the air conditioning</li>
  <li>Check that no personal items were left behind (chargers, clothes, documents)</li>
  <li>Hand over the keys at the time agreed with the team</li>
</ul>

<h3>Items forgotten after departure</h3>

<p>If something is found missing after departure, a message on the same WhatsApp conversation makes it possible to flag the item and arrange its retrieval or shipping, depending on distance and how much time remains before the next guest's stay.</p>

<h2>Extending the stay instead of leaving</h2>

<p>Some travellers realise at the last minute that they'd like to stay a few more nights. This is a different process from simply pushing back the departure time and depends on the apartment's availability for the extra period; how it works is detailed in the article on <a href="/en/blog/prolonger-sejour-residence-oasis">extending a stay at Oasis Residence</a>, worth reading before the last day rather than on the morning of departure.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes at departure</h2>

<p>A few mistakes come up regularly among travellers leaving Filfila, more often from lack of planning than from carelessness.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Not notifying the departure time, which complicates organising the cleaning between stays</li>
  <li>Waiting until the morning of departure to ask for a later check-out during high season</li>
  <li>Underestimating the drive time to Constantine or Annaba airport before a flight</li>
  <li>Forgetting to check drawers and the bathroom before handing over the keys</li>
  <li>Deciding at the last minute to extend the stay without having checked the apartment's availability</li>
</ul>

<h2>The check-out process by type of trip</h2>

<p>The basic process stays the same for every guest, but a few details vary depending on the group's makeup and the apartment format booked, from the 3-guest studio to the 8-guest F3 with terrace.</p>

<h3>A couple or a solo traveller</h3>

<p>For a stay in a <a href="/en/blog/appartement-solo-skikda">solo apartment in Skikda</a> or as a couple, check-out generally takes just a few minutes: fewer bags, less to tidy, and often a departure timed with the key handover agreed by WhatsApp.</p>

<h3>A family with children</h3>

<p>With children, allowing a bit more time before the announced departure helps avoid a last-minute rush: checking for toys or comfort items left in bedrooms, gathering beach gear, and making sure nothing was left in the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> or the booked format, especially on the terrace or balcony.</p>

<h3>A group across several apartments</h3>

<p>When a group occupies several units of the residence, it helps to coordinate a shared departure time or to notify separately for each apartment, so the team can organise the cleaning of each unit without confusion over the respective schedules.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>

<p>Checking out of an apartment at <strong>Oasis Residence in Filfila</strong> stays deliberately simple: no deposit to reclaim, no formal exit inspection, a time coordinated by WhatsApp rather than imposed. That means ending a stay in Skikda without unnecessary paperwork, whether before a flight from Constantine or Annaba or before hitting the road to another wilaya. For any question before the last day, the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a> remains the fastest starting point, and <a href="/en/blog">more tips on the blog</a> cover booking, budget and local activities.</p>
