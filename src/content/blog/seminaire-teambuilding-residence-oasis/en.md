<p>Organizing a <strong>corporate seminar in Filfila</strong> is entirely possible at Oasis Residence: its 7 apartments, including a 132 m² F3 with private terrace, allow a full team to be housed 700 m from the sea while having a shared space for group sessions. This article details actual capacity, logistics and the limits to know before booking for a team.</p>

<p>Between the standardized conference rooms of business hotels and the fixed setting of a classic seminar centre, more and more companies are looking for a hybrid format: comfortable lodging, an outdoor space for breaks and informal workshops, and an atmosphere that breaks from routine without sacrificing actual work. The Filfila coast, in the Skikda wilaya, meets this need for teams willing to trade some logistics for a seaside setting. This article details what Oasis Residence concretely offers for this type of stay, its limits, and how to organize the booking for a group.</p>

<h2>Why an apartment rather than a business hotel for a seminar</h2>

<p>A successful <strong>corporate seminar</strong> depends as much on the content of the sessions as on the setting in which they take place. A standard business hotel offers a functional meeting room but an often impersonal environment, not very conducive to the team cohesion sought in a team-building event. A set of furnished apartments instead recreates a shared-house dynamic: everyone has their own private space in the evening, while meeting in a common area during the day.</p>

<h3>The apartment format favours cohesion</h3>

<p>Unlike hotel rooms lined up along a corridor, apartments spread across several floors of the same residence naturally create subgroups at meals and during breaks. Participants sometimes cook together thanks to the fitted kitchens, which reinforces the team spirit sought in a team-building event, far from the impersonal breakfast room of a classic hotel.</p>

<h3>The F3 as a group session space</h3>

<p>The F3 with private terrace, 132 m² in size, acts as the central space for collective moments: a workshop in a circle, an end-of-day debrief, or simply a shared meal after a work session. It does not replace a fully equipped conference room (no screen or sound system supplied), but works very well for a participative work format in a small group, limited to the space available on site.</p>

<h2>Organizing the work side of the seminar</h2>

<p>A corporate seminar usually combines structured work sessions with downtime or team-building activities. The residence covers the second part well thanks to the proximity of the sea and local activities, but the first requires preparation in advance on the equipment side.</p>

<h3>Connectivity and digital tools</h3>

<p>Every apartment has <strong>high-speed Wi-Fi</strong> included in the rate, sufficient for video calls, sending heavy files or collaborating on online tools during hybrid sessions. For teams combining an on-site seminar with staying connected to colleagues back at the office, this point remains decisive in choosing accommodation, as detailed in the article on <a href="/en/blog/teletravail-residence-oasis">remote work from the residence</a>.</p>

<h3>Equipment to bring</h3>

<p>The residence supplies no projector, flipchart or sound system. For a formal presentation, the organizer must bring their own portable equipment (laptop, Bluetooth speaker, portable display stand). Teams needing heavier projection equipment are better off renting it in advance from a provider in Skikda or bringing it from their head office.</p>

<h2>The team-building and downtime side</h2>

<p>The sea's immediate proximity, <strong>700 m</strong> from the residence, and Filfila's natural sites are the main asset for the team-cohesion side of a seminar. Between work sessions, a walk to the beach, a group meal on the F3 terrace or a fishing trip nearby is often enough to break the routine of a classic seminar held in a closed room.</p>

<h2>Organizing the booking for a business group</h2>

<p>Booking several apartments at once requires slightly different coordination from an individual booking. It is recommended to centralize the request with a single contact person on the company side, who deals directly with the residence to confirm dates, the number of units and the team allocation before paying the deposits.</p>

<h3>Practical steps</h3>

<ol>
  <li>Determine the number of participants and the desired allocation by unit type</li>
  <li>Contact the residence via the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a> to check simultaneous availability of the 7 units on the target dates</li>
  <li>Pay a one-night deposit by CCP transfer for each unit blocked</li>
  <li>Pay the balance on arrival, in dinars or euros, with no security deposit required</li>
  <li>Plan a joint or staggered arrival depending on flights via the Constantine or Annaba airports</li>
</ol>

<h3>Cost and applicable discount</h3>

<p>The standard −10% discount applies from 7 nights per booked unit, as for any stay at the residence. For a short seminar of 2 to 4 nights, this threshold is not reached, but the off-peak rate already compares favourably with an equivalent block of hotel rooms in Skikda, especially since every apartment includes a fitted kitchen that cuts the meal bill over several days.</p>

<h2>Limits to anticipate before booking</h2>

<p>A few points are worth anticipating to avoid surprises when organizing a corporate seminar in this format.</p>

<ul>
  <li>No fully equipped conference room: bring your own presentation equipment</li>
  <li>No collective catering service on site: meals are prepared in the fitted kitchen or ordered from outside</li>
  <li>Total capacity limited to the 7 units: beyond roughly 27 people, additional lodging is needed</li>
  <li>No airport in Skikda: the group's arrival goes through Constantine (≈100 km) or Annaba (≈110 km), to be factored into the transfer schedule</li>
  <li>Booking several units should be coordinated early in high season (July-August), when simultaneous availability of all 7 apartments is more limited</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Oasis Residence</strong> is a realistic option for a mid-sized corporate seminar or team-building event, thanks to its 7 units, its 132 m² terrace and its immediate proximity to the sea. The format suits a seminar combining small-group work and team-cohesion moments better than a formal conference requiring heavy audiovisual equipment.
