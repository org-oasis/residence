<h2>The studio: who is it really suited for?</h2>

<p>The <strong>ground-floor deluxe studio</strong> at Résidence Oasis relies on compactness and efficiency. At 23 m², it works as a single space combining sleeping area, living room and equipped kitchen, with no interior wall. It's a layout designed for stays where the privacy of a closed bedroom matters less than the base rate and ease of use.</p>

<h3>Advantages of the studio</h3>

<p>The studio's main strength is its base rate, the most accessible of the residence's four layouts, and its ground-floor access, with no stairs to climb with luggage or a stroller. For a solo traveler, a couple with no children, or two friends on a short stay, the open layout poses no real constraint day to day.</p>

<h3>Limits to anticipate</h3>

<p>Beyond 3 people, the studio quickly becomes constraining: the single open space doesn't allow for separation, and adding extra guests strongly reduces available comfort. For a family with two children or a group looking for a real separation between night and day areas, the studio is generally not the right choice; the <a href="/en/blog/quelle-taille-appartement-choisir">guide to choosing the right apartment size</a> covers this point in more depth.</p>

<h2>The classic F2: who is it designed for?</h2>

<p>The <strong>classic F2</strong> adds 10 m² compared to the studio, mostly dedicated to a closed bedroom, separate from the living room. This separation changes day-to-day use in practice: parents can put a child to bed earlier without turning off the living room TV, or enjoy a lounge area distinct from the sleeping space.</p>

<h3>Advantages of the classic F2</h3>

<p>With a base capacity of 4 people, the F2 suits a couple with one or two children, or two adults who each want their own space. The separate bedroom remains the main argument: it allows hosting guests or staying up late in the evening without disturbing the other occupants' sleep, a comfort the studio cannot offer by design.</p>

<h3>When a larger layout is still preferable</h3>

<p>For a group of 5 or more, or an extended multi-generational family, neither the studio nor the classic F2 is really enough: the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a>, with its 8-person capacity, then becomes the natural layout. For a couple looking for a wellness feature rather than extra space, the <a href="/en/blog/f2-classique-ou-jacuzzi-skikda">comparison between the classic F2 and the F2 with jacuzzi</a> details this other alternative.</p>
