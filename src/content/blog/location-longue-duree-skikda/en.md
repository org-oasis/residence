<h2>The discount from 7 nights</h2>

<p>The rule is simple and unique: a <strong>10% discount applies from 7 nights</strong> of booking, on the base rate for the relevant period. There is no additional tier at 14 nights and no progressive scale by duration: whether the stay lasts a week, three weeks, or a full month, the discount stays fixed at 10% once the 7-night threshold is reached.</p>

<h3>Why a single threshold rather than a sliding scale</h3>

<p>This setup simplifies negotiation: there's no need to calculate a different rate depending on whether the stay lasts 10, 20, or 30 nights.
