<h2>How apartments in Skikda are heated</h2>

<p>Most recently built furnished rentals along the Skikda coast, including in Filfila, rely on <strong>reversible air conditioning</strong> rather than central gas heating or a classic electric radiator. It's the most common solution in recent Algerian housing, for a simple reason: a single unit covers both needs, hot and cold, with no extra installation.</p>

<h3>How a reversible air conditioner works</h3>

<p>Technically, a reversible air conditioner works as an air-to-air heat pump. In cooling mode, it draws heat out of the room and releases it outside. In heating mode, the cycle reverses: the unit captures heat contained in the outside air, even when it's cool, and releases it indoors. The same wall-mounted unit therefore handles both functions, controlled from a remote that allows precise temperature settings.</p>

<h3>A living area heated quickly</h3>

<p>In a compact studio or F2, the reversible air conditioning reaches a comfortable temperature within a few dozen minutes. The living room and bedroom each have their own unit, allowing you to heat only the room in use rather than the whole apartment, a more energy-efficient approach than classic central heating.</p>

<p>A typical winter day in Skikda follows a pattern: a cool morning, a daytime that warms up noticeably under the sun when it's out, then an evening when the temperature drops again.
