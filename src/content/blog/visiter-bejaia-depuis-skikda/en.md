<p>Visiting <strong>Béjaïa from Skikda</strong> is worth it for travellers who enjoy coastal roads and port towns set against the mountains, but it shouldn't be taken lightly: it requires a full day of driving round trip, or even an overnight stay. For a traveller based in Filfila, 700 m from the sea, the question often comes up after already exploring closer sites in Skikda province, such as Collo or Cap Bougaroun.</p>

<p>This article covers the route, what there is to see on site, the best way to split the journey via Jijel, and how to organise this excursion without sacrificing the rest of the beach stay in Filfila.</p>

<h2>Why Béjaïa appeals to travellers based in Skikda</h2>

<p>Béjaïa stands out for its position between the sea and mountainous terrain, an active old harbour, and a town centre dense with colonial and Ottoman heritage. For a traveller who has already explored the beaches of Filfila and the coves of Stora, Béjaïa offers a clear change of atmosphere: a genuine medium-sized port city, with more urban life than Skikda or Collo.</p>

<h3>A destination further away than the province's classics</h3>

<p>Unlike Collo or Cap Bougaroun, which stay within Skikda province and suit a half-day or a full day from Filfila, Béjaïa belongs to another province, further west along the coast. The distance and driving time are notably greater than a classic excursion to Jijel or Collo, which completely changes the logistics of the trip.</p>

<h3>A different view of the coast around Skikda</h3>

<p>The road leading to Béjaïa follows a varied coastline, with mountain passes and views over the gulf as the city approaches. It's an interesting contrast to the rockier, wilder coast of Collo or Cap Bougaroun, closer to Filfila.</p>

<h2>The route from Skikda: going via Jijel</h2>

<p>The most logical route to reach <strong>Béjaïa from Skikda</strong> follows the coast through Jijel. It's a longer trip than a direct excursion to Collo, and it requires factoring the driving time into the day's planning.</p>

<h3>Splitting the trip into two stages</h3>

<p>Rather than attempting a direct round trip in a single day, many travellers prefer to explore Jijel on a separate, shorter outing, then reserve a dedicated weekend for Béjaïa with an overnight stay. For the Jijel excursion alone, planning stays simpler and can be done from Filfila in one comfortable day.</p>

<h3>Planning an overnight stay rather than a round trip</h3>

<p>With a full day devoted only to driving, the time actually available to visit Béjaïa remains limited if the goal is to sleep back in Filfila that same evening. Planning an overnight stay in Béjaïa makes it possible to enjoy the town centre, the harbour and the surroundings without rushing, then hit the road again the next day.</p>

<h2>What to see and do in Béjaïa</h2>

<p>Once on site, Béjaïa concentrates several points of interest within a reasonable area, which suits a traveller who only has a day or two.</p>

<ul>
  <li>The old harbour and the waterfront, lively at the end of the day</li>
  <li>The town centre, with its shopping streets and urban heritage</li>
  <li>Viewpoints over the gulf from the higher parts of the city</li>
  <li>The overall atmosphere between sea and mountain, quite different from the Skikda coastline</li>
</ul>

<h3>Avoid trying to see everything in a single day</h3>

<p>The most common mistake is wanting to combine the drive, a full visit of the town, and the return trip in the same day. Given the round-trip driving time from Filfila, it's more realistic to focus on the town centre and the waterfront rather than packing in multiple stops, especially if an overnight stay isn't planned.</p>

<h2>Planning the excursion without sacrificing the beach stay in Filfila</h2>

<p>For a traveller staying in Filfila, the challenge is fitting the Béjaïa excursion in without cutting into beach days, the main appeal of a stay in Skikda province. The most practical solution is to schedule this outing mid-stay or toward the end, after already enjoying Larbi Ben M'hidi beach and the immediate surroundings of the residence.</p>

<h3>How long a stay to plan in order to include Béjaïa</h3>

<p>To comfortably combine several beach days in Filfila with an excursion to Béjaïa including an overnight stay, a week-long stay or longer remains the best-suited format. Beyond 7 nights, a 10% discount applies to the rental rate, which eases the overall budget for a stay that includes this more distant excursion.</p>

<h3>Renting a vehicle for the day or the weekend</h3>

<p>Renting a car remains the most flexible solution for an excursion of this scale, particularly if an overnight stay in Béjaïa is planned. It allows free stops along the coastal road, notably in Jijel, without depending on public transport schedules.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Underestimating the driving time and trying to return to Filfila the same evening with no margin</li>
  <li>Not planning an overnight stay if the goal is to really discover the town</li>
  <li>Confusing Béjaïa with a nearby excursion like Collo or Cap Bougaroun, which are shorter</li>
  <li>Setting off without checking road conditions, especially outside the fair-weather season</li>
  <li>Scheduling this excursion right at the start of the stay rather than after enjoying the beaches of Filfila</li>
</ul>
