<h2>What time to buy fresh bread</h2>

<p>Algerian bakeries generally open early in the morning, well before most tourist-facing shops, to serve warm bread from breakfast time. Stopping by very early in the morning guarantees the widest choice, especially for pastries, which often sell out quickly once the neighbourhood's regular customers have passed through.</p>
