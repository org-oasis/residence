<h2>The heating system in Résidence Oasis apartments</h2>

<p>Unlike some seasonal rentals equipped only for summer, the <strong>7 apartments at the residence</strong> all include reversible air conditioning in the bedroom and living room. The same unit handles cooling during hot weather and heating once temperatures drop, with no separate installation needed.</p>

<h3>Reversible air conditioning: how it works</h3>

<p>Reversible air conditioning works like a heat pump: in heating mode, it draws heat from the outside air and releases it indoors. The temperature rise is quick, usually just a few minutes for a standard-sized room, far faster than a traditional electric storage radiator.</p>

<h3>Room by room, not a single central system</h3>

<p>Each apartment has its own unit, adjustable independently from the rest of the residence. This means a studio for 3 can be heated to a different temperature than an F3 for 8 at the same time, according to the occupants' needs, without depending on a single boiler shared by the whole building.</p>

<h2>What an apartment actually faces in winter in Skikda</h2>

<p>Winter in Skikda, from December to February, follows a Mediterranean pattern: damper and cooler than summer, without the harsh cold found further inland, such as in Constantine. Rainfall concentrates during this period, with sometimes heavy showers interspersed with sunny days. It's precisely this damp-rather-than-freezing profile that makes reversible air conditioning sufficient: it doesn't have to fight extreme cold, only offset ambient chill and humidity.</p>

<h3>A smart move against humidity, not just the cold</h3>

<p>Reversible air conditioning does more than warm the air: most units also include a dehumidifying mode, useful during the prolonged rainy spells of a Mediterranean winter. By slightly drying the air in a room, this mode reduces the damp-cold feeling and window condensation, a comfort a simple electric storage radiator can't provide.</p>

<h3>Off the swimming season, but not off the stay</h3>

<p>The swimming season in Skikda runs from June to November. Outside that window, well-heated apartments still allow for a comfortable stay built around other activities: heritage, local cuisine, coastal hikes or a trip to Constantine, about 100 km from Filfila.</p>
