<p>Yes, a last-minute booking at Résidence Oasis is possible depending on availability among the <strong>7 apartments</strong> at the residence in Filfila. Contact is made directly via WhatsApp, a one-night deposit paid by CCP bank transfer confirms the dates, and the balance is paid on arrival in dinars or euros, with no security deposit required.</p>

<p>An unexpected change of plans or simply the urge for a spontaneous trip to the sea: the question of a late booking comes up regularly before a stay in Skikda. This article details how a last-minute booking works at the residence, which periods make finding an available unit harder, which format has the best odds of staying free, and how to maximize your chances of confirming quickly.</p>

<h2>Why booking direct helps at the last minute</h2>

<p>Booking directly, without a third-party platform, cuts out intermediaries and therefore the response time. A message sent via WhatsApp usually gets a quick answer about real availability, unlike a request sent through a booking platform, which adds an extra validation step.</p>

<h3>A single point of contact to check availability</h3>

<p>Contacting the residence's team directly avoids searching across several potentially outdated listings. A single WhatsApp exchange tells you the actual availability among the 7 apartments, from studio to F3 with terrace, for the date you need.</p>

<h2>How a late booking works at Résidence Oasis</h2>

<p>The booking process stays the same whether the request is made months in advance or the day before arrival. Only the actual availability of the units varies by period.</p>

<h3>Direct contact via WhatsApp</h3>

<p>The first message states the arrival date, length of stay and number of people. The team then indicates whether a format fits, or suggests an alternative available on the same dates. This first exchange happens through the residence's <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>, which centralizes WhatsApp, Telegram and phone.</p>

<h3>One-night deposit by CCP transfer</h3>

<p>Once availability is confirmed, a deposit equal to one night is paid by CCP bank transfer to officially secure the dates. This step stays identical to a standard booking and requires no extra documentation because of the late timing.</p>

<h3>Balance on arrival, no deposit</h3>

<p>The balance of the stay is paid directly on arrival, in dinars or euros, whichever the traveler prefers. No security deposit is required, which simplifies a last-minute arrangement with limited preparation time. The exact arrival process is detailed in <a href="/en/blog/check-in-arrivee-residence-oasis">a dedicated article on check-in</a>, and a late-night arrival specifically in <a href="/en/blog/arrivee-tardive-residence-oasis">an article on late arrivals</a>.</p>

<p>When the requested date is no longer available, several options remain worth considering before giving up on a stay in Filfila. <h2>Mistakes to avoid for a last-minute booking</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Contacting several channels in parallel for the same request, which slows down handling rather than speeding it up</li>
  <li>Not stating the exact number of people in the first message, which delays a suitable format proposal</li>
  <li>Waiting until the very last moment in the height of August without considering an alternative format</li>
  <li>Forgetting to confirm the deposit quickly once availability is announced, which can leave the date open to another traveler</li>

</ul>
