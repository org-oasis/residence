<p>Playing <strong>sports in Filfila</strong> during a vacation is entirely possible, just without an equipped gym on site. Jogging along the 7 km of Larbi Ben M'hidi beach, cycling to Stora about 20 minutes away, sea swimming from June to November and hiking in the Filfila forest cover most of what an active stay needs, with no club membership required.</p>

<p>For a traveller used to running or training regularly, the absence of a gym in a holiday residence can be a concern at first. In Filfila, a seaside district of the Skikda wilaya, most needs are met outdoors: a walkable coastline, wide beaches, nearby forest trails and water activities accessible without a license. This article details the options available season by season, the hours to favour in summer, and how to organise a stay that stays active without sacrificing rest.</p>

<h2>Jogging and running around Filfila</h2>

<p>Running remains the simplest sports activity to practise in Filfila, with no equipment or booking needed. The coastline offers natural terrain, from firm sand along the shore to the coastal road for runners who prefer asphalt.</p>

<h3>Running along Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</h3>

<p>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach (formerly Jeanne d'Arc) stretches for about 7 km, making it an ideal running ground for varying distances without retracing the same stretch. The sand is more comfortable early in the morning, before the heat and summer crowds make running less pleasant.</p>

<h3>Best hours by season</h3>

<p>In July and August, running before 9 am or after 6 pm remains the best way to avoid the strong midday heat. In spring and autumn, milder temperatures allow running at broader hours, including midday for visitors used to a regular training schedule.</p>

<h2>Cycling between Filfila and Stora</h2>

<p>Cycling is an efficient alternative to running for covering more distance while exploring the coastline. The route between Filfila and Stora, about 20 minutes by car, also suits a cycling outing for trained riders, following part of the coast. The dedicated guide on <a href="/en/blog/velo-filfila-stora">cycling between Filfila and Stora</a> covers the route, bike rental and precautions on the coastal road.</p>

<h2>Sea swimming, the most accessible activity</h2>

<p>Sea swimming remains the most accessible sports activity in Filfila, with no equipment or travel needed from accommodation close to the beach. The bathing season runs from June to November, with water reaching about 26 °C in August, allowing extended swim sessions with no sense of cold.</p>

<p>Outside this window, the sea stays accessible but colder, limiting swimming to an occasional practice rather than regular training. Swimmers used to training year-round should favour summer and early autumn to keep a steady pace.</p>

<h2>Light water sports: paddleboarding and kayaking</h2>

<p>To vary the effort beyond swimming, paddleboarding and kayaking work the arms and core while offering a different view of the coast. These activities are available as free rentals in Filfila, with no sports license required, making them accessible to a passing traveller. The guide on <a href="/en/blog/paddle-kayak-filfila">paddleboarding and kayaking in Filfila</a> covers rental points and sea conditions to favour.</p>

<p>For a broader look at the wilaya's water activities, the article on <a href="/en/blog/activites-nautiques-skikda">water activities in Skikda</a> also covers diving and organised sea outings, with the dedicated guide on <a href="/en/blog/plongee-skikda">diving in Skikda</a> for travellers wanting a more supervised practice.</p>

<h2>Hiking and active walking in the Filfila forest</h2>

<p>For less sunny days or to vary the effort away from the beach, the Filfila forest offers hiking trails accessible from the Jeanne d'Arc district. Active walking through the woods usefully complements seaside outings, with a tougher effort on the uphill sections. The guide on <a href="/en/blog/randonnee-foret-filfila">hiking in the Filfila forest</a> details the available trails and their difficulty level.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<p>A few mistakes recur among travellers wanting to stay active during their stay in Filfila. Anticipating them avoids discomfort and cut-short sessions.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Running in direct sun between 11 am and 5 pm in July-August, a window to avoid for any prolonged effort</li>
  <li>Setting off hiking in the forest without enough water, even for an outing under two hours</li>
  <li>Underestimating the wind on the cycling route to Stora, which can noticeably slow the return leg</li>
  <li>Swimming far from shore without checking the day's sea conditions, especially off-season</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last day of the stay to try paddleboarding or kayaking in case of unfavourable weather</li>
</ul>
