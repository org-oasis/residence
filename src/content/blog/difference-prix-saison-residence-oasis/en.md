<p>Yes, the nightly rate at <strong>Residence Oasis</strong> does change by season: July and August see the strongest demand of the year, while the rest of the year uses a base rate, shown from <strong>{{TIER_STUDIO_EUR}} €</strong> for the studio. An automatic <strong>10%</strong> discount is added from 7 nights booked, regardless of the chosen period.</p>

<p>For a traveler comparing several possible dates before booking, this seasonal variation directly affects the total budget of the stay. It still helps to understand which periods push the rate up, which rate the default amount shown on the site reflects, and how to combine the choice of dates with the long-stay discount to optimize the budget. This article explains the mechanism without fixing a rigid price grid, since actual rates must always be confirmed directly with the residence for each stay.</p>

<h2>Why the nightly rate varies by period</h2>

<p>As with most seasonal rentals along the Algerian coast, the nightly rate in Filfila follows the curve of tourist demand. The <strong>swimming season</strong> runs from June to November, with water reaching around 26 °C in the middle of August. This window concentrates most bookings, and the rate adjusts accordingly over the weeks.</p>

<h3>July-August: the peak of demand</h3>

<p><strong>July and August</strong> represent the busiest period of the year for the residence's 7 apartments. This is when Algerian families and the diaspora take advantage of school holidays and summer leave, pushing demand, and therefore the nightly rate, to its highest level.</p>

<h3>June and September: intermediate steps</h3>

<p>The first half of June and the second half of September form transition steps, with demand more moderate than the core of summer but still above the rest of the year. The sea remains swimmable during both periods, making them a good compromise for anyone looking to avoid the July-August crowds without giving up the beach.</p>

<h3>The base rate, valid for the rest of the year</h3>

<p>From October to May, and outside the most requested summer windows, the <strong>base rate</strong> applies. This is the rate, and only this one, reflected in the amounts shown by default on the site and throughout the blog, such as the {{TIER_F2_EUR}} € for the classic F2 or the {{TIER_F3_EUR}} € for the F3 with terrace. These figures should never be read as a summer-peak rate.</p>

<h2>How to get the best possible rate</h2>

<p>Two levers help reduce the total cost of a stay at <strong>Residence Oasis</strong>, and they can be combined or used separately depending on each traveler's calendar constraints.</p>

<h3>Choosing dates outside July-August</h3>

<p>Shifting a stay toward late spring, September, or autumn unlocks a noticeably more accessible rate while keeping pleasant weather. The sea remains swimmable until November, leaving a wide window to step outside the summer peak without giving up the beach.</p>

<h3>Booking 7 nights or more for the automatic discount</h3>

<p>Any booking of 7 consecutive nights or more benefits from an <strong>automatic 10% discount</strong>, regardless of the chosen season. There is no further step beyond 7 nights: the discount stays fixed at 10%, with no variation based on the total length of stay. Combining this discount with a period outside July-August remains the most economical formula at equal capacity.</p>

<h2>What the rate covers, regardless of season</h2>

<p>The rate level changes by period, but what it covers stays the same year-round. Water, electricity, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, an equipped kitchen, and free parking are included in all cases, with no hidden surcharge or extra service fee for a direct booking.</p>

<ul>
  <li>No deposit required, regardless of season</li>
  <li>A one-night deposit by CCP bank transfer to confirm the booking</li>
  <li>Balance paid on arrival, in dinars or euros</li>
  <li>Automatic 10% discount from 7 nights, across all seasons</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why some travelers still book during the summer peak</h2>

<p>Despite the higher rate, July and August remain the preferred months for many Algerian families and the diaspora, for reasons that go beyond price alone. School holidays concentrate the whole family's availability in this window, the summer atmosphere in Filfila is at its liveliest, and the water temperature, close to 26 °C in August, stays the most comfortable of the year. For a family stay whose dates cannot move, the seasonal surcharge becomes secondary compared with the constraint of the school or work calendar.</p>

<h2>Matching the apartment format to the chosen season</h2>

<p>The choice of format can also be adjusted to the chosen period. During the summer peak, a studio or a classic F2 is often enough for a controlled budget, since most of the time is spent outdoors between the beach and activities. Outside the peak, when days get shorter and more time is spent indoors, a more comfortable format such as the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> or the F3 with terrace makes more sense, especially since their base rate remains more accessible than in full summer.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid regarding seasonal pricing</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Reading the default rate shown on the site as a summer-peak price, when it actually reflects the base rate for the rest of the year</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last minute to book for July-August, the period when availability shrinks fastest across the 7 apartments</li>
  <li>Forgetting that the 7-night discount also applies during the summer peak, and therefore not asking for it when requesting a quote</li>
  <li>Comparing a base rate shown elsewhere with another property's summer rate, which skews any comparison</li>
  <li>Not anticipating that a premium format such as the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> follows the same seasonal logic as the other formats</li>
</ul>
