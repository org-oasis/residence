<h3>Weddings, family reunions or seminars</h3>

<p>Three situations come up most often for a multi-apartment booking: a wedding bringing together close family and guests from out of town, an extended family reunion (several generations, several households), or a company seminar or team retreat. <p>Unlike a single large apartment where the whole group shares the same spaces, several separate apartments preserve each household's privacy: every couple, nuclear family, or group of friends has its own space to sleep and rest, while still being able to meet up for meals or beach outings.</p>
