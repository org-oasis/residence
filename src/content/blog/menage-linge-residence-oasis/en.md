<p>Yes, <strong>housekeeping at Oasis Residence</strong> is handled before every arrival and after every departure, with linen renewed mid-stay from <strong>7 nights</strong> of booking. During the stay, day-to-day upkeep (dishes, trash) stays with the guest, as with any independent furnished rental, well equipped with a washing machine for free use in every apartment.</p>

<p>This question comes up often before booking a furnished apartment rather than a hotel room: who handles housekeeping, how often is linen changed, and what is left for the guest to manage during a week-long stay or longer in Filfila? The answer concretely shapes how a family or group of friends organises a stay, especially for a multi-week rental. This article details what is included on arrival, how often linen is actually renewed, the line between what the residence handles and what stays with the group, and best practices for enjoying a consistently comfortable apartment with no surprises.</p>

<h2>What is provided on arrival in every apartment</h2>

<p>Before every arrival, the apartment gets a <strong>full clean</strong>: floors, surfaces, bathroom, kitchen and bedding. A full set of sheets and bath towels is set up ready to use, at no extra charge. This baseline is the same across the residence's seven units, from the studio to the F3 with terrace.</p>

<h3>The special case of the F2 with jacuzzi and sauna</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/appartement-jacuzzi-skikda">F2 with private jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna</a> includes a dedicated wellness towel set, in addition to the standard bath towels. This distinction avoids using the same towels for the jacuzzi and for daily washing, a practical detail for a multi-day stay.</p>

<h3>What the advertised rate covers</h3>

<p>Bed linen, towels, and cleaning before and after the stay are included in the nightly rate shown for each unit, with no extra line to settle on site. The <a href="/en/blog/equipements-appartements-skikda">amenities common to all apartments</a> (Wi-Fi, air conditioning, fitted kitchen, washing machine) follow the same logic: everything is included, with no deposit required at checkout.</p>

<h2>How often linen is renewed during the stay</h2>

<p>For bookings of <strong>7 nights or more</strong>, bed linen and towels are renewed once, mid-stay. For shorter stays, a single full set covers the whole booking, matching the standard practice of an independent furnished rental rather than daily hotel-style service.</p>

<h3>Needing extra linen during the stay</h3>

<p>A young child, a stay that ends up extending, or simply a one-off need for clean towels: a message to the team on WhatsApp is enough to arrange an extra visit. Response times match any other request during the stay, usually handled the same day.</p>

<h2>What stays with the guest during the stay</h2>

<p>An independent furnished rental does not work like a hotel with a daily housekeeping round. <strong>Day-to-day upkeep</strong> during the stay naturally stays with the occupants, which is also the trade-off for real privacy and fully flexible hours, with no staff entering the apartment mid-stay.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Doing the dishes after meals, with a sink and personal supplies to bring along</li>
  <li>Taking out the trash regularly, especially in the summer heat</li>
  <li>Tidying and airing rooms daily</li>
  <li>Washing personal laundry: every apartment has a <a href="/en/blog/lave-linge-appartement-skikda">washing machine for free use</a>, included in the rate, with no token or separate charge</li>
  <li>Reporting any technical issue right away (see the distinction with real emergencies) rather than waiting until checkout</li>
</ul>

<h3>What falls to the residence instead</h3>

<p>Anything related to the overall condition of the apartment on arrival (cleanliness, working equipment, bedding) falls to the residence. An equipment fault or a cleanliness issue found on arrival should be reported immediately: that is a different matter from the day-to-day upkeep that stays with the guest during their occupancy.</p>

<h2>Housekeeping and linen by unit size</h2>

<p>The principle does not change by unit, but the practical daily load scales with the number of guests. A studio for one or a couple needs minimal upkeep; an <a href="/en/blog/f3-terrasse-privative-skikda">F3 with private terrace</a> rented by a large group or several families naturally means more dishes, personal laundry and daily tidying, without the residence's linen renewal frequency changing.</p>

<h2>Best practices for a trouble-free stay</h2>

<p>A few simple habits avoid housekeeping and linen hassles, especially for an extended stay in Filfila during the bathing season that runs from June to November, when the apartment sees more use (sand, beach towels, more laundry to wash).</p>

<ul>
  <li>Report any cleanliness issue found on arrival right away, before unpacking</li>
  <li>Use the washing machine from the first few days to avoid a laundry backlog by the end of the stay</li>
  <li>Bring your own beach towels, separate from the bath towels provided, to limit their wear</li>
  <li>Let the team know ahead of time about extending the stay, to adjust linen renewal if needed</li>
  <li>Keep the trash bins closed and taken out regularly, useful year-round but especially in peak summer</li>
</ul>

<h3>Common mistake: waiting until checkout to report an issue</h3>

<p>Waiting until the end of the stay to report a missing sheet, a damaged towel or a cleanliness concern needlessly complicates the fix. A quick message on WhatsApp allows for a fix during the stay rather than a discussion after the fact, once the apartment has been vacated.</p>

<h2>How to raise a housekeeping or linen request</h2>

<p>Any request related to housekeeping or linen goes through the same channels as the booking itself: <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a>. The team confirms the need and arranges a visit if necessary, with no hidden fee added to the rate agreed at booking.</p>
