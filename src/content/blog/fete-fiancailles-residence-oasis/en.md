<p>Hosting an <strong>engagement party (khotba)</strong> at Residence Oasis requires no special arrangement: the F3 with private terrace, which holds up to 8 guests at base capacity, combines a spacious living area for the ceremony and traditional meal with an outdoor terrace for the reception and photos. Booking follows the same process as a regular stay, through WhatsApp and a one-night deposit.</p>

<p>The khotba, the central moment of Algerian engagements where both families gather for the exchange of gifts and the couple's blessing, needs a setting that is both intimate and spacious enough to properly host relatives who sometimes travel from afar. In Filfila, 700 m from the sea, Residence Oasis offers an alternative to a classic rented hall: a private setting, a nearby shoreline for couple photos, and several apartment formats depending on the size of the gathered family. This article covers which unit to choose, how to organize the reception on site, the budget to expect, and the practical points to plan for so the day runs smoothly.</p>

<h2>Why choose an apartment over a rented hall</h2>

<p>The khotba remains first and foremost a family ceremony, often more intimate than a wedding, which makes a spacious apartment relevant as an alternative to a hall rented for the occasion. Many families prefer this format for the scheduling flexibility and private setting it offers.</p>

<h3>A private setting for a family ceremony</h3>

<p>Unlike a hall shared with other events the same day, an apartment booked for the khotba stays for the family's exclusive use throughout the stay. There's no imposed departure time or shared spaces with another group, which leaves the time needed for each step of the ceremony: welcoming both families, the gift exchange, the meal, then the informal moments that often carry on into the evening.</p>

<h3>The nearby sea for the couple's photos</h3>

<p>Filfila sits 700 m from the sea, which makes it easy to fit a beachside photo session into the day's program, before or after the ceremony itself. That's an advantage a classic downtown reception hall doesn't offer, being generally far from the shoreline.</p>

<h2>Which apartment to choose for a khotba</h2>

<p>The choice of unit depends on the number of expected guests and the layout wanted for the day: an indoor-focused reception, or a combined indoor-terrace use.</p>

<h3>The F3 with terrace, the reference format</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> remains the best-suited choice for hosting the extended family around a khotba. Its spacious living room covers the ceremony and meal indoors, while the private terrace lets the reception extend outdoors, for photos or a moment of tea and pastries after the main meal. With a base capacity of 8, it suits a close family core extended to the nearest relatives.</p>

<h3>The F2 with jacuzzi for a more intimate moment</h3>

<p>For the engaged couple who wish to extend the stay after the khotba in a more intimate setting, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> offers a complementary option, alongside the main unit booked for the family ceremony. It's a common combination: the F3 for the reception day, another format for the couple in the following days.</p>

<h3>Combining several apartments for a large family</h3>

<p>When the number of guests exceeds a single unit's capacity, especially if relatives travel from another city or abroad for the occasion, booking several of the residence's seven apartments in parallel houses everyone on the same site. Each family keeps its own space for the night, while staying a few meters from each other for meals and the ceremony itself, without relying on a hotel away from the reception venue.</p>

<h2>Organizing the khotba day</h2>

<p>Once the unit or units are chosen, several practical points are worth planning ahead so the day runs smoothly, especially if it gathers a large number of guests in a single day.</p>

<h3>Decoration and preparing the terrace</h3>

<p>The F3's private terrace lends itself well to simple decoration for the occasion: a tablecloth, flowers, trays of traditional pastries laid out for the gift exchange. It's recommended to plan the decoration in advance, as the residence provides the space but no dedicated event decoration service.</p>

<h3>Meal, pastries and catering</h3>

<p>The fully equipped kitchen (stove, oven, fridge, coffee maker) lets you prepare some traditional dishes and pastries directly on site, notably the day before or the morning of the ceremony. For the main meal serving a large number of guests, hiring an outside caterer remains a common and entirely workable solution: nothing prevents a delivery straight to the apartment, with the living room and terrace serving as the reception space.</p>

<h3>Photos and key moments of the day</h3>

<p>The nearby Larbi Ben M'hidi beach makes it easy to fit a couple's beachside photo session into the program, alongside the photos taken on the terrace during the family reception. Planning this moment early or late in the day, depending on the light wanted, avoids disrupting the rest of the ceremony's schedule.</p>

<h2>Budget and booking</h2>

<p>The budget for a khotba organized in an apartment stays notably more flexible than that of a classic reception hall, since it's essentially limited to the rate of the night or nights booked, with no separate hall rental fee.</p>
