<p>Yes, a ground-floor apartment exists in Skikda: the <strong>Studio Deluxe</strong> at Résidence Oasis, in Filfila, sits on the ground floor of the residence, 700 m from the sea. The F2 Classic and the F2 with a private jacuzzi, on the first floor, also keep the number of steps lower than the units on the upper floors.</p>

<p>Travelers with heavy luggage, families with a stroller, or people who simply prefer to avoid stairs on vacation: the question of access level comes up regularly before booking. This article details which units at the residence qualify, what "ground floor" actually means on site, and how to check that the layout matches your needs before booking.</p>

<h2>Why look for a step-free apartment in Skikda</h2>

<p>Arriving after several hours on the road from Constantine or Annaba, or after a longer trip from further afield, with full suitcases, changes how a flight of stairs feels. For a family with a stroller, a senior traveler, or simply someone who prefers direct access, a ground-floor unit noticeably simplifies arrival and the daily trips to the car or the beach.</p>

<h3>A criterion often overlooked when booking</h3>

<p>Most listings highlight the view, the size, or the amenities, rarely the access level. Yet for a stay of a week or more, climbing several flights of stairs multiple times a day with beach bags, groceries, or luggage has a real effect on the comfort of the stay.</p>

<h2>The Studio Deluxe, the only truly ground-floor unit</h2>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/studio-deluxe-1">ground-floor deluxe studio</a> at Résidence Oasis is the only apartment at the residence sitting directly at street level, with no floor to climb. It's the format to prioritize for anyone looking for step-free access.</p>

<h3>Layout and amenities of the studio</h3>

<p>Across 23 m², the studio combines a sleeping area, an equipped kitchen, and a bathroom, with the amenities shared by every unit at the residence: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, TV, refrigerator, oven, hob, freezer and washing machine. It is capped at 3 guests, which makes it a suitable format for a solo traveler, a couple, or a parent with one child.</p>

<h2>First-floor F2 units, an alternative for a couple or small family</h2>

<p>For a group larger than 3 people who still wants to limit the number of floors, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-classique-2">first-floor classic F2</a> and the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> are both on the first floor of the building, cutting the distance compared with the F2 and deluxe F2 units on the second floor, or the F3 with a private terrace, on the third floor.</p>

<h3>Capacity of the first-floor F2 units</h3>

<p>The classic F2 and the F2 with private jacuzzi each host up to 4 guests at base capacity, with the option to add up to 2 extra guests for a surcharge. The F2 with jacuzzi adds a private jacuzzi and a Norwegian sauna, for a couple wanting to combine easy access with a relaxing touch.</p>

<h2>What "ground floor" actually means at the residence</h2>

<p>The Studio Deluxe sits directly at street level, with no stairs to reach it. The classic F2 and the F2 with jacuzzi, on the first floor, do involve climbing one flight of stairs from the building's entrance. Since accessibility needs vary a great deal from person to person (door width, bathroom layout, whether a ramp is present), it's recommended to describe your exact needs via <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a> before booking, rather than relying solely on the word "ground floor."</p>

<h2>Who benefits most from a step-free apartment</h2>

<p>The need for step-free access isn't limited to travelers with reduced mobility. Several traveler profiles genuinely benefit from avoiding stairs during their stay in Filfila.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Families with a stroller</strong>: no need to carry a folded stroller up and down on every trip to the beach or the car</li>
  <li><strong>Senior travelers</strong>: fewer repeated efforts, especially with luggage or grocery bags</li>
  <li><strong>People recovering</strong> from a recent surgery or injury</li>
  <li><strong>Groups arriving with a loaded car</strong>: coolers, parasols and beach gear move more easily on the ground floor</li>
  <li><strong>Long stays</strong>: over several weeks, the daily effort saved matters more than on a short weekend</li>
</ul>

<h2>Checklist before booking an accessible apartment</h2>

<p>A few simple questions asked before confirming a booking help avoid any surprise on arrival, especially for a specific accessibility need.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Is the unit directly at street level, or does it require climbing one or more floors from the building's entrance?</li>
  <li>Does the door and bathroom width suit a wheelchair or a walker, if needed?</li>
  <li>Is the parking right next to the unit's entrance?</li>
  <li>Does the planned number of guests match the format's base capacity, or does it require a surcharge for extra guests?</li>
  <li>Has the specific need been communicated directly to the team before paying the booking deposit?</li>
</ul>

<p>Describing your exact needs via <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a> before booking remains the most reliable way to confirm a format fits a specific situation, rather than relying solely on the word "ground floor" in a listing.</p>
