<p>The <strong>sunset in Skikda</strong> is best enjoyed along the Filfila seafront and its Larbi Ben M'hidi beach, nearly 7 km long, but also from the surrounding hills and, for those after wide open spaces, Cap Bougaroun further west. None of these spots require a long trip from a stay in the Jeanne d'Arc district: a simple evening walk is usually enough.</p>

<p>For travellers planning a stay in Filfila, the sunset question often comes up at the end of the daily programme: after a day at the beach or on an excursion, where should you settle in to enjoy the evening sky without organising an extra outing? This article covers the best viewpoints around Skikda, the most favourable season, options from an apartment with a terrace, and a few practical tips so you don't miss the moment.</p>

<h2>The best sunset viewpoints in Filfila</h2>

<p>Filfila brings together several interesting angles on the sea without needing to travel far from the residential district. The coastline's layout, combining a long beach and nearby hills, multiplies the options depending on the mood: a seaside walk, a high balcony, or a rocky headland.</p>

<h3>The Larbi Ben M'hidi beach seafront</h3>

<p><strong>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</strong> (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), stretching nearly 7 km, remains the simplest option. In the late afternoon, foot traffic drops noticeably compared with the midday peak, making a walk on the sand or along the seafront more pleasant. The beach's width makes it easy to find a clear spot with an unobstructed view of the sea horizon.</p>

<h3>The hills and heights around Filfila</h3>

<p>Above the district, the hills that border Filfila offer an elevated view over the bay. This kind of viewpoint suits a short evening outing, on foot or by car depending on the distance from the apartment, to watch the sky change colour over the sea and the typical coastal vegetation of the Filfila forest.</p>

<h3>Stora, between the harbour and the rocky coastline</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/blog/plage-stora">Stora fishing harbour</a> offers a different late-afternoon atmosphere: boats coming back in, lively quays, and a view over the rocky coastline stretching beyond the harbour. This is best combined with a trip already planned to Stora rather than a dedicated outing purely for the sunset.</p>

<h2>Cap Bougaroun, for a wilder panorama</h2>

<p>Further west of Filfila, <a href="/en/blog/cap-bougaroun-depuis-filfila">Cap Bougaroun</a> is the furthest point of the wilaya's rocky coastline. The site draws visitors looking for a quieter panorama, with cliffs dropping straight into the sea. It requires more planning than a simple beach walk, but remains reachable within half a day from Filfila.</p>

<h3>Planning a trip to Cap Bougaroun</h3>

<p>To enjoy the site before nightfall without rushing the return trip, it's best to leave in the mid-afternoon. The coastal road leading to the cape crosses landscapes worth the detour in their own right, making the drive there just as enjoyable as the destination.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Plan to leave in the mid-afternoon to arrive with a margin before sunset</li>
  <li>Bring water and light clothing in summer, warmer layers outside the bathing season</li>
  <li>Check the weather and road conditions before setting off, especially off-season</li>
  <li>Plan the night-time return trip with good headlights, as the coastal road is not always lit</li>
</ul>

<h2>Enjoying the sunset from your apartment in Filfila</h2>

<p>On evenings without a planned outing, an apartment with a well-oriented outdoor space allows you to enjoy the evening sky without leaving the residence. This is worth considering when choosing an apartment, especially for a longer stay where several evenings will simply be spent on site.</p>

<h3>The F3 with private terrace</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> has the largest outdoor space in the residence, suited to an evening dinner with a view of the surroundings. The terrace allows the evening sky to be enjoyed without any timing constraint, unlike an outing that depends on the weather or traffic.</p>

<h3>Balconies and upper floors</h3>

<p>Apartments on the upper floors generally enjoy a more open view over the rooftops and surrounding greenery than ground-floor units. This is not always enough for a direct sea view, but it remains pleasant for watching the sky change colour in the evening, drink in hand on the balcony.</p>

<h2>When to go for a good sunset</h2>

<p>The <strong>bathing season</strong>, running from June to November, offers the most favourable conditions: generally clear skies, mild evenings, and the option to extend the outing with one last swim. In August, the water stays around 26 °C, allowing the sunset to be enjoyed with your feet in the water without feeling cold.</p>

<h2>Dining while watching the sunset</h2>

<p>Several spots around Stora and central Skikda offer a sea-facing terrace for an evening dinner. During peak season, these tables fill up quickly in the late afternoon: booking a few hours ahead, or the same morning for that evening, remains the most reliable way to secure a seat with an open view.</p>

<h3>Combining beach time and dinner</h3>

<p>A simple approach is to enjoy the beach until late afternoon, go back to freshen up, then head out again for an evening dinner with a sea view. This avoids the busiest waiting time and leaves room to choose a table with a good angle on the horizon.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<p>A few mistakes come up repeatedly among visitors unfamiliar with the Skikda coastline. Anticipating them avoids an evening spoiled by poor timing or a poor choice of spot.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Arriving too late at an elevated viewpoint and missing the last minutes of light</li>
  <li>Choosing a beach spot blocked by buildings or terrain without checking the viewing angle beforehand</li>
  <li>Setting off for Cap Bougaroun without enough time margin for the return trip</li>
  <li>Not booking a sea-view terrace table on a peak-season evening and ending up without a spot</li>
  <li>Underestimating the evening chill outside the bathing season and going out without warm clothing</li>
</ul>
