<p>Celebrating a <strong>wedding anniversary at Oasis Residence</strong> is possible both as a couple and with the extended family. The F2 with private jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna suits an intimate getaway for two, while the F3 with private terrace, which accommodates up to 8 people at base capacity, allows gathering children and relatives to mark the occasion together.</p>

<p>Between couples who want to get away from daily life and families who prefer celebrating the event all together, the needs differ noticeably. In Filfila, 700 m from the sea, Oasis Residence offers several formats allowing the stay to be adapted to the chosen setup, without going through a reception hall or a classic hotel. This article details which apartment to favour depending on the couple's or family's profile, how to organise the meal for the occasion, the budget to anticipate and the practical steps to book.</p>

<h2>Why celebrate a wedding anniversary in an apartment rather than at a hotel</h2>

<p>A wedding anniversary works well in a private setting rather than a standard hotel room. The apartment remains for the exclusive use of the couple or family throughout the stay, with a fitted kitchen to prepare a tailored meal and, depending on the format, an outdoor space to extend the evening.</p>

<h3>An intimate setting for a couple</h3>

<p>For a couple who simply wants to mark the date without organising a reception, an apartment offers more privacy than a hotel room: no shared dining room, no imposed schedule, and the possibility of enjoying private wellness equipment rather than one shared with other guests.</p>

<h3>A shared space to celebrate as a family</h3>

<p>For a family who prefers celebrating the anniversary all together - children, parents, sometimes grandparents - a more spacious format with a terrace allows organising a convivial meal without booking a dedicated hall or depending on a restaurant's schedule.</p>

<h2>Which apartment to choose depending on the desired format</h2>

<p>The choice of format depends above all on the number of people and the type of celebration sought: a romantic getaway for two or a family gathering around the event.</p>

<h3>The F2 with jacuzzi and sauna for a couple</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> combines a two-person jacuzzi and a Norwegian sauna, both integrated into the apartment's private space. For a couple who wants to mark the anniversary without leaving the comfort of the residence, this is the most suitable format: air conditioning, fitted kitchen and a private hot bath included in the nightly rate, with no surcharge for jacuzzi or sauna electricity use.</p>

<h3>The F3 with terrace for a family celebration</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> accommodates up to 8 people at base capacity, with up to {{EXTRA_PERSON_MAX}} extra guests possible for a surcharge per night and per person. The terrace works well for an outdoor meal to mark the occasion as a family, and the space remains spacious enough to welcome several generations without feeling cramped.</p>

<h3>A classic F2 for a simpler format</h3>

<p>For a couple who prefers a simpler setup, without wellness equipment, a classic F2 covers the essential needs at a more accessible rate: fitted kitchen, air conditioning and standard comfort, sufficient for a short getaway centred on time spent together rather than equipment.</p>

<h2>Organising the meal and the atmosphere for the occasion</h2>

<p>The residence provides the space and equipment, but does not offer an integrated decoration or catering service: these elements remain to be arranged in advance if the couple or family wants to mark the occasion further.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Prepare a meal on-site with the fitted kitchen (hob, oven, fridge, coffee maker)</li>
  <li>Have a meal delivered from a local restaurant, notably fresh fish from Stora</li>
  <li>Plan simple decoration brought along for the F3 terrace or the F2 living room</li>
  <li>Combine the celebration with a daytime outing (beach, excursion) before the evening meal</li>

<h2>When to book for a wedding anniversary</h2>

<p>If the anniversary date falls during the high-demand period (June to September), booking several weeks ahead is advisable to guarantee availability of the desired format, particularly the F2 with jacuzzi whose supply remains limited. Outside these windows, the base rate applies and availability is generally more flexible, even for a booking closer to the date.</p>

<h2>Booking a wedding anniversary stay</h2>

<p>Booking is done directly by WhatsApp, specifying the exact date and the desired format among the residence's <a href="/en/apartments">available studios and F2/F3 units</a>. A one-night deposit by CCP transfer confirms the booking, with the balance settled on arrival in dinars or euros, with no security deposit required. To view the equipment before booking, the <a href="/en/gallery">residence photo gallery</a> presents each format in detail.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>

<p>Celebrating a <strong>wedding anniversary at Oasis Residence</strong> adapts well to both a couple and an extended family: the F2 with jacuzzi and sauna for an intimate getaway, the F3 with private terrace for a celebration gathering several generations. The meal and decoration remain to be organised independently, but the space, the equipment and the proximity to the sea offer a suitable setting to mark the occasion without going through a classic reception hall. For any question about availability or the most suitable format, <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a> allow getting a quick answer.</p>
