<p>A <strong>rainy day in Skikda</strong> doesn't derail a stay built around the beach: the regional museum, historic mosques, covered souks and downtown restaurants provide plenty to fill a full day. Skikda sits 20 km from Filfila, about 25 to 30 minutes by road, making it an easy escape to improvise as soon as the sky clouds over.</p>

<p>Rain stays rare during the swimming season, from June to November, but it's never entirely ruled out, particularly at the start or end of the season. For a stay of several days planned at Résidence Oasis, it helps to have a few reliable options in mind rather than losing a whole day. This article details the indoor and semi-covered activities accessible from Filfila, and the best way to string them together into one outing.</p>

<h2>When is rain likely in Skikda</h2>

<p>Skikda province has a Mediterranean climate: dry, hot summers and wetter autumns and winters. Rainfall is heavily concentrated outside the <strong>swimming season</strong>, which runs from June to November, with the sea reaching about 26°C at the height of summer. A rainy day in the middle of the season is therefore the exception rather than the rule, but it can occur in late spring or in the first weeks of autumn.</p>

<h3>Generally short showers</h3>

<p>When it does rain in Skikda, it most often takes the form of relatively short showers rather than full days under a low sky. It's still worth having a beach alternative ready rather than relying solely on the morning forecast.</p>
