<p>The <strong>best restaurants in Skikda</strong> fall into three distinct areas: Stora harbour for fresh fish, downtown for traditional Algerian cuisine, and the area around Filfila for its immediate closeness to seaside accommodation. Within 20 minutes of each other by road, these three hubs cover most of what a stay calls for, from a quick post-beach meal to a proper evening table.</p>

<p>For a traveller preparing a stay in Filfila, the question comes up quickly: where to eat, at what budget, and should downtown, the harbour, or nearby addresses take priority? This guide details the three areas worth knowing, the traditional dishes to try, budget benchmarks, and the best way to combine restaurant outings with cooking in the apartment during the stay.</p>

<h2>The three areas to eat well in Skikda</h2>

<p>Unlike a large city that concentrates its culinary offer in a single district, the Skikda region spreads its best addresses across three different logics: the working harbour at Stora, the urban buzz downtown, and the practicality of proximity in Filfila.</p>

<h3>Stora, the reference for fresh fish</h3>

<p>The <strong>harbour of Stora</strong> holds the best fresh-fish addresses in the whole region. Its restaurants sit a few metres from the docks, supplied directly by fishing boats returning in late morning and early afternoon. Our article on <a href="/en/blog/plage-stora">Stora beach</a> details the harbour's overall atmosphere, a good complement to a beach stay in Filfila.</p>

<h3>Downtown, for traditional cuisine and the widest choice</h3>

<p>Downtown Skikda, about 20 km from Filfila, gathers the region's highest density of restaurants, with a wide range: traditional Algerian cuisine, grills, pastries, and more formal establishments suited to a family or friends' dinner. It's the best area to vary things across several evenings.</p>

<h3>Filfila, for proximity and practicality</h3>

<p>Around the Jeanne d'Arc district in Filfila, the offer stays more modest but practical: ideal for a quick meal between two swims, with no long trip during the height of the swimming season. This proximity usefully complements the more elaborate outings to Stora or downtown, without requiring a trip for every meal.</p>

<h3>Fresh fish, the must-try specialty</h3>

<p>Fresh fish remains the specialty most associated with the Skikda region, driven by the ongoing activity of Stora harbour. Grilled sardines, rockfish and seafood depending on the day's catch make up most of the menus in the harbour area, with a freshness hard to match elsewhere on the coast.</p>

<h3>Traditional Algerian cuisine day to day</h3>

<p>Beyond fish, chorba, couscous and grilled meats form the backbone of local cuisine, available at most downtown establishments. These dishes suit a family lunch or a dinner after a day of excursions well.</p>
