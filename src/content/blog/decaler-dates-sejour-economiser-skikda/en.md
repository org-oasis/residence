<p>Shifting your dates by <strong>just a few days</strong> can change the rate of a stay at Residence Oasis when the planned stay sits near one of the five boundaries in the yearly pricing grid. The grid distinguishes the base rate, valid from October to May, from four pricier windows covering June, July-August and September. Moving an arrival or departure forward or back by two or three days is sometimes enough to shift all or part of the stay into a cheaper period.</p>

<p>For a traveler preparing a stay in Filfila with some flexibility on dates, for instance a week of flexible leave in late August or early September, the question is worth asking before booking: is it better to arrive two days earlier, or two days later? This article explains how the residence's pricing grid actually works, in which cases shifting dates has a real effect on price, and how to combine that logic with the discount granted for longer stays.</p>

<h2>How Residence Oasis's seasonal pricing grid works</h2>

<p>Unlike a single rate valid all year, Residence Oasis applies a grid that follows seasonal demand, stronger in summer and at the edges of the swimming season, calmer the rest of the year.</p>

<h3>The base rate, valid the rest of the year</h3>

<p>From October to May, the base rate applies, the most affordable in the grid, starting from <strong>{{TIER_STUDIO_EUR}} € / {{TIER_STUDIO_DZD}} DA</strong> for a studio and <strong>{{TIER_F2_EUR}} € / {{TIER_F2_DZD}} DA</strong> for a classic F2. This is the period that offers the most room for a traveler with flexible dates.</p>

<h2>Shifting your dates by a few days: the real impact</h2>

<p>The effect of a date shift depends entirely on where the planned stay sits relative to the boundaries of the periods listed above. Two very different cases come up in practice.</p>

<h3>A stay already well inside a period</h3>

<p>For a stay planned, say, from July 10 to 17, right in the middle of the July-August period, shifting the dates by two or three days changes nothing: the whole stay stays within the most expensive period of the year, regardless of the direction of the shift.</p>

<h3>A stay close to a period boundary</h3>

<p>The situation changes considerably when the planned dates fall within a day or two of a boundary between two periods. A stay starting on August 29 or 30, just before the shift into the September 1-15 window, would have its first nights billed at the July-August rate and the following nights at the lower early-September rate. Shifting the arrival by a few days, to start the stay fully in September, can in that case lower the average rate for the whole stay.</p>

<h3>And if my stay overlaps two periods?</h3>

<p>In that case, the rate applied depends on the period in effect on each precise date of the stay: each night is tied to the pricing window covering it on that exact date. A stay running, for example, from August 28 to September 6 combines part at the July-August rate and part at the September 1-15 rate. It's recommended to send the exact dates considered via WhatsApp to get the precise breakdown before confirming the booking.</p>

<h2>Combining a date shift with the long-stay discount</h2>

<p>Choosing precise dates isn't the only lever available to optimize the budget of a stay at Residence Oasis; it combines usefully with the discount granted for longer stays.</p>

<h3>The 10% discount from 7 nights</h3>

<p>A 10% discount applies automatically from 7 consecutive nights onward, regardless of which pricing period or periods the stay falls under. For a traveler whose dates already overlap two periods, this discount remains available on the whole stay once the 7-night threshold is reached, adding to the potential savings gained by adjusting the dates by a few days.</p>
