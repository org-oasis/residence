<p>Planning a budget for two weeks requires a different approach than a simple weekend: accommodation weighs more over time, but eating habits and the choice of local transport also strongly affect the final bill. This article details, item by item, what to anticipate for a two-week stay in Skikda, how the 10% discount works alongside seasonality, and concrete levers to optimise the budget without sacrificing comfort.</p>

<h2>Why a two-week stay changes the budget logic</h2>

<p>Over a short stay, accommodation and fixed costs largely dominate the budget. Over two weeks, the balance shifts: daily meals, repeated trips and local activities carry more cumulative weight, even though the nightly rate for the apartment stays the same from the first to the last day.</p>

<h3>The 10% discount from 7 nights applies to the whole stay</h3>

<p>The principle is simple: from 7 consecutive nights booked, a 10% discount applies to the total amount, with no additional tier at 10 or 14 nights. For a two-week stay, this means the discount benefits all 14 nights, not only from the eighth night onward. The calculation is detailed in <a href="/en/blog/remise-10-pourcent-7-nuits">the 10% discount from 7 nights</a>, useful for comparing precisely with a shorter booking.</p>

<h3>Accommodation remains the most predictable item</h3>

<p>Unlike meals or transport, whose cost depends on choices made on-site, the accommodation price is fixed at booking time and does not change afterward. It is therefore the item to secure first when building a reliable budget for two weeks, before even estimating the rest of the spending.</p>

<h2>Other budget items to anticipate</h2>

<p>Beyond accommodation, three items remain to anticipate for a long stay: food, local transport and activities. Their weight varies greatly depending on each traveller's habits, unlike the apartment price which stays fixed.</p>

<h3>Food: fitted kitchen or restaurants</h3>

<p>Every apartment has a fitted kitchen (hob, oven, fridge), allowing part of the meals to be prepared on-site rather than eating out every time. Over two weeks, alternating home cooking and restaurant outings - notably fresh fish in Stora or downtown - remains the most balanced approach between budget and local discovery.</p>

<h3>Local transport over two weeks</h3>

<p>For a short stay, taxis or occasional trips are enough. Over two weeks, a car rental often becomes more relevant, especially to combine several outings (Stora, Collo, Constantine, Cap Bougaroun) without depending on taxi availability for every trip. The residence's car park is free and secure, avoiding any extra cost throughout the stay.</p>

<h3>Activities and excursions</h3>

<p>The bathing season runs from June to November, with sea temperature reaching around 26 °C in August. Over two weeks, alternating beach days, excursions (Stora, Collo, Constantine) and relaxed time at the apartment helps spread the activities budget over the whole duration rather than concentrating it on the first few days.</p>
