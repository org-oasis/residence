<p>No: Résidence Oasis does not charge for an included breakfast plan. The <strong>7 apartments</strong> in Filfila each have an equipped kitchen (cooktop, oven, refrigerator, coffee maker) that lets guests prepare their own breakfast, lunch and dinner, following the seasonal-rental model rather than a hotel with half-board.</p>

<p>For a traveller used to hotels, the absence of a served breakfast can seem like a gap. In reality, it's a normal feature of the equipped furnished rental, which offers more autonomy, free timing and a generally better meal budget for a family or group in exchange. This article details what each apartment's kitchen actually allows, where to shop near Filfila, and how this setup compares to a hotel stay.</p>

<h2>The "equipped rental" model rather than a hotel with half-board</h2>

<p>Résidence Oasis positions itself as a set of <strong>7 furnished, equipped apartments</strong>, not as a hotel establishment with a catering service. This distinction matters before booking: the nightly rate covers the apartment and running costs (water, electricity, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, parking, upkeep), but no meal is planned or billed separately, not breakfast and not dinner.</p>

<h3>Autonomy rather than an imposed plan</h3>

<p>This model particularly suits families, groups of friends and multi-day stays, where the freedom to eat whenever you want, to cook according to your own dietary habits (halal, special diet, allergy) or simply to save on meals matters more than the comfort of a buffet served at a fixed time. A breakfast enjoyed quietly before heading to the beach, with no restaurant-hall schedule, remains one of the advantages most mentioned by travellers who choose this format.</p>
