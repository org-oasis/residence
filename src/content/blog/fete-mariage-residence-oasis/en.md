<p>Organizing a <strong>wedding reception at Résidence Oasis</strong> is possible with no special paperwork: the F3 with private terrace, which sleeps up to 8 guests at base capacity, combines a spacious living area for the meal and ceremony with an outdoor terrace for the evening and photos. Seven independent apartments also allow the extended family to stay on the same site.</p>

<p>An Algerian wedding traditionally spans several days and often brings together relatives who have traveled far, which complicates lodging logistics around the ceremony itself. In Filfila, 700 m from the sea, Résidence Oasis offers a practical alternative to classic event halls: a private setting for the reception, the option to house several families on the same site, and direct access to the coastline for the couple's photos. This article covers which apartment to choose depending on the wedding's size, how to organize the meal and decoration, the budget to plan for, and the practical points to keep the day running smoothly.</p>

<h2>Why an apartment rather than an event hall</h2>

<p>A wedding reception usually spans several days: welcoming the families, the ceremony, the meal, then informal moments that often stretch late into the evening. An apartment booked for the occasion stays for the family's exclusive use throughout the stay, with no imposed departure time and no space shared with another event.</p>

<h3>A private setting for several days of celebration</h3>

<p>Unlike an event hall rented by the hour or the day, an apartment in Filfila remains available from check-in to check-out, leaving enough time for every stage of the wedding — preparations, ceremony, meal and evening — without an external schedule to manage on top of the family's own organization.</p>

<h3>The sea nearby for the couple's photos</h3>

<p>Filfila sits 700 m from the sea, making it easy to fit a beachside photo session into the wedding program, before or after the ceremony. The newlyweds get a natural backdrop that is hard to find from a classic reception hall in a city center, typically further from the coast.</p>

<h2>Which apartment to choose depending on the wedding size</h2>

<p>The choice of apartment depends on the number of guests expected and the setup wanted for the reception day.</p>

<h3>The F3 with terrace, the main format</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> remains the best-suited choice for the main reception. Its spacious living area hosts the ceremony and the meal indoors, while the private terrace extends the celebration outdoors for the evening and photos. With a base capacity of 8, it suits a close family core extended to the nearest relatives.</p>

<h3>Combining several apartments for the extended family</h3>

<p>When the number of guests exceeds a single unit's capacity, especially if relatives are coming from another city or abroad for the occasion, booking several of the residence's seven apartments in parallel houses everyone on the same site. Each family keeps its own space for the night while staying a few steps from each other for meals and the ceremony, without relying on a hotel far from the reception venue. The <a href="/en/blog/louer-plusieurs-appartements-groupe-skikda">guide on renting several apartments for a group</a> details how to split families across the site.</p>

<h3>The F2 with jacuzzi to extend the stay as a couple</h3>

<p>After the reception, many newlyweds choose to extend their stay in the more intimate <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> for a few extra nights before the guests return home. It's a frequent combination: the F3 for the reception day, another format for the couple in the days that follow.</p>

<h2>Organizing the day and the decoration</h2>

<p>Once the apartment or apartments are chosen, several practical points are worth planning ahead so the day runs smoothly, especially if it brings together a large number of guests.</p>

<h3>Decorating the terrace and living area</h3>

<p>The F3's private terrace lends itself well to simple decoration for the occasion: tablecloths, flowers, an arch or string lights depending on the style wanted. It is best to plan the decoration in advance, as the residence provides the space but no dedicated decoration service for the event.</p>

<h3>Catering or home cooking</h3>

<p>The equipped kitchen (hob, oven, fridge, coffee maker) allows some dishes and traditional pastries to be prepared on site, often the day before or the morning of the ceremony. For the main meal serving a large number of guests, calling in an outside caterer remains a frequent and entirely possible solution: nothing prevents a delivery straight to the apartment, with the living area and terrace then serving as the reception space.</p>

<h3>Key moments and photos</h3>

<p>The nearby Larbi Ben M'hidi beach makes it easy to fit a couple's photo session by the sea into the program, alongside photos taken on the terrace during the reception. Planning this moment early or late in the day, depending on the light wanted, avoids disrupting the rest of the ceremony's schedule.</p>
