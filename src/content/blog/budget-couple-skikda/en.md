<p>For a <strong>romantic trip to Skikda</strong>, the budget rests on three items: accommodation, meals and, for a couple seeking a more premium setting, wellness. A classic F2 starts at {{TIER_F2_EUR}} € a night at the base rate, versus {{TIER_F2JACUZZI_EUR}} € for an F2 with private jacuzzi and sauna, two formats sized for 4 people but perfectly suited to a couple.</p>

<p>Planning a <strong>romantic weekend or week</strong> in Filfila raises a recurring question: should you pick a simple format to keep the budget in check, or invest in an apartment with wellness amenities to mark the occasion? This guide details every spending item specific to a couple's stay, compares the formats available at Résidence Oasis and offers three budget profiles to help gauge your own planning.</p>

<h2>The spending items of a couple's trip to Skikda</h2>

<p>A two-person stay is structured differently from a family stay: the accommodation item weighs proportionally heavier, since it isn't split across several people, while transport and activity items generally stay light for a couple prioritizing relaxation and beaches over a packed schedule.</p>

<h3>Accommodation: the central item for two people</h3>

<p>Unlike a large family that spreads the nightly rate across several heads, a couple carries the apartment's rate alone. Choosing the format (studio, classic F2 or F2 with jacuzzi) therefore becomes the most decisive budget lever for the whole trip.</p>

<h3>Wellness: the differentiating item of a romantic stay</h3>

<p>This is the item that most clearly sets a romantic trip apart from a typical family stay. The private jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna, available only on the dedicated F2 format, represent an acknowledged extra but one fully included in the nightly rate, with no usage fees.</p>

<h3>Meals and activities: flexible items</h3>

<p>As with any stay in Skikda, the meal budget depends mostly on the trade-off between cooking and eating out. Activities, meanwhile, stay inexpensive since the beaches of Filfila, Larbi Ben M'hidi and Stora are free, letting a couple build a full stay without stacking up extra expenses.</p>

<h3>The classic F2: the space-price balance</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f2-classique-2">F2 family-friendly apartment</a> suits a couple just as well when they want a bit more space than a studio, without targeting wellness amenities. It's the most requested format for a budget-conscious romantic stay.</p>

<h3>The F2 with jacuzzi: the premium choice</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> remains the reference format for a romantic stay that stands out: a two-person jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna included in the nightly rate, with no usage surcharge. The price gap with a classic F2 is best justified for a special occasion (a couple's anniversary, a proposal, a honeymoon) rather than an everyday stay.</p>

<h2>The effect of stay length on the budget</h2>

<p>Stay length directly influences the total budget, regardless of the format chosen. A two- or three-night weekend remains the simplest formula to budget for, but doesn't benefit from any particular discount on the nightly rate.</p>

<h3>The 7-night threshold</h3>

<p>A full week, i.e. 7 nights, automatically triggers a <strong>10% discount</strong> on the total stay, whatever the format booked. For a couple torn between a short stay and a full week, this threshold is worth factoring in: extending by a few nights can, once the discount applies, meaningfully lower the average nightly rate compared to a very short stay.</p>

<h3>A weekend remains accessible regardless</h3>

<p>For a couple with only two or three days, the F2 with jacuzzi remains entirely accessible without reaching the weekly threshold. The nightly rate then applies without a discount, but the total budget stays manageable since no deposit is required and all charges (water, electricity, Wi-Fi, air conditioning) are included.</p>

<h2>Meal budget for a romantic trip</h2>

<p>The food item remains, as with any stay in Skikda, the most flexible part of the budget. Each apartment's equipped kitchen (hob, oven, fridge, coffee maker) lets a couple easily prepare breakfasts and part of the meals, notably with <a href="/en/blog/poisson-frais-stora">fresh fish bought in Stora</a>.</p>

<h3>Alternating cooking and restaurant outings</h3>

<p>For a romantic stay, alternating a few home-cooked meals with one or two restaurant outings, particularly around Stora harbour renowned for its seafood, offers a good balance between a controlled budget and local culinary discovery. <a href="/en/blog/prix-restaurant-skikda">Restaurant prices in Skikda</a> remain generally affordable compared to European standards.</p>
