<p>Spending <strong>New Year's in Skikda</strong> mainly means a calm winter stay by the sea, outside the swimming season. The swimming season runs from June to November: in late December, Filfila's appeal lies more in the mild Mediterranean climate, the most affordable base rates of the year and a 10% discount from 7 nights, than in the beach itself. It is an option for a restful rather than a festive New Year's Eve.</p>

<p>For a traveller considering ending or starting the year away from the colder weather of Northern Europe or Algeria's high plateaus, the natural question is: what can you actually do in Skikda in late December, and is the trip worth it outside the summer season? This article details the climate at this time of year, the activities available, the budget to expect, and the best way to organise a New Year's stay in Filfila.</p>

<h2>Skikda's climate for the year-end holidays</h2>

<p>Skikda enjoys a Mediterranean climate, milder in winter than most of Algeria, but noticeably wetter than in summer. Late-December days alternate between pleasant clear spells for a coastal walk and rainy periods, sometimes with coastal wind. The sea is still there to look at and the seaside scenery keeps its charm, but swimming is no longer part of the plan.</p>

<h3>A programme to adapt to the weather</h3>

<p>Unlike a summer stay where the beach structures the whole day, a New Year's stay in Skikda calls for a more flexible programme: outdoor outings as soon as a clear spell allows, indoor or nearby activities on rainy days. Well-equipped accommodation with heating and reverse-cycle air conditioning, like the Résidence Oasis apartments, then becomes important for a comfortable stay whatever the day's weather.</p>

<h2>What to do in Skikda during the year-end holidays</h2>

<p>No swimming does not mean no activities. Several options work well over a year-end week, between cultural outings, excursions and relaxing moments at the apartment.</p>

<h3>Heritage and culture</h3>

<p>Skikda city centre and its surroundings suit a winter visit well, without the heat or the crowds of summer. The traditional hammam, a particularly appreciated moment of relaxation in winter, pairs well with a day of heritage visits in cool weather.</p>

<h3>Day excursions</h3>

<p>Late December remains a good time for a day trip inland, especially to Constantine, about 100 km away, whose altitude brings a cooler, drier climate. Annaba, about 110 km away, offers a coastal alternative for a full-day outing.</p>

<h3>Relaxing at the apartment</h3>

<p>For stays in an F2 fitted with an <a href="/en/blog/appartement-jacuzzi-skikda">apartment with a jacuzzi</a>, the jacuzzi and sauna come into their own in winter: it is precisely the season where the warmth-and-relaxation combination best makes up for the lack of sea swimming. On the wetter days, indoor activities documented on the blog keep the programme pleasant without depending entirely on the weather.</p>

<h2>How to organise a New Year's stay in Filfila</h2>

<p>Staying in the Jeanne d'Arc district of Filfila, 700 m from the sea, allows for winter coastal walks while staying close to the residence's amenities. The <a href="/en/apartments">available studios and F2/F3 units</a> suit both a couple and a family gathered for the holidays, with an <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> suited to a larger group.</p>

<h3>Booking and practical terms</h3>

<p>Booking is done directly via WhatsApp, with a one-night deposit paid by bank transfer (CCP) to confirm the dates, the balance paid on arrival in dinars or euros, with no deposit required. With demand lower than in summer, booking a few weeks ahead is generally enough to secure the desired format, via the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>.</p>
