<p>A family stay in Filfila isn't just about the beach: every apartment at Résidence Oasis, including the F3 that holds up to 8 guests, includes Wi-Fi, a TV and an equipped kitchen, enough to fill an evening, a rain shower or the hottest part of the afternoon without leaving the building.</p>

<p>The F3 with private terrace adds a sheltered outdoor space to extend outdoor play.</p>

<p>For parents travelling with children, knowing in advance what is already available on site and what is worth packing avoids a few flat evenings after a day of swimming. This article covers the amenities available in the apartments, the activities worth packing, and how to organise the moments when a bit of quiet indoors does the whole family good.</p>

<h2>Why plan indoor activities during a stay in Filfila</h2>

<p>The swimming season runs from June to November, with the water reaching about 26 °C in August, which leaves plenty of time to enjoy the sea. But no stay happens entirely outdoors: a rain shower, a nap schedule shifted for the youngest, or simply an evening after dinner naturally call for an indoor activity.</p>

<h3>Short showers, evenings to fill</h3>

<p>When rain does fall, it most often takes the form of short showers rather than entire overcast days, as detailed in the article on <a href="/en/blog/activites-jour-de-pluie-skikda">rainy-day activities in Skikda</a>, which leans more towards cultural outings. Evenings, on the other hand, are almost always spent at the apartment with the children, which makes it worth knowing the available amenities well.</p>

<h2>The amenities already in the apartments</h2>

<p>Every unit in the residence shares a common base of amenities: Wi-Fi, TV, equipped kitchen, bathroom, air conditioning, refrigerator, oven, hob, freezer and washing machine. This base is already enough to organise several indoor activities without any extra gear.</p>

<h3>Wi-Fi and TV for screen time in moderation</h3>

<p>Wi-Fi makes it easy to start a film or series together, or to stay in touch with relatives far from Filfila. Used in moderation, a screen remains a good complement to a full day at the beach, especially for a quiet evening before the youngest go to bed.</p>

<h3>The jacuzzi and sauna for families with teens</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> and its Norwegian sauna suit a family with one or two teenagers who enjoy this kind of moment, under parental supervision. For young children, vigilance must stay constant and the water temperature lowered, which makes it a better fit for a small group than a large family.</p>

<h3>The F3 terrace for sheltered outdoor play</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> offers an outdoor space of its own, useful for a quiet ball game, chalk drawing, or simply getting some air without leaving the residence. It's a welcome asset for the extended families who typically book this format, designed for 8 guests at base capacity.</p>

<h2>What games and activities to pack</h2>

<p>Beyond the apartment's fixed amenities, a few simple items in the luggage are enough to fill several evenings without relying solely on screens.</p>

<ul>
  <li>One or two compact board games suited to the children's age</li>
  <li>A classic deck of cards, also handy for the adults in the evening</li>
  <li>A puzzle or an activity book for the youngest</li>
  <li>Simple drawing supplies (markers, paper, chalk for the F3 terrace)</li>
  <li>A few books or an e-reader for older children</li>
  <li>A handheld console or a pre-loaded tablet for travel and quiet evenings</li>
</ul>

<p>In high summer, the hottest hours of the day are not ideal for a long outing with young children, especially away from the water.
