<p>Extending a stay at <strong>Oasis Residence</strong> in Filfila is possible on simple WhatsApp request, subject to the availability of the apartment currently occupied or an equivalent format among the residence's 7 units, with a rate calculated from the price grid in effect on the dates of the extra nights.</p>

<p>A stay in Skikda that ends sooner than expected, particularly pleasant weather, or simply the wish to stay a little longer by the sea: reasons to extend are never in short supply. This article details the process to follow, the factors that affect availability and rate, and the right habits to maximise the chances of securing extra nights.</p>

<h2>How to request a stay extension</h2>

<p>Extending a <strong>stay in Filfila</strong> follows the same channel as the original booking: a WhatsApp message to the residence's team, specifying the number of extra nights wanted and, if possible, some flexibility on the end date. The earlier the request comes during the stay, the higher the chances of a favourable answer.</p>

<h3>The right time to ask</h3>

<p>Ideally, the request is made a few days before the originally planned departure date, not on the morning of check-out itself. This gives the team time to check the calendar of the other apartments and, if needed, propose an alternative such as a format change.</p>

<h3>What to specify in the request</h3>

<ul>
  <li>The number of extra nights wanted</li>
  <li>Any flexibility on the end date (for example 2 to 4 more nights)</li>
  <li>The number of travellers, if this changes from the original booking</li>
  <li>A preferred format if the current apartment isn't available for the requested nights</li>
</ul>

<h2>Availability: what works in favour of an extension</h2>

<p>The possibility of extending depends almost entirely on the booking calendar of other guests. An apartment already booked by another traveller for the following nights obviously can't be freed up, which explains why availability varies so much depending on the time of year.</p>

<h3>Outside high season: more flexibility</h3>

<p>From October to May, demand is lower and the residence's 7 apartments generally show more consecutive availability. An extension of a few nights, or even a full week, has good chances of being accepted during these periods, including for an <a href="/en/blog/reserver-appartement-skikda-hors-saison">off-season stay in Skikda</a> decided with little advance notice.</p>

<h3>July-August: plan further ahead</h3>

<p>During high season, the strong demand of July and August mechanically reduces availability. An extension remains possible, but depends directly on what other travellers have already booked for the following days; it is then worth considering, if needed, a format change rather than an extension in the same apartment.</p>

<h2>The rate for an extension</h2>

<p>Extra nights are not billed at a special rate: they simply follow the price grid in effect on the relevant date, which varies by season.</p>

<h3>A season change during an extension</h3>

<p>Some extensions overlap a change of pricing period, for example an end of stay sliding from late August into early September. In this case, the extra nights are billed at the new period's rate, which may be lower than the nights already booked if demand drops on those dates.</p>

<h2>The 7-night threshold and its discount</h2>

<p>The 10% discount from 7 nights applies to the total length of the stay, original booking and extension combined. A 5-night stay extended by 2 extra nights thus reaches the 7-night threshold and may qualify for the discount on the whole period, a point worth checking directly with the team when requesting the extension.</p>

<h2>Switching apartments to extend</h2>

<p>When the originally booked format isn't available for the extra nights but another unit is, switching apartments mid-stay remains an option. Moving for example from a <a href="/en/apartments/studio-deluxe-1">furnished studio for two</a> to an <a href="/en/apartments/f2-classique-2">F2 family-friendly apartment</a> makes it possible to extend a stay in Filfila without waiting for the original unit to free up, at a rate recalculated according to the new format and the period involved.</p>

<h2>Paying for the extra nights</h2>

<p>Payment for the added nights is generally made at the actual departure, on top of the balance already planned for the original stay, in the same currency as the rest of the stay: Algerian dinars (DZD) or euros (EUR). For an extension decided well ahead of time, an additional deposit may be requested, as for any new booking.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<p>A few mistakes come up regularly among travellers wanting to extend their stay in Filfila, more often from lack of planning than from bad intent.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Waiting until the morning of departure to ask for an extension during the swimming season</li>
  <li>Assuming the rate will stay the same when a season change could alter the applicable grid</li>
  <li>Not mentioning any flexibility on dates, which reduces options if the original apartment isn't free</li>
  <li>Forgetting to check whether the extension crosses the 7-night threshold that unlocks the 10% discount</li>
  <li>Not mentioning extra travellers if the group grows during the extension</li>
</ul>

<h2>When an extension isn't possible</h2>

<p>Sometimes neither the original apartment nor another format is available for the requested nights, particularly at the height of summer. In that case, the team can suggest an alternative: departing on the originally planned date with the option to return later in the season, or pointing to the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a> to explore other options based on the calendar.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>

<p>Extending a stay at <strong>Oasis Residence in Filfila</strong> remains a simple, direct process over WhatsApp, but the outcome largely depends on apartment availability for the extra nights and the season involved. Planning the request ahead, staying flexible on dates or format, and checking the effect of the 7-night threshold help maximise the chances of staying a little longer by the sea.
