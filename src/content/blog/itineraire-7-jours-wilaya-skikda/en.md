<p>A <strong>7-day itinerary across the Skikda province</strong> makes it possible to combine a genuine beach stay in Filfila with several more ambitious excursions to Collo, Constantine and Annaba, roughly 100 and 110 km away. The most realistic plan gives the first three days to the sea, then spreads the excursions across the following days to avoid stacking several long drives in a row.</p>

<p>Three days are enough to cover the essentials of Filfila and Stora, but a full week opens the door to trips a short stay cannot comfortably fit in. This article lays out a day-by-day plan across 7 days, alternatives depending on the type of trip, the budget to expect and the best periods to enjoy it.</p>

<h2>Overview of the 7-day plan</h2>

<p>The suggested split alternates beach days and excursions, paced to avoid two long drives back to back. The first three days stay centered on Filfila and Stora, days 4 to 6 open up toward the rest of the province, and the last day closes the stay with a beach day.</p>

<h2>Days 1 and 2: settling in and enjoying Filfila</h2>

<p>The first two days are mainly for settling in and enjoying the beaches closest to the apartment, without much travel. Filfila concentrates most of the amenities needed to start the stay without rushing.</p>

<h3>Day 1: check-in and first swim</h3>

<p>After arrival, head to the nearest beach for a first swim. <a href="/en/blog/plage-larbi-ben-mhidi">Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</a>, stretching almost 7 km, remains the simplest option for a relaxed morning or afternoon. For families, the Marina d'Or Water Park rounds out the first afternoon well.</p>

<h3>Day 2: beach and the Filfila forest</h3>

<p>The second day can alternate between a morning at the beach and a shorter trip to the Filfila forest for a change of scenery without going far from the apartment. It is also a good time to scout nearby restaurants and shops before the busier days ahead.</p>

<h2>Day 3: Stora, the harbor and a boat trip</h2>

<p>The third day turns toward <a href="/en/blog/plage-stora">Stora</a>, west of Skikda, for a different atmosphere: an active fishing harbor, a boat trip to rocky coves, and fresh fish restaurants. The morning suits a boat trip well, while the afternoon can be spent at the Grande Plage or Les Calatines beach, both a few minutes from the harbor.</p>

<h2>Day 4: excursion to Collo</h2>

<p>The fourth day opens the stay beyond the province's center with a full day in <a href="/en/blog/visiter-collo-depuis-skikda">Collo</a>, to the west. The coastline there is less built up than Filfila's or Stora's, with different scenery that justifies a full day rather than a quick stop. This excursion requires a car and suits travelers willing to spend several hours on the road that day.</p>

<h2>Day 5: excursion to Constantine</h2>

<p>The fifth day moves further from the coast with an excursion to <a href="/en/blog/visiter-constantine-depuis-skikda">Constantine</a>, about 100 km from Skikda. The city, known for its suspension bridges over the Rhumel gorges, offers a sharp contrast with the previous beach days. Plan for most of the day for the round trip and the visit, making this a full day trip rather than a quick stop.</p>

<h3>Planning the trip to Constantine</h3>

<p>Skikda's nearest airport is in fact in Constantine (Mohamed Boudiaf), about 100 km away: travelers arriving or departing through it can use day five's excursion to get familiar with the area around the airport ahead of the return trip, without a dedicated extra drive.</p>

<h2>Day 6: Annaba or Skikda's heritage</h2>

<p>The sixth day leaves a choice between two options depending on how much driving the group has already done.</p>

<h3>Option A: excursion to Annaba</h3>

<p>For a more ambitious outing, <a href="/en/blog/annaba-depuis-skikda">Annaba</a> lies about 110 km from Skikda and also has its own airport (Rabah Bitat), useful for travelers arriving or departing through that city rather than Constantine. Annaba's coastline and city center offer a different feel from Skikda, worth a full day.</p>

<h3>Option B: Skikda's heritage and city center</h3>

<p>For a shorter option, Skikda's city center, only 20 km from Filfila, concentrates several heritage sites and traditional markets. This alternative suits families with young children well, or travelers who want to keep some free time for a late-afternoon swim.</p>

<h2>Day 7: last beach day and departure</h2>

<p>The last day closes the stay on a relaxed note rather than a long drive back. A morning at the beach in Filfila, possibly followed by a stop at local markets for last-minute souvenirs, wraps up the week without rushing before departure.</p>

<h2>Adapting the itinerary to your travel style</h2>

<p>This seven-day plan is a flexible base, adjustable to the group's makeup and appetite for long drives.</p>

<h3>Traveling with young children</h3>

<p>Prioritize the Marina d'Or Water Park on the first days, shorten or postpone the Constantine excursion if it seems too long for young children, and pick option B (Skikda city center) for day six. The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> offers the space needed for a family week between excursion days.</p>

<h3>Traveling as a couple or with friends</h3>

<p>The full plan, with Collo, Constantine and Annaba, suits this profile well, as it usually allows more flexibility for longer drives. A more condensed <a href="/en/blog/itineraire-3-jours-skikda">3-day itinerary</a> remains an alternative for those without a full week.</p>

<h3>Without a car</h3>

<p>Without a vehicle, keeping the stay focused on Filfila, Stora and Skikda's city center, all reachable by taxi, remains realistic for most of the week. The excursions to Collo, Constantine and Annaba, however, call for a rental car or carpooling arranged in advance.</p>

<h2>When to do this week-long circuit</h2>

<p>The swimming season in Skikda runs from June to November, with water around 26 °C in August. July and August bring peak crowds to Filfila's and Stora's beaches, which can make booking a boat trip at the last minute harder. June and September offer a good compromise: still-pleasant sea, lighter crowds, and better availability for the rental car needed for the Collo, Constantine and Annaba excursions.</p>
