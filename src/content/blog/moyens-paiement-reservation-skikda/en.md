<p>Booking an apartment at <strong>Résidence Oasis</strong> follows three simple steps: direct contact via WhatsApp, a one-night deposit paid by bank transfer, then a balance paid on arrival in dinars or euros. No international bank card is needed, and no security deposit is ever requested — a different setup from international platforms, worth knowing before booking.</p>

<p>For a traveller used to secure online card payments, this arrangement can be surprising at first. It reflects, however, the standard practice for short-term rentals in Algeria, where bank transfers and cash payment on arrival remain the norm. This article details each payment step, the logic behind the deposit, the accepted currencies, the deposit and discount policy, and the mistakes to avoid so the booking goes smoothly.</p>

<h2>The payment channels available at Résidence Oasis</h2>

<p>The booking process unfolds in two distinct financial steps: a deposit to lock in the dates, then a balance settled on site. Between the two, WhatsApp remains the thread running through all communication.</p>

<h3>WhatsApp for first contact and follow-up</h3>

<p>Every booking starts with a WhatsApp exchange: date availability, choice of apartment format (studio, F2, F2 with jacuzzi, or F3 with terrace), confirmation of the rate for the relevant period. WhatsApp is also used to exchange payment proofs once the deposit is transferred, and to coordinate arrival time before the stay. It's the fastest channel to get a reply, often within a few hours.</p>

<h3>The bank-transfer deposit</h3>

<p>Once the dates are confirmed, a deposit equal to one night's rate is paid by bank transfer. This payment officially locks in the apartment for the requested period and prevents it from being offered to another traveller in the meantime. Bank transfer is a widely used payment method in Algeria, simple to carry out from a postal account, including for diaspora members who still hold an account in the country.</p>

<h3>The balance on arrival, in dinars or euros</h3>

<p>The remaining balance is settled directly on arrival at the apartment, in cash. The traveller freely chooses the currency: Algerian dinars (DZD) or euros (EUR), whichever is most practical. This flexibility avoids the need to exchange a large sum before departure or to hunt for a currency exchange office in Skikda on arrival day.</p>

<h2>Why no international bank card payment</h2>

<p>Short-term rentals in Skikda, as in most of Algeria, still largely operate outside international card payment networks. Résidence Oasis follows this practice: neither the deposit nor the balance can be paid with a foreign Visa or Mastercard. This arrangement, common across the local market, explains why bank transfer and cash remain the two pillars of payment, whether for a studio, a classic F2, or an F3 with terrace.</p>

<p>For a traveller coming from Europe, the best preparation is to plan part of the stay's budget in cash (euros or already-exchanged dinars) before departure, on top of the deposit already paid by transfer.</p>

<h2>No security deposit required</h2>

<p>Unlike much of the premium rental market in Skikda, Résidence Oasis requires <strong>no security deposit</strong>, either at booking or on arrival. This policy rests on a direct trust relationship between traveller and owner, with no prior bank hold and no cash sum tied up throughout the stay. The budget to plan for is therefore limited to the one-night deposit, then the balance paid on arrival.</p>

<h2>The 10% discount from 7 nights</h2>

<p>A <strong>10% discount</strong> applies automatically as soon as the stay reaches 7 consecutive nights, regardless of the apartment format booked. This discount applies to the rate for the relevant stay period and directly reduces the balance paid on arrival. It's particularly attractive for an extended family stay or for diaspora members returning home for several weeks over the summer.</p>

<h2>Concrete steps to book and pay for your stay</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Contact the residence via <a href="/en/contact">WhatsApp, Telegram and phone</a> and state the desired dates</li>
  <li>Receive availability confirmation and the rate applicable to the chosen period</li>
  <li>Pay the one-night deposit by bank transfer to lock in the dates</li>
  <li>Keep the transfer proof and share it via WhatsApp if requested</li>
  <li>Prepare the balance in cash (DZD or EUR) for payment on arrival</li>
  <li>Settle the balance directly at the apartment during <a href="/en/blog/check-in-arrivee-residence-oasis">check-in and arrival at the residence</a></li>

<h2>How much cash to plan for the balance</h2>

<p>The balance amount depends on the apartment format, the number of nights, and the stay's season, since the nightly rate varies by period, as detailed in <a href="/en/blog/combien-coute-nuit-appartement-skikda">our article on the price of a night in Skikda</a>. Once the one-night deposit is deducted, the balance covers the rest of the stay, reduced by 10% from 7 nights onward. Planning a bit extra rather than the exact amount avoids any stress on arrival, especially for travellers paying in euros who want a small margin for exchange.</p>

<h3>Special case: diaspora travellers and Algerian postal accounts</h3>

<p>Many diaspora members keep an active postal account (CCP) in Algeria, which makes transferring the deposit particularly simple from a family or personal account still held in the country. For travellers without such an account, a relative on site can make the transfer on their behalf; this arrangement is common and accepted, provided the reservation holder's name is clearly communicated via WhatsApp to avoid any confusion on arrival.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Planning to pay the full stay by bank card on arrival: only cash payment is possible on site</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last moment to exchange euros or withdraw dinars, when the balance is due right at check-in</li>
  <li>Forgetting to keep the bank-transfer proof for the deposit</li>
  <li>Not communicating the precise arrival time via WhatsApp, which makes check-in and balance payment easier</li>
  <li>Not accounting for the 10% discount from 7 nights when calculating the overall stay budget</li>
</ul>
