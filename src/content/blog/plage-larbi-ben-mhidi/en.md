<p><strong>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</strong>, still widely known by its former name, Jeanne d'Arc beach, is one of the landmark spots on the Skikda coastline. To reach it from the Jeanne d'Arc district in Filfila, allow about 7 km of coastal road, roughly a fifteen-minute drive or taxi ride. On foot, the trip remains possible but takes considerably more time and organization, especially with young children or beach gear.</p>

<p>This article details the different ways to reach this landmark beach, what sets it apart along its 7 km of sand, nearby amenities, the best times to enjoy it, and how to plan a stay in Filfila built around this access to the sea.</p>

<h2>Where Larbi Ben M'hidi beach is located</h2>

<p>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach stretches along the coastline of the Skikda wilaya, near the Filfila area and the Jeanne d'Arc district. At about 7 km of fine sand, it's one of the longest beaches in the region, allowing it to absorb heavy summer footfall without feeling saturated, provided you move a little away from the most popular access points.</p>

<h3>A name inherited from history</h3>

<p>The name Jeanne d'Arc, inherited from the colonial period, remains widely used in everyday speech, on some maps, and in rental listings, while the current official name is Larbi Ben M'hidi, honouring a historical Algerian figure. Both names refer strictly to the same beach and are understood without ambiguity on site, whether by residents, taxi drivers or local shopkeepers.</p>

<h2>How to reach the beach from Filfila</h2>

<p>Several options exist for reaching Larbi Ben M'hidi beach depending on available transport and group size.</p>

<h3>By car or taxi</h3>

<p>The drive from the Jeanne d'Arc district in Filfila takes about 15 minutes over the 7 km of coastal road separating the residential area from this main beach access. A taxi offers a simple alternative for travellers without a vehicle, particularly for a one-way trip with bulky beach gear (parasol, cooler, children's toys).</p>

<h3>On foot</h3>

<p>On foot, allow about 1 h 15 to 1 h 30 depending on walking pace and the heat of the day. This option remains feasible for keen walkers outside the hottest hours, but it isn't suited to a daily family outing with young children or cumbersome gear. For regular use, a car or taxi remains far more practical.</p>

<h3>Renting a car for the day</h3>

<p>For travellers wishing to combine this beach with other sites in the wilaya on the same day (Skikda town centre, Stora, or a longer excursion), renting a car for the day remains the most flexible option, allowing full freedom over arrival and departure times.</p>

<h3>Fine sand and a gentle slope</h3>

<p>The quality of the sand and the gentle slope into the water make this beach one of the most recommended for families with young children, compared for instance with Stora beach, which is rockier and more oriented toward its fishing-harbour atmosphere than classic swimming.</p>

<h3>A beach that absorbs the crowds</h3>

<p>Thanks to its 7 km length, it's generally still possible to find a quieter spot by moving a little away from the main access points, even at the height of high season, unlike shorter beaches that fill up quickly in July-August.</p>

<h2>Amenities and services nearby</h2>

<p>The area around Larbi Ben M'hidi beach and Filfila benefits from the presence of several tourist complexes, including the Marina d'Or Water Park, Rusica Park 4★ and Royal Tulip 5★, which strengthen the dining and activity options beyond simple swimming. Informal parking areas exist near the main access points, particularly in demand during high season.</p>

<h3>Parking and arriving by car</h3>

<p>In high season, arriving early in the morning or in the late afternoon greatly eases the search for a parking spot near the busiest access points. Staying at a residence with its own private, secure parking, as is the case in Filfila, avoids this daily hassle.</p>

<h2>Best time to enjoy the beach</h2>

<p>The swimming season on this beach, as along the whole Skikda coastline, runs from June to November. The water reaches around 26 °C in August, the year's peak temperature. July and August offer the most stable weather but also the highest footfall along the 7 km of sand. June and September make excellent alternatives: the sea remains already or still very pleasant, with considerably fewer people than at the height of summer.</p>

<h3>Off-season</h3>

<p>From October to May, the beach lends itself more to long coastal walks than to classic swimming, as water temperature becomes less inviting for most visitors. It's nonetheless a pleasant time to discover the site without the summer crowds.</p>

<h2>Safety and vigilance on the beach</h2>

<p>The high footfall at Larbi Ben M'hidi beach in high season statistically increases the likelihood of an informal security presence and the number of potential witnesses in case of a problem, a real advantage compared with more isolated coves. It has, however, no guaranteed permanent supervision: watching the state of the sea before entering the water and maintaining constant parental vigilance remain essential, especially with young children.</p>
