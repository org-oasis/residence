<p>A restaurant meal in Skikda averages <strong>400 to 800 DA for a quick option, 1,200 to 2,500 DA for a dish at a classic restaurant, and 2,500 to 5,000 DA or more for a fish or seafood meal</strong> by the sea. The gap comes down to the type of establishment, the dish chosen, and the location — especially proximity to the coast and the busiest tourist areas.</p>

<p>For a French-speaking traveller or a member of the diaspora planning a stay in Skikda, anticipating the <strong>food budget</strong> helps balance the rest of the holiday spending, between accommodation, activities and excursions. The good news: compared with European standards, eating in Skikda remains very affordable, even at quality establishments. This article breaks down prices by restaurant type, offers a budget grid by traveller profile, and gives concrete tips for eating well without blowing the budget.</p>

<h2>Factors that influence the price of a meal in Skikda</h2>

<p>Three main variables explain most of the gaps in <strong>Skikda restaurant prices</strong>: the type of establishment, the dish ordered, and the geographic location.</p>

<h3>The type of establishment</h3>

<p>A sandwich shop or neighbourhood diner offers quick, generous options at tight prices. A classic table-service restaurant applies a mid-range grid. Fish and seafood specialists, often located right on the seafront, sit at the top of the range due to ingredient costs and the setting on offer.</p>

<h3>The dish chosen</h3>

<p>Grilled meats, semolina-based dishes (couscous, chorba) and pizzas or pasta remain among the most economical options. Fish and seafood, particularly shellfish, push the bill up faster, as do composed dishes served with several side dishes.</p>

<h3>Location</h3>

<p>Establishments directly on the Stora seafront or in the busiest tourist areas generally charge 15 to 30 % more than an equivalent place in the city centre or a residential neighbourhood. This premium stays moderate compared to gaps seen in other Mediterranean destinations.</p>

<h2>Where to eat in Skikda and Filfila</h2>

<p>The province's coastline offers a range of places to eat, from street stalls to seaside restaurants. Stora is well known for its small fresh fish establishments, serving produce caught the same day at prices that remain reasonable given the freshness on offer. Downtown Skikda has more popular restaurants and diners, often cheaper than the coastal tourist addresses.</p>

<p>In Filfila, dining options remain more limited than downtown, which pushes many visitors to favour an apartment with an equipped kitchen for most meals, saving restaurant outings for discovery evenings or special occasions.</p>

<h2>Money-saving tips for eating in Skikda</h2>

<ul>
