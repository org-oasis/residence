<p>In August, the <strong>water temperature in Skikda</strong> reaches its yearly peak, around <strong>26 °C</strong>, across the whole wilaya coastline, from Filfila to Stora. This warmth, built up since spring, makes August the reference month for swimmers who want a comfortable sea above all, with no adjustment period when getting in.</p>

<p>For a traveller planning a beach stay in Filfila, knowing this figure precisely helps weigh several candidate months and anticipate the comfort level to expect on site. This article details Skikda's water temperature in August, how it evolves through the month, how it compares with July and September, and practical tips for making the most of swimming during this period.</p>

<h2>The water temperature in Skikda in August</h2>

<p>August marks the sea's <strong>yearly thermal peak</strong> along the Skikda coastline. The water reaches around 26 °C, a temperature that allows long swimming sessions with no sensation of cold, even late in the day or in the evening for the more daring.</p>

<h3>Heat built up since spring</h3>

<p>This high temperature results from gradual warming that starts in spring. The water, still cool in April-May, rises steadily through June, then crosses a clear threshold in July before peaking in August. This thermal inertia explains why the water stays warm well after the atmospheric heat peak, which is usually observed a bit earlier in summer.</p>

<h3>A stable temperature throughout the month</h3>

<p>Unlike air temperature, which can vary noticeably day to day, the sea in August stays remarkably stable. The gap between the start and end of the month is minimal, which makes planning a beach stay particularly reliable during this period.</p>

<h3>August compared with July</h3>

<p>The gap between July and August stays modest, around one degree. July already offers very pleasant water, while August adds that last degree that makes all the difference for swimmers most sensitive to cold, particularly young children who often dread the moment of getting in.</p>

<h3>August compared with September</h3>

<p>September keeps much of the warmth built up in August, especially in the first fortnight. The temperature drop stays gradual and limited, which allows you to enjoy still very comfortable water while generally benefiting from lower crowds than at the height of summer.</p>

<h3>What water at 26 °C actually feels like</h3>

<p>As a reference point, a temperature of <strong>26 °C</strong> is roughly that of a heated pool. Getting in involves none of the small thermal shock felt in spring or early season, which makes swimming accessible even to the most cold-sensitive people, young children included. It's also a temperature that limits cold-related fatigue during long sessions, a real advantage for extended nautical activities like snorkelling or open-water swimming.</p>

<h2>Why the sea stays this warm in Skikda in August</h2>

<p>Several factors explain this particularly favourable temperature along the Skikda wilaya coastline.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Near-continuous sunshine from June to August, with very few clouds to slow the warming</li>
  <li>Relatively shallow water along certain stretches of coast, which speeds up the temperature rise</li>
  <li>A south-western Mediterranean climate broadly milder than the northern Mediterranean coasts</li>
  <li>Thermal inertia that extends summer warmth well beyond the air temperature peak</li>
</ul>

<h2>Activities that benefit from August's warm water</h2>

<p>A sea this warm naturally encourages extended nautical activities, beyond simple beach swimming. It's the month when boat excursions from Stora harbour draw the most people, the calm, warm sea making the trip particularly pleasant for watching the rocky coast from offshore. Snorkelling also benefits from this period, with generally good visibility and water warm enough for long sessions without a wetsuit.</p>

<p>For families, the <strong>Marina d'Or Water Park</strong> in Filfila pairs well with a beach day, especially when midday heat calls for alternating between pools and shaded areas. This beach-water park combination is particularly popular in August, a period when the warm sea makes every nautical activity enjoyable from morning to evening.</p>

<h2>Making the most of swimming in August in Skikda</h2>

<p>Water at 26 °C opens up many possibilities for organising your beach days, provided you account for a few precautions linked to the strong atmospheric heat that usually comes with this period.</p>

<h3>The best times of day</h3>

<p>In early morning and late afternoon, the water stays very close to its average temperature while the air is more bearable, which often makes these families' favourite windows. Midday (noon-4pm) remains fine for swimming, but direct sun exposure calls for more caution.</p>

<h3>Precautions worth keeping despite the warm water</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Stay hydrated regularly, including outside swimming sessions</li>
  <li>Apply and reapply sunscreen, since warm water offers no protection at all from UV rays</li>
  <li>Watch children at all times, as the pleasant water temperature encourages longer sessions</li>
  <li>Favour shade and less intense hours for activities outside the water</li>
</ul>
