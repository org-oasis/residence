<p>Hosting a birthday at Résidence Oasis requires no special arrangement: the F3 with private terrace, which sleeps up to 8 guests at base capacity, combines a spacious living space for the meal with an outdoor terrace to extend the evening. Booking follows the same process as a regular stay, via WhatsApp and a one-night deposit.</p>

<p>Between a studio for two, several F2 units for medium-sized groups, and an F3 with terrace for larger gatherings, the residence offers several possible setups for a private party: a birthday, a family reunion, or simply an evening with friends during a stay in Filfila. This article details which apartment to choose depending on group size, what's included for hosting on site, how booking works, and the practical points to plan ahead so the evening goes smoothly.</p>

<h2>Which Apartment to Choose for a Private Party</h2>

<p>The choice of apartment depends mainly on the number of guests and the type of evening planned. The residence has seven independent apartments on the same site, leaving several options depending on whether the birthday brings together a couple, a family, or a larger group of friends.</p>

<h3>The F3 with Terrace, the Reference Format for Hosting</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> remains the most natural choice for hosting a party on site. Its spacious living room allows for a dining table, and the independent outdoor terrace offers extra space to extend the evening after the cake, without disturbing neighboring units.</p>

<h3>The F2 with Jacuzzi, for a More Intimate Mood</h3>

<p>For a couple's birthday or a gathering of two or three close friends, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> offers a more intimate setting, with the jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna as a complement to the evening rather than a simple meal. This format suits a large group less well but remains a popular choice for a small-scale celebration.</p>

<h3>Several Apartments for a Large Group</h3>

<p>Beyond the capacity of a single unit, booking <a href="/en/blog/louer-plusieurs-appartements-groupe-skikda">several apartments for a large group</a> allows organizing a larger birthday or family gathering while keeping everyone on the same site in Filfila. The F3 then serves as the shared living space for the meal and the evening, while the F2 units and the studio split the sleeping arrangements.</p>

<h2>What's Included for Hosting on Site</h2>

<p>Every apartment at the residence is equipped with a full kitchen, allowing part of the meal to be prepared on site or a delivered dish to simply be reheated. Nothing prevents having a caterer, a takeaway meal, or a birthday cake delivered directly to the apartment either.</p>

<h3>Equipped Kitchen and Living Space</h3>

<p>A refrigerator, oven, stovetop, and small appliances are available in every unit, with enough capacity to store drinks and preparations for an evening. The living room, more spacious in the F3, allows for a shared table without cluttering the circulation space.</p>

<h3>The Terrace, an Asset for Extending the Evening</h3>

<p>The F3's private terrace works well for spending time outdoors after the meal, especially during the <strong>swimming season, which runs from June to November</strong>, with mild evenings that make staying outside pleasant. The space is accessible only from the apartment, with no direct line of sight to neighboring units.</p>

<h3>Parking and Guest Arrival</h3>

<p>The residence's free, secure parking makes it easier for guests arriving by their own means, without any parking constraint on surrounding streets. This is a useful point to share with guests who aren't staying on site but are only coming for the evening.</p>

<h2>Booking a Stay Around a Party or Birthday</h2>

<p>Booking is done directly via WhatsApp, stating the dates, the number of people sleeping on site, and, if needed, an estimate of the number of guests for the evening. A one-night deposit paid by CCP transfer confirms the booking, with the balance paid on arrival in dinars or euros, with no deposit required.</p>

<h3>Planning a Longer Stay</h3>

<p>For a stay that combines several vacation days with a birthday evening in the middle, the 10% discount from 7 nights applies normally, as with any regular stay. This allows organizing a full week in Filfila that includes the celebration without adding to the overall budget of the stay.</p>

<h2>Best Practices for a Successful Party on Site</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Inform the reception in advance if the number of guests for the evening exceeds the number of people sleeping on site</li>
  <li>Favor the F3 with terrace for a group larger than 4 people, to keep enough room to move around</li>
  <li>Order a caterer or a cake in advance, especially in peak season when local demand is higher</li>
  <li>Keep music at a reasonable hour in the evening, out of consideration for other residents on site</li>
  <li>Plan parking for guests arriving by their own means, letting them know what's available on site</li>
</ul>

<h2>Birthday or Reception: a Few Common Use Cases</h2>

<p>Several types of celebrations come up regularly among stay requests in Filfila: a child's birthday combined with a family stay, an evening with friends during summer vacation, or an extended family gathering around a specific event. In each case, the choice of apartment mainly depends on the number of people sleeping on site rather than the total number of guests expected for the evening itself, which leaves some flexibility to host more people on a one-off basis.</p>

<h2>Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Booking a studio or a standard F2 expecting to host a large group for the evening: the F3 remains better sized</li>
  <li>Forgetting to inform the reception of a large number of guests for the evening</li>
  <li>Not planning ahead for a caterer or cake order during peak summer season, a period of high local demand</li>
  <li>Keeping music going very late without consideration for other units at the residence</li>
  <li>Underestimating the need for several apartments for a group well beyond the capacity of a single unit</li>
</ul>
