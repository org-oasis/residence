<p>Renting an apartment at <strong>Oasis Residence for a photo or video shoot</strong> is possible, subject to prior agreement with the residence: the 7 units offer three distinct looks (a 132 m² terrace, jacuzzi-sauna, everyday-life interior), 700 m from a beach in the Skikda wilaya. This article details the available settings, practical conditions and limits to know before scheduling a session.</p>

<p>Between the constraints of a closed photo studio and the lack of atypical locations available for rent in Skikda, more and more photographers, independent video makers and local communication agencies are looking for real, furnished settings close to the sea for wedding, lifestyle, fashion or advertising content shoots. Oasis Residence, in Filfila, combines several looks in one location bookable in a single reservation. This article explains what the residence concretely offers for this type of use, how to organize a crew's visit, and the points to anticipate for a smooth shoot.</p>

<h2>Why choose a furnished apartment rather than a studio for a shoot</h2>

<p>A <strong>classic photo studio</strong> offers full control over light but remains a neutral, often generic backdrop. A real furnished apartment, in a seaside setting, brings an authenticity hard to reproduce in a studio: a real fitted kitchen, a real terrace, a real view, with no cardboard set. For a lifestyle, wedding or fashion shoot looking for a lived-in setting rather than a plain background, this type of location changes the nature of the resulting visuals.</p>

<h3>Three looks in one location</h3>

<p>The residence brings together three distinct visual registers on one site, avoiding the need to move between several shooting locations in the same day: a wellness look with the F2 Suite jacuzzi-sauna, a family or lifestyle look with the classic F2 units, and a large-outdoor-space look with the F3 and its terrace.</p>

<h3>Simplified logistics for the crew</h3>

<p>Housing the shoot crew on site, in one or more units of the residence, avoids daily trips to a distant shooting location. For a multi-day shoot, this logic of lodging plus setting in the same place meaningfully reduces logistical fatigue compared with a daily round trip from central Skikda.</p>

<h2>Settings available in the residence</h2>

<h3>The F3 and its 132 m² terrace</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> offers the residence's most generous volume, with an open view suited to wide shots. It is the most requested setting for group sessions (family, team, small filmed event) or for shots requiring room for lighting equipment and assistants.</p>

<h3>The F2 Suite jacuzzi and sauna</h3>

<p>For a wellness, intimate or premium feel, the <a href="/en/blog/appartement-jacuzzi-skikda">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> and its Norwegian sauna are a rare setting in Skikda. This type of space is regularly sought for couple sessions, high-end lifestyle content or advertising visuals tied to wellness.</p>

<h3>Classic F2 units for an everyday-life feel</h3>

<p>The classic F2 units, furnished and fitted with a real kitchen, suit sessions looking for a more sober feel: food content, a couple or family life scene, or a neutral indoor shot without the "premium" jacuzzi angle.</p>

<h2>Light and nearby outdoor locations</h2>

<p>The residence is located <strong>700 m</strong> from the sea, in the Jeanne d'Arc district of Filfila, which makes it easy to follow an indoor session with outdoor shots without a long trip. Larbi Ben M'hidi beach (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), about 7 km long, offers enough space to vary framing without repeating the same shots across several days of shooting.</p>

<h3>Best times for natural light</h3>

<p>As with any shoot on the Mediterranean, sunrise and late afternoon offer the softest light, with a low sun that limits harsh contrast on the terrace or by the sea. The bathing season, from June to November, also allows water scenes to be worked in without the temperature making it uncomfortable for models.</p>

<h2>Organizing a crew's visit</h2>

<p>Prior agreement with the residence remains necessary before any session, whether the use is personal, commercial or institutional. It is recommended to state from first contact the intended use, the number of people on site (technical crew, models, guests) and the desired length of stay on location.</p>

<h3>Practical steps</h3>

<ol>
  <li>Choose the unit(s) matching the setting sought (F3, F2 jacuzzi, classic F2)</li>
  <li>Contact the residence via the <a href="/en/gallery">residence photo gallery</a> to view the spaces before confirming the choice</li>
  <li>State the intended use (photo, video, number of people) when requesting the booking</li>
  <li>Pay a one-night deposit by CCP transfer to confirm the date</li>
  <li>Pay the balance on arrival, in dinars or euros, with no security deposit required</li>
</ol>

<h3>Duration and applicable rate</h3>

<p>No separate "shoot day" package exists: the rate applied is the standard nightly rate of the chosen unit. For a single-day session, booking one night remains the simplest option; for a crew that needs early-morning access or a late departure, two consecutive nights avoid the constraints tied to check-in and check-out times.</p>

<h2>Limits to anticipate</h2>

<p>A few points should be considered before scheduling a shoot in the residence, to avoid any disappointment on the day.</p>

<ul>
  <li>No blackout studio or professional neutral backdrop: the settings are real, furnished and lived-in apartments</li>
  <li>No generator or reinforced electrical setup for heavy lighting equipment</li>
  <li>Each unit's capacity must be respected: do not exceed the number of people allowed on site during the session</li>
  <li>Prior agreement with the residence is mandatory before any technical crew's visit</li>
  <li>High season (July-August): more limited availability, as units are in high demand for regular stays</li>
</ul>

<h2>When to schedule a shoot in Filfila</h2>

<p>Low season, from October to May, offers wider unit availability and soft light all day, ideal for a shoot without the crowd constraints of the beach. High season (July-August) brings a livelier atmosphere and pleasantly warm water (around 26 °C in August), useful for scenes needing warm water, but booking should be made several weeks ahead given the limited simultaneous availability of units.</p>

<p><strong>Oasis Residence</strong> suits a photo or video shoot for crews looking for a real, lived-in setting rather than a neutral studio, with three looks available on one site 700 m from the sea. The format particularly suits lifestyle, wellness, wedding or brand-content sessions, provided prior agreement, unit capacity and the absence of on-site technical equipment are anticipated.
