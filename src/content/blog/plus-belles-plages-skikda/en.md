<p>Finding <strong>the most beautiful beaches in Skikda</strong> requires knowing a coastline that stretches from Filfila to Collo, with very different moods from one cove to the next. Between Filfila's fine sand, the size of Grande Plage, the discretion of Les Calatines and the lively Stora harbour, the Skikda region offers a much richer choice than generic tourist maps suggest.</p>

<p>For a traveller planning their stay, the question isn't just "where to swim" but rather "which beach fits which purpose": family relaxation, quiet time as a couple, or a lively atmosphere with fish restaurants nearby. This article details the region's must-see beaches, their respective strengths, the bathing season, and practical tips for organizing beach days without surprises.</p>

<h2>Overview of the beaches in the Skikda region</h2>

<p>The coastline of the <strong>Skikda region</strong> alternates sandy beaches, rocky coves and harbour areas over nearly 300 km of coast. Most beaches visited by travellers are concentrated between Filfila and Stora, a few kilometres from the town centre, with Collo further west for lovers of a wilder coastline.</p>

<h3>Filfila and Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</h3>

<p>The <strong>Larbi Ben M'hidi</strong> beach, formerly Jeanne d'Arc, remains the reference point of the Jeanne d'Arc district in Filfila. Its fine sand and shallow water near the shore make it a safe bet for families with young children. Its immediate proximity to the furnished residences in the area, about 700 m away, makes it the most practical choice for a stay without daily transport hassle.</p>

<h3>Grande Plage, for large groups</h3>

<p>Larger, Grande Plage easily accommodates extended families and groups of friends. Its wide sand lets you set up several parasols without feeling cramped, even at the height of the high season. It's the most suitable option when a stay brings together several generations or several households.</p>

<h3>Les Calatines, for peace and quiet</h3>

<p>Les Calatines offer a more low-key setting, appreciated by visitors seeking calm away from the crowds of the larger beaches. Access is generally less direct, which naturally filters attendance and preserves a more peaceful atmosphere, especially outside the height of summer.</p>

<h3>Stora, between harbour and beach</h3>

<p>West of Skikda, Stora combines an active fishing harbour with an atmosphere different from the rest of the coastline. It's also the preferred starting point for a boat excursion to the coves and sea caves of the rocky coast, an activity that breaks up the classic beach routine.</p>

<h2>Bathing season and water temperature</h2>

<p>The <strong>bathing season in Skikda</strong> runs from June to November, a wide window compared to other Mediterranean regions. July and August see the most crowds, with water around 26 °C in August. June and September remain prime months: the sea is already or still warm, with noticeably fewer people on the sand than at the height of summer.</p>

<h3>Attendance by period</h3>

<ul>
  <li>July-August: high attendance at Larbi Ben M'hidi and Grande Plage, book accommodation ahead</li>
  <li>June and September: excellent balance of weather and attendance, sea still warm</li>
  <li>October-November: swimming still possible for the hardier, beaches nearly empty</li>
  <li>December-May: outside the bathing season, but coastal walks remain pleasant</li>
</ul>

<h2>Complementary beach activities</h2>

<p>Beyond swimming, several activities round out a beach stay in Skikda. The <strong>Marina d'Or Water Park</strong> in Filfila offers slides and pools for days when children want something other than sand. For upscale accommodation near the sites, the 4-star Rusica Park and the 5-star Royal Tulip round out the local hotel offering, as an alternative to furnished apartments.</p>

<h3>Boat excursion from Stora</h3>

<p>From Stora harbour, boat excursions let you follow the rocky coastline and discover coves and sea caves unreachable on foot. It's one of the most recommended ways to see the Skikda coastline from another angle, complementing days of beach relaxation.</p>

<h2>Practical tips before a beach day in Skikda</h2>

<ul>
