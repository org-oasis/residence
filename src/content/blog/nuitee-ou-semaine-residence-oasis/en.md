<p>Yes, <strong>Residence Oasis</strong> can be booked by the night just as easily as by the week, with no minimum length of stay imposed. A single night is enough to confirm a booking, with a deposit equal to one night's rate paid by CCP bank transfer. From <strong>7 consecutive nights</strong>, an automatic <strong>10%</strong> discount applies to the total stay.</p>

<p>For a traveler passing through Skikda as much as for a family planning a full week in Filfila, the booking format directly affects both the budget and the organization of the stay. This article explains exactly how nightly booking works, how the long-stay discount is triggered, and how to choose between the two depending on the actual length of the planned stay.</p>

<h2>Booking by the night: how it works</h2>

<p>Unlike some properties that impose a minimum of 2 or 3 nights, <strong>Residence Oasis</strong> accepts bookings from a single night, across all 7 apartments. This flexibility suits a traveler in transit just as well as someone who wants to try a format before extending their stay.</p>

<h3>The deposit stays the same regardless of length</h3>

<p>Whether the stay covers one night or several weeks, the <strong>deposit requested</strong> always matches the rate of a single night, paid by CCP bank transfer to confirm the dates. This keeps booking simple: there is no different calculation depending on whether 1 night or 10 nights are booked.</p>

<h3>The balance is settled on arrival, with no deposit</h3>

<p>The remaining balance, whether for a short visit or a long stay, is settled on arrival in Algerian dinars or euros. No security deposit is required in either case, which also simplifies things for a last-minute or quickly decided stay.</p>

<h2>Booking 7 nights or more: the automatic 10% discount</h2>

<p>As soon as the length of stay reaches <strong>7 consecutive nights</strong>, a <strong>10%</strong> discount applies automatically to the total rate, whatever apartment format is chosen. This single threshold has no variant: no additional tier at 14 nights, no percentage that would change with length beyond 7 nights.</p>

<h3>The calculation covers the whole stay</h3>

<p>The discount is not calculated night by night but on the total amount of the stay, once the 7-night threshold is reached or exceeded. A 10- or 15-night stay therefore gets the same 10% reduction as a stay of exactly 7 nights, with no extra benefit tied to length beyond that threshold.</p>

<h2>Which booking format to choose depending on your profile</h2>

<p>The choice between a short and a long booking depends above all on the actual length of the planned stay, more than on a tariff-optimization strategy at all costs.</p>

<h3>For a 1- to 3-night stay</h3>

<p>A traveler on a business trip, in transit between Constantine or Annaba and their final destination, or simply curious to discover the region on a short stay, books by the night with no particular constraint. The <a href="/en/apartments/studio-deluxe-1">furnished studio for two</a> suits this kind of visit well.</p>

<h3>For a full week or more</h3>

<p>A family or group planning a week or more of vacation has every reason to book directly for 7 nights or more to unlock the 10% discount from the moment of confirmation. For this kind of stay, the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> offers the space needed for a larger family.</p>

<h3>Extending a stay already underway</h3>

<p>A stay initially booked by the night can be extended once on site, subject to availability on the following dates. If the extension pushes the total past the 7-night threshold, the 10% discount applies retroactively to the whole recalculated stay.</p>

<h2>A calculation example by length of stay</h2>

<p>The calculation mechanism stays the same regardless of the length booked: the nightly rate of the chosen format, shown in the table above, is multiplied by the number of nights, then the 10% discount applies to that total once the 7-night threshold is reached. For a 3-night stay, the amount simply equals the nightly rate multiplied by 3, with no reduction. For a 7-night stay in the same apartment, the same calculation applies, then the resulting total is reduced by 10%, which in practice makes the seventh night cheaper than the first six.</p>

<h2>Booking for a group: nightly or weekly across several apartments</h2>

<p>For a group or extended family booking several apartments at once, each unit follows its own rule for length and discount. A group booking an F3 and an F2 for 7 nights benefits from the 10% discount on each of the two apartments, calculated separately on each format's rate. It remains possible to combine different lengths depending on each family's needs within the group, for example an F3 booked for 10 nights and a studio booked for just 4 nights by a couple joining the group later in the stay. For a group booking across several units, it is best to send all the dates at once to the team to get a consolidated quote.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Booking several short, consecutive stays thinking they add up to the same benefits as a single booking of 7 nights or more</li>
  <li>Forgetting to ask for the 10% discount when requesting a quote for a stay of 7 nights or more, even though it is automatic but should appear on the quote sent</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last minute to book a full week during a high-demand period, which reduces availability across the 7 apartments</li>
  <li>Thinking an extra security deposit is required for a short stay, which is never the case</li>
  <li>Confusing the deposit amount, always equal to one night, with the total balance of the stay</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to confirm a booking, by the night or by the week</h2>

<p>Booking is done exclusively direct via WhatsApp, regardless of the length planned. The team confirms availability, calculates the exact rate based on dates and length, applies the 10% discount if the stay reaches 7 nights, then sends the details for the deposit by CCP bank transfer. To start a request, the <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a> centralizes every available channel.</p>

For a short stay as much as for a full vacation, the one-night deposit by CCP bank transfer and the absence of a security deposit remain the same simple rules.
