<p>Booking an apartment at Oasis Residence in Filfila relies on a deposit equal to <strong>a single night</strong>, paid by CCP transfer after confirmation on WhatsApp. The balance is paid on arrival, in Algerian dinars or in euros, and no additional security deposit is ever required, whatever the length of stay.</p>

<p>For a traveller preparing a stay on the Skikda coast, often from abroad or another wilaya, understanding how the deposit works before committing avoids unpleasant surprises. Unlike some booking platforms that require the full stay amount upfront or hold a security deposit on a credit card, the process here is deliberately simple: one transfer, one point of contact, and a balance settled only on arrival.</p>

<h2>The principle: a deposit capped at one night</h2>

<p>Whatever the format booked — studio, F2, F2 with jacuzzi or F3 with terrace — the <strong>booking deposit</strong> always equals the rate of a single night in that apartment, not a percentage of the total stay nor the full amount. This principle stays the same whether the booking covers 3 nights or 3 weeks.</p>

<h3>Why this amount and not another</h3>

<p>Setting the deposit at one night's rate secures the booking without demanding a disproportionate financial commitment before arrival. The exact amount therefore depends on the format chosen and the period of the stay, since the nightly rate varies by season.</p>

<h2>The steps of the CCP transfer</h2>

<p>The <strong>CCP transfer</strong> (Compte Chèque Postal) remains the most common and accessible way to pay a deposit in Algeria, including for diaspora members who have the deposit transferred by a relative on the ground.</p>

<h3>1. Confirm dates and format on WhatsApp</h3>

<p>The first contact happens through the residence's <a href="/en/contact">contact page and directions</a>, which lists the WhatsApp, Telegram and phone details. The availability of the desired apartment is checked for the requested dates before any payment step.</p>

<h3>2. Receive the CCP account details</h3>

<p>Once the dates are confirmed, the CCP account details are sent directly by message. No payment is requested before this step, and no third-party platform is involved in the transaction.</p>

<h3>3. Make the transfer at an Algérie Poste branch</h3>

<p>The transfer is made at the counter of an Algérie Poste branch, or by a relative with a CCP account if the traveller is booking from abroad. A receipt or screenshot of the transfer is then enough to confirm the booking with the residence.</p>

<h3>4. Keep the balance for arrival</h3>

<p>The remaining balance — the total rate minus the one-night deposit already paid — only needs to be settled on arrival, in DZD or EUR according to the traveller's preference.</p>

<h2>The balance on arrival: DZD or EUR, your choice</h2>

<p>Unlike the deposit, which must go through the CCP transfer, the <strong>balance of the stay</strong> offers some flexibility. It is paid in cash on arrival, in whichever currency suits the traveller best.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Payment in Algerian dinars (DZD), convenient for residents and travellers already holding local cash</li>
  <li>Payment in euros (EUR), convenient for travellers arriving from abroad who have not yet exchanged their money</li>
  <li>No extra exchange fee applied by the residence on a balance paid in euros</li>
  <li>No security deposit added when settling the balance</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why no security deposit is required</h2>

<p>Many rental platforms impose a <strong>security deposit</strong>, in cash or through a bank pre-authorisation, which locks part of the traveller's funds for the whole stay. Oasis Residence works differently: the one-night deposit covers only the booking commitment, and no additional sum is held or requested as a guarantee.</p>

<p>This choice simplifies the total stay budget calculation right from the booking: a one-night deposit, then the full balance on arrival, with no security-deposit line to anticipate or recover after departure.</p>

<h2>Special case: a stay of 7 nights or more</h2>

<p>From 7 nights of rental, a 10% discount applies to the total rate of the stay. This reduction is factored in before the final balance is calculated, but the deposit stays based on the rate of a single night, with no adjustment linked to the discount. How this discount works is explained in the article on the <a href="/en/blog/remise-10-pourcent-7-nuits">10% discount from 7 nights</a>.</p>

<h2>Reserving abroad: what actually changes</h2>

<p>A significant share of travellers who book at Oasis Residence do so from France, Belgium or another country where the Algerian diaspora lives, ahead of a trip back home for the holidays. The CCP transfer requires an active Algerian postal account, which is not always the case for a traveller settled abroad for several years.</p>

<h3>Having a relative on the ground make the transfer</h3>

<p>In that case, the most common solution is to ask a relative still in Algeria — a parent, a sibling, a trusted friend — to make the CCP transfer from their own account, on the traveller's behalf. The residence attaches the booking to the final traveller's name as soon as the dates and apartment are confirmed on WhatsApp, regardless of the name on the transfer itself.</p>

<h3>Allowing for postal processing time</h3>

<p>A CCP transfer made at an Algérie Poste counter is not instant like an online card payment. It is therefore best to start the booking process several days before the planned arrival date, particularly for a stay planned in July or August, the period of highest demand when availability closes up faster.</p>

<h3>Keeping a written record of each step</h3>

<p>For a traveller organising their stay remotely, keeping the WhatsApp exchange where the dates, format and rate were confirmed remains the best safeguard, alongside the CCP transfer receipt. These two elements are usually enough to resolve any ambiguity on arrival, with no further document needed.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid</h2>

<p>A few mistakes come up regularly among travellers booking for the first time through this process, whether local travellers or diaspora members organising a stay for family still in Algeria.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Making the transfer before receiving written confirmation of the dates and apartment on WhatsApp</li>
  <li>Forgetting to keep the receipt or screenshot of the CCP transfer as proof of payment</li>
  <li>Assuming a larger deposit speeds up or further secures the booking</li>
  <li>Planning the balance in a single currency when both are accepted on arrival</li>
  <li>Not clarifying the cancellation conditions before making the transfer, especially in high season</li>
</ul>
