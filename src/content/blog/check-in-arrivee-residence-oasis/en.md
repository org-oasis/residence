<p>Arrival at an apartment of <strong>Oasis Residence</strong> is set up before departure, over WhatsApp: a one-night deposit confirms the booking, the balance is settled on-site in dinars or euros, and reception stays available 24/7, 700 m from the sea, with no deposit ever asked for during the stay.</p>

<p>For a traveller preparing a first stay in Skikda, the exact check-in process often stays unclear: does booking go through a platform, is a deposit required, is there a fixed arrival time? This article details every step, from the first contact to the key handover, so that checking in to an apartment in Filfila holds no unpleasant surprise.</p>

<h2>Booking before arrival: WhatsApp and CCP deposit</h2>

<p><strong>Booking an apartment in Filfila</strong> always starts with a direct exchange, most often on WhatsApp. There is no complicated form and no mandatory third-party platform charging a commission: one message is enough to start the conversation.</p>

<h3>Information to share before booking</h3>

<p>Before paying any deposit, it helps to specify the stay dates, the number of travellers and the desired format among the <a href="/en/apartments">available studios and F2/F3 units</a>. The team then confirms availability and the rate applicable to the chosen period, the grid varying by season.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Precise arrival and departure dates</li>
  <li>Number of adults and children (up to 2 extra guests possible per unit, with a surcharge)</li>
  <li>Desired format: studio, F2, F2 with jacuzzi or F3 with terrace</li>
  <li>Planned mode of transport to Filfila, to anticipate the arrival time</li>
</ul>

<h3>The deposit and booking confirmation</h3>

<p>Once dates and rate are confirmed, a <strong>one-night deposit</strong> paid by CCP transfer locks in the booking. The deposit amount simply depends on the chosen format and season.</p>

<h2>Arrival day in Filfila</h2>

<p>On the day, it is enough to notify the team of the estimated arrival time, especially after a flight landing at Constantine airport (Mohamed Boudiaf) or Annaba airport (Rabah Bitat), the two airports closest to Skikda, located respectively about 100 km and 110 km from the residence. Expect a drive of a bit over an hour from either one, depending on traffic and route.</p>

<h3>Access to the residence and parking</h3>

<p>Oasis Residence is located in the Jeanne d'Arc neighborhood of Filfila, 700 m from the sea. <strong>Free, secure parking</strong> is available from arrival, with no booking or extra charge, which makes things much easier for travellers arriving by rental car from either airport.</p>

<h3>Welcome, key handover and walk-through</h3>

<p>The residence's reception operates <strong>24/7</strong>, covering both a mid-afternoon arrival and a late one after a night drive. Key handover comes with a quick tour of the apartment: amenities, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, fitted kitchen, so everything is clear from the first minutes of the stay.</p>

<h2>No deposit: what this means in practice</h2>

<p>Unlike much of the seasonal rental market, Oasis Residence asks for <strong>no deposit</strong>, neither in cash, nor as a card pre-authorization, nor as a guarantee cheque. No amount is held on arrival in anticipation of possible damage.</p>

<h2>What to do in case of a late arrival or delayed flight</h2>

<p>A flight delay when <a href="/en/blog/comment-reserver-appartement-skikda-a-distance">booking an apartment remotely</a> from abroad is not a problem in itself: simply notify the team via WhatsApp as soon as the delay is known. With reception available 24/7, an arrival delayed by a few hours, or even in the middle of the night after a drive from Constantine or Annaba airport, stays manageable at no extra cost.</p>

<h2>The stay balance: dinars or euros</h2>

<p>The <strong>remaining balance</strong>, meaning the total stay amount minus the deposit already paid, is settled on arrival. Two currencies are accepted: Algerian dinar (DZD) or euro (EUR). This flexibility avoids having to exchange the entire budget before departure, a point appreciated by diaspora families travelling with part of their savings already in euros.</p>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid before arrival</h2>

<p>A few mistakes come up regularly among travellers discovering Filfila for the first time. Anticipating them makes the arrival smoother.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Not notifying the estimated arrival time, which needlessly delays the key handover</li>
  <li>Bringing only euro cash without knowing the balance can also be paid in dinars</li>
  <li>Forgetting to specify the exact number of travellers, especially with extra guests beyond the unit's base capacity</li>
  <li>Underestimating the drive time from Constantine or Annaba airport, which usually exceeds an hour</li>
  <li>Waiting until the last moment to book during peak summer season, when availability shrinks fast</li>
</ul>

<h2>Preparing a booking based on group size</h2>

<p>The apartment format to book depends directly on the number of travellers. A couple or a small family fits well in a studio or F2, while an extended family or a small group of friends will do better booking the F3 with terrace, sized for a larger number of people. Up to 2 extra guests stay possible per unit, with a surcharge, avoiding the need to book two apartments for just one or two people beyond the base capacity.</p>

<h3>Travelling as a group or an extended family</h3>

<p>For a group wishing to stay in several neighboring apartments while keeping shared living easy, the residence allows booking several units in parallel. This simply requires specifying, from the first WhatsApp exchange, the total number of people and the desired split between apartments, so the team can propose a coherent combination of studios, F2s and F3s based on availability.</p>

<h2>What to prepare before departure</h2>

<p>Beyond the booking itself, a few practical points make for a stress-free arrival. Keeping the residence's WhatsApp contact easily accessible during the journey, whether by plane then car from Constantine or Annaba airport, or directly by road from another wilaya, allows reporting anything unexpected in real time: a delay, a schedule change, a last-minute question.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Save the residence's WhatsApp contact before departure</li>
  <li>Plan a realistic estimate of the drive time from the airport or starting point</li>
  <li>Check that the balance amount matches the total minus the deposit already paid</li>
  <li>Bring some cash, in DZD or EUR, to settle the balance on arrival</li>
  <li>Confirm the exact number of travellers if extra guests join the original group</li>
</ul>
