<p>In Skikda, summer settles in from July to August with a hot, dry Mediterranean climate, strong sunshine and a sea that reaches about 26°C in the height of August. It's the busiest time of year on the coast, driven by a bathing season that officially runs from June to November.</p>

<p>For a French-speaking traveller, an expatriate, or a member of the diaspora planning a stay, summer in Skikda raises a simple but decisive question: when to book to enjoy a warm sea without the heaviest crowds, and what the days of July and August actually look like on this stretch of the Algerian coast. This article breaks down the summer climate month by month, sea temperature, how rates and crowds evolve, and the activities and habits that make the most of the season.</p>

<h2>Skikda's Mediterranean climate in the height of summer</h2>

<p>Skikda enjoys a classic <strong>Mediterranean climate</strong>: hot, dry summers and mild, rainy winters. In summer, this pattern reaches its clearest expression. Skies stay clear on the vast majority of July and August days, rainfall becomes extremely rare, and the immediate proximity of the sea tempers the heat compared to more inland parts of the region, thanks to a sea breeze that regularly sets in toward the end of the day.</p>

<h3>July: the start of the high season</h3>

<p>July marks the entry into high season. Heat settles in for good, days reach their longest, and the sea keeps warming after spring. Crowds build week by week, without yet reaching the peak seen in the heart of August.</p>

<h3>August: peak heat, sea temperature and crowds</h3>

<p>August concentrates the year's most stable heat, the warmest sea (about 26°C) and the heaviest crowds on the beaches of Filfila, Stora and Grande Plage. It's the month when local life organizes most clearly around the beach, terrace meals and evening outings once the heat eases.</p>

<h2>The sea in summer: temperature and bathing comfort</h2>

<p>The <strong>bathing season</strong> in Skikda officially runs from June to November, but summer is undeniably its core. The sea warms up progressively from June, crosses a clear threshold in July, then reaches its annual peak of about 26°C in the height of August. This water temperature, combined with long, sunny days, explains why July-August remains the most sought-after period for families and groups enjoying the beaches of <a href="/en/blog/plage-larbi-ben-mhidi">Larbi Ben M'hidi</a> or Grande Plage.</p>

<p>Compared to neighbouring seasons, summer's water clearly outshines <a href="/en/blog/climat-skikda-printemps">spring's</a>, still cold in May, and stays warmer than autumn's from late September onward. For a swimmer who values a comfortable sea above all for long sessions, summer remains the reference period, despite heavier crowds than the rest of the year.</p>

<h2>Recommended activities in Skikda in summer</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Morning or late-afternoon swims in Filfila, when the heat is less intense than at midday</li>
  <li>Boat excursion from Stora to explore the coves and sea caves of the rocky coast</li>
  <li>Trips to Collo or Cap Bougaroun, best planned early in the morning to avoid midday heat</li>
  <li>Fresh fish dinners in the evening in Stora, once the heat eases</li>
  <li>Relaxing in an air-conditioned private jacuzzi during the hottest hours</li>
</ul>

<h2>Common mistakes to avoid in summer</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Booking at the last minute expecting to easily find an available F3 or F2 jacuzzi in the height of August</li>
  <li>Planning a long car trip in the early afternoon, the hottest time of day</li>
  <li>Underestimating crowds at the best-known beaches without planning an alternative like Les Calatines</li>
  <li>Forgetting to check the air conditioning is working properly before confirming a booking in peak heat</li>
  <li>Comparing Skikda's summer directly to more humid Northern European summers, when the heat here stays dry and tempered by the sea breeze</li>
</ul>
