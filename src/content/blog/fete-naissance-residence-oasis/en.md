<p>Hosting a <strong>baby celebration at Résidence Oasis</strong> requires no special arrangement: the F2 with private jacuzzi, with a base capacity of 4 people, suits a small circle of relatives, while the F3 with private terrace, which accommodates up to 8 people, better suits a wider family gathering with a meal and decorations. Both apartments sit 700 m from the sea, in Filfila.</p>

<p>Celebrating a new baby's arrival outside a traditional reception hall appeals to more and more families: a private setting, flexible timing, an equipped kitchen to prepare part of the meal yourselves, and the option to extend the stay by the sea after the celebration. This article explains which apartment to choose depending on group size, how to organize the day on site, what budget to plan, and the practical points to cover so everything runs smoothly.</p>

<h2>Why choose an apartment over a reception hall</h2>

<p>A baby celebration is often a more intimate event than a wedding or an engagement party, which makes a spacious apartment a relevant alternative to a hall booked for the occasion. The apartment format allows more flexibility on timing and how the day unfolds.</p>

<h3>A private setting for a family moment</h3>

<p>Unlike a hall shared with other events on the same day, an apartment booked for the celebration remains for the family's exclusive use throughout the stay. There is no imposed check-out time or shared spaces with another group, leaving the time needed to welcome relatives, share the meal, and then enjoy more informal moments later in the day.</p>

<h3>Extending the stay by the sea</h3>

<p>Filfila sits 700 m from the sea, which allows extending the celebration with a few days of relaxation by the beach for the parents and relatives who travelled from afar, without changing accommodation or reorganizing transport between two addresses.</p>

<h2>Which apartment to choose depending on group size</h2>

<p>The choice of apartment mostly depends on the expected number of guests and the type of moment sought: a small, calm gathering, or a larger family reception.</p>

<h3>The F2 with jacuzzi for a small circle</h3>

<p>For a celebration bringing together the parents and a few very close relatives, the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> offers a calm, comfortable setting, with a base capacity of 4 people. It's a suitable format for a more intimate moment, away from the bustle of a large reception, for example for new parents who want to mark the occasion without too many people around the baby.</p>

<h3>The F3 with terrace for a family gathering</h3>

<p>Once the number of guests exceeds 4 people, the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> becomes the most practical choice. Its spacious 132 m² living room hosts the meal and reception indoors, while the terrace extends the celebration outdoors, for photos or a moment of tea and pastries after the main meal. Its base capacity of 8 people suits an extended family circle including grandparents and the closest relatives.</p>

<h3>Combining two apartments for a larger family</h3>

<p>When the number of guests exceeds a single unit's capacity, particularly if relatives are travelling from another city for the occasion, booking a second apartment at the residence in parallel allows housing everyone on the same site. Each family keeps its own space for the night, while staying a few metres from each other for the meal and the celebration itself.</p>

<h2>Organizing the celebration day</h2>

<p>Once the apartment is chosen, a few practical points are worth planning ahead so the day runs smoothly, particularly with a newborn or very young child at the centre of attention.</p>

<h3>Decorating the terrace or living room</h3>

<p>The F3's terrace or the F2's living room lend themselves well to simple decorations for the occasion: balloons, garlands, a photo corner for the baby. It's best to plan the decoration in advance, as the residence provides the space but no dedicated decoration service for the event.</p>

<h3>Meal, pastries and catering</h3>

<p>The equipped kitchen (hob, oven, fridge, coffee maker) allows preparing some dishes and pastries directly on site, the day before or the morning of. For a larger main meal, calling on an outside caterer remains a common solution: nothing prevents a delivery directly to the apartment, with the living room and terrace then serving as the reception space.</p>

<h3>Setting up a baby corner without crowding the reception space</h3>

<p>In the F3, the separate bedrooms allow setting up a changing corner or a playpen for the baby away from the reception space, useful for naps in the middle of the day. The F2, more compact, remains suitable if the group stays small and the baby can stay close to the parents throughout the celebration.</p>

<h2>Budget and booking</h2>

<p>The budget for a baby celebration organized in an apartment stays far more flexible than that of a traditional reception hall, since it's essentially limited to the rate of the night(s) booked, with no separate hall rental fee.</p>
