<p><strong>Oasis Residence</strong> does not offer a formal childcare or babysitting service on site. For a family stay in Filfila, organisation therefore relies on three practical levers: choosing the right apartment among the 7 units available, renting baby gear on request, and, where useful, booking several units so the extended family can naturally take turns.</p>

<p>For parents travelling with young children, the absence of an on-site childcare service can seem daunting at first glance. In practice, good preparation ahead of time is usually enough to organise a stress-free stay: a suitable apartment format, gear checked before arrival, nearby activities to occupy the children, and, where relevant, several units booked together so grandparents or other relatives can step in. This article covers these options one by one to prepare a family stay with no unwelcome surprises.</p>

<h2>Is there a childcare service at Oasis Residence?</h2>

<p>No. No <strong>babysitting</strong> or childcare service is offered directly by the residence. This is worth clarifying before booking for families counting on occasional evening childcare: there is no in-house offer equivalent to a hotel's kids club or nursery service.</p>

<p>This lack of a formal service does not, however, prevent a comfortable family stay. The layout in independent apartments, rather than hotel rooms, actually offers more flexibility to adapt the space and pace to children's needs, provided the right format is chosen and a few practical details are anticipated.</p>

<h2>Which apartment to choose for a stay with young children</h2>

<p>The choice of unit directly affects how easily children can be supervised during the stay. The residence's 7 apartments break down into formats suited to different uses depending on children's ages and group size.</p>

<h3>The F3 with terrace for families with several children</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a> accommodates up to 8 people at base capacity, making it the format best suited to a large family or several young children. The private terrace offers a secure outdoor space, with no direct overlook from neighbours, where children can play under supervision without leaving the unit.</p>

<h3>Ground floor and first level to avoid stairs</h3>

<p>With a stroller or a toddler just learning to walk, climbing several floors on every outing quickly becomes a hassle. The Studio Deluxe and the first-level F2 units limit this inconvenience. The dedicated guide on the <a href="/en/blog/appartement-rez-de-chaussee-skikda">stair-free apartment in Skikda</a> details exactly which units in the residence sit on the ground floor or first level.</p>

<h3>The jacuzzi F2, best kept for slightly older children</h3>

<p>The <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a> suits a couple with one or two older children able to understand safety rules around a water feature. For very young children, this format requires constant parental supervision around the jacuzzi, as noted in the guide dedicated to this apartment.</p>

<h2>Baby gear: what is provided and what to check</h2>

<p>Each apartment's fully fitted kitchen allows preparing bottles and small meals with no hassle, with a fridge, hob and oven available in all 7 units. Specific baby gear (travel cot, high chair, bottle warmer), however, is not provided as standard in every apartment and must be flagged in advance. The dedicated guide on <a href="/en/blog/location-bebe-skikda">renting baby gear in Skikda</a> details the equipment available on request and the lead time to plan before arrival.</p>

<p>A few mistakes recur among families who do not plan enough ahead for a stay with young children. Spotting them early avoids surprises once on site.</p>

<ul>
