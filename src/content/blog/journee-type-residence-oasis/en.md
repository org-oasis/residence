<p>A <strong>typical day at Résidence Oasis</strong> follows a simple rhythm built around the closeness of the sea, only <strong>700 m</strong> from the residence: waking up and breakfast in the apartment, a beach morning, lunch in or at a restaurant, an afternoon of rest or an excursion, then sunset and a quiet dinner. This flow leaves plenty of room for spontaneity while keeping a clear structure.</p>

<p>For a traveller still weighing up different places to stay in Filfila, picturing the concrete flow of a day often helps more than a list of amenities. This article walks through, hour by hour, what a stay at the residence looks like, the variations depending on the apartment booked, and how booking and payment are organised beforehand.</p>

<h2>Morning: waking up, breakfast and heading to the beach</h2>

<p>Mornings usually start without a forced wake-up: most guests on holiday in Filfila ease into the day, enjoying the air conditioning and the quiet of the Jeanne d'Arc neighbourhood. Every apartment has a <strong>fully equipped kitchen</strong> (stove, oven, refrigerator, coffee maker), making it easy to prepare breakfast on site rather than relying on an outside service.</p>

<h3>700 m to the sea</h3>

<p>The residence sits <strong>700 m from the sea</strong>, which makes the walk to the beach only a few minutes long, with no need for a car for the morning outing. This detail directly shapes the rhythm of the day: it becomes easy to go back and forth several times rather than settling in for the whole day starting at 9 am.</p>

<h2>Morning at the beach</h2>

<p><strong>Larbi Ben M'hidi beach</strong> (formerly Jeanne d'Arc), stretching over roughly 7 km of sand, offers enough room to find a quieter spot even in peak season. Swimming season runs from <strong>June to November</strong>, with water reaching around <strong>26°C in August</strong>, keeping the sea pleasant for most of the morning without a cold shock when entering the water.</p>

<p>For families with young children, the morning rhythm often includes more frequent shade breaks and an earlier return before the hottest part of midday.</p>

<h2>Lunch: cooking in or eating out</h2>

<p>Lunch marks a natural turning point in the day. Two options coexist without excluding each other: preparing a simple meal in the apartment using the kitchen facilities, or going out to one of the <a href="/en/blog/meilleurs-restaurants-skikda">best restaurants in Skikda</a>. Many guests alternate, saving the restaurant outing for a specific occasion, such as a fresh-fish lunch after a morning in Stora, covered in the article on <a href="/en/blog/poisson-frais-stora">where to eat fresh fish in Stora</a>.</p>

<h3>Cooking on site, a real asset for longer stays</h3>

<p>For a stay of a week or more, alternating between home-cooked meals and outings helps manage a budget while keeping the freedom to eat out whenever the mood strikes. The equipped kitchen remains one of the features most valued by families and groups staying several days in a row.</p>

<h2>Afternoon: rest, jacuzzi or an excursion</h2>

<p>The afternoon is the part of the day that varies most, depending on the apartment booked and what the group feels like doing. Three scenarios come up most often.</p>

<h3>A nap or quiet time in the apartment</h3>

<p>After a morning at the beach, a midday break in the air-conditioned apartment is the most common choice, especially in July and August when the midday heat discourages going out.</p>

<h3>Jacuzzi and sauna for guests in the premium F2</h3>

<p>For a couple staying in the <a href="/en/apartments/f2-jacuzzi-3">F2 with private jacuzzi</a>, the afternoon can extend into a wellness session on site, jacuzzi and Norwegian sauna included in the nightly rate, with no outing or surcharge needed.</p>

<h3>An excursion for a change of scenery</h3>

<p>Once or twice a week, many guests devote the afternoon or the whole day to a wider excursion: Stora harbour about a 20-minute drive away, Skikda's heritage town centre about 20 km away, or a longer trip to Cap Bougaroun or Collo for stays of more than a week.</p>

<h2>A variant for families with children</h2>

<p>For a group staying in the <a href="/en/apartments/f3-terrasse-7">F3 with private terrace</a>, the afternoon often shifts toward calm outdoor time on the terrace rather than another beach trip, particularly useful after a morning already full with young children. On windier days when swimming becomes less comfortable, a session at Filfila's water park remains a supervised alternative families appreciate.</p>

<h2>End of day: sunset and dinner</h2>

<p>The late afternoon brings a return to a slower pace. Filfila's seafront offers one of the simplest vantage points for a sunset, reachable within minutes on foot, with no special planning needed. Details on the best viewpoints by season are in the article on <a href="/en/blog/coucher-soleil-skikda">where to watch the sunset in Skikda and Filfila</a>.</p>

<p>Dinner follows the same logic as lunch: an equipped kitchen for a simple meal on site, or a restaurant outing for a different evening. The evening itself tends to stay quiet, as the residence is not in a nightlife-heavy area.</p>

<h2>How this day changes depending on the apartment booked</h2>

<p>The booked unit mostly shapes the afternoon and evening. A studio or a classic F2 suits a simple rhythm centred on the beach and outings, while an F2 with jacuzzi favours relaxing on site, and an F3 with private terrace leaves more room for calm outdoor time with several children. The article on <a href="/en/blog/quelle-taille-appartement-choisir">which apartment size to choose by number of travellers</a> details each unit's capacity to help settle this choice before booking.</p>

<h2>What to do on a day with less favourable weather</h2>

<p>Some days don't suit a classic beach program, whether due to strong wind or accumulated tiredness after several active days. The program then naturally shifts to a calmer morning in the apartment, a trip to Skikda's town centre for a change of scenery, or an excursion already planned to Stora or the city's heritage sites. The equipped kitchen also turns an indoor day into a pleasant moment cooking together as a family.</p>
